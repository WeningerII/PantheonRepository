# Ptolemaic ancestry and account repair

The founding-family pilot and the seven-person Thais/Artakama expansion left
ancestry claims as endpoints. This batch reconstructs the consulted named
ancestral chains and makes a selected account drive the displayed tree,
descent assessment, parental summaries and inherited-power candidates.

## Source scope

61 new records reuse the existing founders and divine/mythic ancestors:

- 18 records in [the transmitted Satyrus pedigree](ptolemaic-claimed-ancestry.md),
  including the distinct Macedonian namesakes and the separate Herodotean
  sequence. Ptolemy has 22 parent edges to Zeus and to Dionysus in Satyrus's
  transmitted genealogy. No conjectural generations are inserted.
- 34 records in [the connected Apollodoran branches](ptolemaic-aetolian-ancestry.md),
  retaining alternative fathers and unnamed-parent gaps.
- 9 records in [the reported paternal ancestry](ptolemaic-paternal-ancestry.md),
  including competing accounts of Amyntas III's father and the Lyncestian
  maternal-grandparent relationship across an unnamed mother.

The earlier seven targets remain their existing identities: Thais, her sons
Lagus and Leontiscus, Eirene, Eunostus of Soloi, Artakama and Artabazus II.
The two Lagus records remain distinct. Eurydice in this batch is Philip II's
mother, not Ptolemy's wife. Ptolemy's Eurydice and Berenice branches remain
outside this batch.

## Interpretation and presentation

Conventional historical parentage remains the default. Claimed divine descent
is visibly selectable on the profile. Grouped accounts follow the same cited
pedigree recursively; where the group has no account, the separately cited
recorded parentage is used and disclosed. Selecting Satyrus currently reaches
65 records across 36 generations when those existing mythic branches are
included. The initial tree remains bounded; **Show all ancestors** explicitly
expands the complete reachable ancestry.

Ptolemy's selected claim now displays **Claimed divine ancestry**, with an
unquantified fraction where sources omit parents. Neither a fabricated 50%
value nor a direct divine parent is required to represent this ancestry.
Posthumous apotheosis and Egyptian royal affiliations remain distinct.
Unknown parents, conflicting groups, cycles and indeterminate conception-time
status prevent false numerical precision. A recorded classification is labeled
as such; default and selected descent assessments use the same model.

Static profiles expose full account pedigrees, source notes, translated
epithets and reciprocal alternative-child navigation. Lazy loading and the
bounded initial render are preserved. Neutral behavioral fixtures cover the
shared model and presentation rather than branching on a person's identity.
