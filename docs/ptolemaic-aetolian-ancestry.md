# Aetolian ancestry accounts adjoining the Ptolemaic pedigree

The Satyrus quotation names Althaea and her father Thestius. The newly supplied
Oeneus alternative for Deianira also opens the Calydonian family. Their ancestry
is therefore followed through every named parent in the consulted Apollodoran
passages, rather than stopping at those three records.

The directly consulted source is [Apollodorus, Library 1](https://www.theoi.com/Text/Apollodorus1.html):
1.7.2–10, 1.8.1, 1.9.8 and 1.9.11. Claim-level citations use the relevant section.
Thirty-four absent records are added, alongside reusable existing identities.
For these mythical figures, the primary Apollodoran parent assertions also supply
the default lineage. Named divine ancestry receives the appropriate ancestry
tier through the shared classification pipeline; unknown coparents do not acquire
invented identities or asserted fractions. Historical Macedonian and Ptolemaic
records retain their mortal baseline and separate claimed accounts.

The account group `apollodoran-aetolian-pedigree` is separate from
`satyrus-ptolemaic-pedigree`. Selecting the former for a visible ancestor follows
its cited Apollodoran ancestors. It does not recast the longer Satyrus quotation
as a historically established genealogy. The alternative accounts for Aetolus,
Endymion and Hellen remain individually selectable.

Achelous is absent from the existing registry and is added as a river deity.
[Hesiod, Theogony 337–345](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html) supplies
Oceanus and Tethys as his parents in a separately named, cited account. This
Hesiodic assertion is not falsely attributed to Apollodorus.

The consulted passages leave the parents of Cleoboea, Phorbus father of Pronoe,
Dorus father of Xanthippe, Deimachus father of Enarete, Orseis, Alcidice and
Iphianassa unnamed. These are source-specific endpoints, not assertions that no
other source could preserve an account. The unnamed Naiad receives no invented
record. Deucalion's mother and several other coparents remain unknown here.
Amythaon's marriage to Idomene is not imported as proof of Aeolia's maternity.

Identity distinctions are explicit: Agenor of Pleuron is not the Phoenician
Agenor; Protogenia daughter of Deucalion is not Calydon's daughter; Epicaste is
not Jocasta; Deucalion son of Prometheus is not the Cretan prince; Aeolus son of
Hellen is not the ruler of the winds; Pronoe is not the Nereid; and Iphianassa
is not assumed to be Iphigenia. The two adjacent men called Dorus in Apollodorus
are not silently merged with Xanthippe's father without a source identification.
