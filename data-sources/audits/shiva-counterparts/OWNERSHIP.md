# Ownership decisions

The lane starts at `hindu_shiva`. The seven originally connected records were
resolved by explicit IDs, not display labels or fuzzy runtime matching.

- Own Hindu Shiva forms, their source-specific families, Bactrian Oesho,
  Balinese Batara Guru, Bodo Bathou/Mainao, Kirati Paruhang/Sumnima, and Japanese
  Okuninushi/Daikokuten contexts and their newly researched branches.
- PR 142 reserves the connected Kalash counterpart records to the Sakra lane.
  `kalash_mahandeo` is an ambiguous overlap: this lane records its resolved name
  and reviews the Shiva endpoint only; it does not edit Mahandeo or Moni. The
  owner must verify the Jettmar/Parkes claims and the reciprocal relationship.
- PR 142 explicitly assigns the reciprocal Rigveda 10.72 Daksha account to this
  lane. This is the recorded decision before editing `hindu_daksha`. Author only
  the Daksha endpoint; leave Aditi and every Indra family record to that lane.
- The Maruts are an overlap with the Indra network. Their collective identity
  and Suyasa's parent reference are recorded without selecting an individual
  father or creating a competing collective record. That endpoint remains open
  for the Sakra owner. No equation with the ordinary alias Marut on Vayu.
- Existing Batak, Bugis and Malay Batara Guru records and Bai/Tibetan Mahakala
  contexts are separately inventoried. Similar names alone do not merge them
  into Bali, Hindu Shiva, or Japanese Daikokuten.
- Shared correction infrastructure was published separately in PR 145 and
  merged as 52d08105c0c8609bbf42d660de336020eca0d5c7. All three other lanes requested
  this behavior. Their authored corrections still require their own integration.
