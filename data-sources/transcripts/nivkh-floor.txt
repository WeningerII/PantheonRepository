================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the three remaining individuated figures the ethnographic record supports: Kurŋ, the supreme sky-universe god central to Shternberg's account of Gilyak religion; Tly yz (Tly nivkh), the Master of the Sky who completes the mountain-sea-sky triad with the existing Pal-yz and Tol-yz and has its own entry in Tokarev's Mify narodov mira (signed by Taksami); and Tugrsh ytik, the Fire Grandmother of the hearth documented by Kreinovich and Ostrovsky. Tly yz is wired to Kurŋ via a directed subordinate-to relation reflecting Shternberg's identification of the sky with kurŋ. Candidates rejected: Tairnadz/Taikhnad (an alias of the existing Tol-yz), the wind god "Laizna" (attested only in non-scholarly popular material), and milk/kinr evil spirits and kekhn shaman helpers (spirit classes, not named individuals).
