================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added five well-attested, individuated Aymara figures beyond the existing set: Ekeko/Iqiqu (god of abundance, from Bertonio 1612 and Ponce Sanginés's archaeological study Tunupa y Ekako); Copacati and the Copacabana idol, the complementary rain/celestial-water and lake/fish wak'as of Lake Titicaca documented by Ramos Gavilán (1621) and analyzed by Salles-Reese (1997), which I wired to each other as a Hanan/Hurin complementary pair; Phaxsi, the personified moon and feminine complement of the existing Tata Inti (attested in Bertonio and van den Berg 1990), wired as consort-of Inti; and Wari, the primordial chthonic deity of the world below reconstructed by Bouysse-Cassagne (1988). I deliberately excluded generic spirit-classes such as the anchanchu (a demon type rather than a named individual) and did not re-author Tunupa despite the scholarly equation of Ekeko with Thunupa, since Tunupa already exists in the corpus.
