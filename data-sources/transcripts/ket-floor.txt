================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added the three named 'master' (owner) spirits that Ket hunting-and-fishing religion individuates alongside the cosmic deities already in the corpus: Kaigus, host of the forest and its game (also met as a bear or a 'forest-mother'); Kholai, mistress of the hunted animals; and Ulgys, master of the rivers and their fish. All three are named, offering-receiving beings documented by Alekseenko (1967) and Vajda (2011), distinct from generic unnamed nature spirits, which is why they clear the individuation bar. I checked for but did not add a moon or earth deity: in the accessible scholarship the Moon of the Es–Hosedem tale and the earth are impersonal, unnamed features rather than individuated figures, so they were excluded rather than padded in. The three masters are left solitary because the sources give them no attested parentage or myth-kinship to Es, Hosedem, Tomam, Alba or Doh.
