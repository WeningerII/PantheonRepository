Tradition: Sogdian (pre-Islamic Transoxiana / Penjikent)
Author: figure-authoring researcher
Date: 2026-06-26

PURPOSE
Fills the Zarafshan-valley core of the Central Asia gap with the distinctive Sogdian pantheon of the Penjikent and Afrasiab murals — Nana the lion-borne city-patron, Adbag (Ahura Mazda), the Shiva-clad wind-god Weshparkar, Zurvan (Time), and the mourning vegetation-goddess Žēmāt. Iranian-yazata forms carry equated-with links to their Zoroastrian/Greek originals; Žēmāt and the others draw on Grenet, Marshak and Shenkar.
