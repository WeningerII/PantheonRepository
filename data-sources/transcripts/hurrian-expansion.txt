PANTHEON REGISTRY — FIGURE-AUTHORING TRANSCRIPT
Tradition: Hurrian
Eras (registered, exact): mythic
Author pass: Hurrian held 14 records. This adds 21.

================================================================================
THE GAP — THE CORPUS HELD KUMARBI AND NOT ANU
================================================================================

A 46-name check across the WHOLE registry returned 21 hits and 25 absences —
and FOUR OF THE TWENTY-ONE HITS ARE FALSE for this tradition's purposes, which
is part of the finding: see below.

The Kumarbi cycle turns on one act. In the *Song of Emergence* — the Hittite
adaptation of the Hurrian myth — **Alalu** is king in heaven; in the ninth year
his cup-bearer **Anu** overthrows him; nine years later Anu's own cup-bearer
**Kumarbi** overthrows him in turn, and in the struggle **bites off Anu's penis
and swallows it.** Anu curses him and tells him that from the seed now inside him
five gods will be born who will depose him, and the first of them is **Teshub**.

The registry held **Alalu**. It held **Kumarbi**. It held **Teshub**. **It held
no Hurrian Anu** — the god who is bitten, whose curse sets the whole cycle
running, and who is the father of the god the cycle is about.

And a name-sweep says otherwise, which is the point. "Anu" comes back as a HIT,
against `mesopotamian_anu` and against `irish_danu`, which carries Anu as an
alias. **Neither is the Anu of the Kumarbi cycle.** Four of this sweep's
twenty-one hits are like that — Ishara resolving onto `eblaite_ishara`, Adamma
onto `eblaite_adamma`, Kubaba onto `lydian_kuvava`, Nikkal onto
`canaanite_nikkal` — all of them real figures in other traditions and none of
them this tradition's record of the same cult.

That is this programme's recurring finding in its most compressed form yet: the
corpus held both ends of a swallowing and not the thing swallowed.

It also held **Ullikummi**, the stone grown to overthrow Teshub, and not
**Upelluri**, the giant on whose shoulder he was set to grow — the being who was
already alive when heaven and earth were separated and who carries them on his
back.

And it held Kumarbi's son Ullikummi and Kumarbi's son Hedammu and not
**Silver**, the son of Kumarbi who was king in heaven for a time and has a song
of the cycle to himself.

================================================================================
AND THE CIRCLE OF HEBAT
================================================================================

Hurrian offering-lists — the *kaluti* — are organised around a principal deity
and name the circle that goes with them. The corpus held **Hebat** and
**Sharruma** and none of the rest of her circle: not **Takitu** her attendant,
not **Allanzu** and **Kunzishalli** her other children, not **Shuwala**, not
**Adamma**, not **Kubaba**, not **Nabarbi**.

It held **Teshub** and not the two bulls that pull his wagon, **Sheri** and
**Hurri** — "day" and "night" — nor **Tilla**, who replaces Hurri in the eastern
tradition.

It held the moon god **Kushuh** and not **Nikkal**, his consort.

And it held no Hurrian goddess of the underworld at all: not **Allani**, whom
the scholarship calls one of the three primary Hurrian goddesses beside Ishara
and Shaushka, and not **Ishara**, who is invoked beside her.

================================================================================
WHAT THIS BATCH DOES NOT INGEST
================================================================================

The **Irsirra**, the nursery goddesses who carry the infant Ullikummi to
Upelluri's shoulder, are **a collective** — the sources call them a collective in
as many words — on the ground that rejected the Cwn Annwn, the Matronae, the
aralez and the jeoseung saja. The *kaluti* themselves are **lists**. The Dark
Earth is a **place**.

**Hutena and Hutellura are ONE record**, because the sources write them as a
hyphenated pair in the offering lists and give neither a separate act.
