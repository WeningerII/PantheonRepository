================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added six well-attested, individuated Diné Holy People absent from the existing set: First Man (Áłtsé Hastiin) and First Woman (Áłtsé Asdzą́ą́), the primordial couple and foster parents of the already-registered Changing Woman; Coyote (Mąʼii / Áłtsé Hashké), the trickster; Black God (Haashchʼééshzhiní), god of fire and maker of the constellations; Talking God (Haashchʼééłtiʼí), chief of the yéʼii; and Water Sprinkler (Tó Neinilí), the rain god. Each is a named, individuated deity documented across the core scholarly corpus (Matthews 1897 and 1902, Reichard 1950, O'Bryan 1956, Haile 1984, Zolbrod 1984, Luckert 1979, Griffin-Pierce 1992), and I wired them into the existing pantheon via the First Man/First Woman spousal pair and their fosterage of Changing Woman, plus the yéʼii companion cluster. I deliberately excluded generic classes (the yéʼii collectively, unnamed Holy People, monster-classes) and aspects of existing figures such as White Shell Woman; further named beings like Salt Woman or the Moon-bearer exist but are more thinly individuated, so I stopped at the securely attested core to avoid padding.
