================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the only two genuinely individuated mythic beings the scholarship documents beyond the existing corpus: Unwabu the chameleon and Intulo the lizard, the paired messengers of Unkulunkulu in the Nguni origin-of-death myth (Callaway 1870; Werner 1933; Bozongwana 1983), both wired to the existing ndebele_unkulunkulu as subordinates and to each other as reciprocated rivals. I deliberately excluded the Njelele shrine triad names Shologulu, Banyanchaba, and Lunji (Daneel 1970; Werbner 1989) because scholars describe them explicitly as father/mother/son manifestations of Mwali, i.e. aspects of the existing ndebele_umlimu, which the brief forbids re-authoring. Nomkhubulwana/Inkosazana was excluded as attested for the Zulu rather than the Zimbabwean Ndebele, and the venerated royal ancestors Mzilikazi and Lobengula were excluded as historical persons falling outside the registered primordial/mythic-past era keys.
