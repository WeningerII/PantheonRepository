PANTHEON REGISTRY — FIGURE-AUTHORING TRANSCRIPT
Tradition: Kyrgyz (the Manas epic trilogy), Central Asia / Tian Shan
Registered era vocabulary for Kyrgyz: heroic-age, semetey-cycle, seitek-cycle, wider-cycle
Author: figure-authoring researcher
Date: 2026-06-26

PURPOSE
Fills the Central Asian gap (the 55-88E steppe/Tian Shan belt) with the heroes of the
Kyrgyz oral epic of Manas — the Manas-Semetey-Seitek trilogy — together with the
self-standing wider-cycle epics Er Töshtük and Kojojash. These are mortal heroic figures
(epic heroes and their adversaries), not gods. The dynastic line Jakyp -> Manas ->
Semetey -> Seitek is wired through parentIds; the forty companions (kyrk choro) and the
Kalmyk/Chinese antagonists are wired as companion / sworn-companion / enemy / rival edges.

SOURCE BASIS
Critical editions and recordings of the living oral tradition:
  - Arthur T. Hatto (ed./trans.), The Memorial Feast for Kökötöy-Khan (Oxford, London
    Oriental Series 33, 1977) — critical edition of Radloff's Bok Murun text.
  - Arthur T. Hatto, The Manas of Wilhelm Radloff (Wiesbaden, Asiatische Forschungen 110, 1990).
  - W. Radloff (V. V. Radlov), Proben der Volkslitteratur ... Vol. V: Der Dialect der
    Kara-Kirgisen (St. Petersburg, 1885) — the foundational 1862-69 recordings.
  - Chokan Ch. Valikhanov, 'Smert' Kukotay-khana i ego pominki' (recorded 1856).
  - Daniel Prior (trans.), The Memorial Feast for Kökötöy Khan (Penguin Classics, 2022) —
    the Sagymbai Orozbakov variant.
  - The Sayakbai Karalaev variant of the trilogy (Kyrgyz Academy of Sciences editions).
  - UNESCO Representative List, 'Kyrgyz epic trilogy: Manas, Semetey, Seytek' (2013).
