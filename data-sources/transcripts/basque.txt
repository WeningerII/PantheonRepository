PANTHEON REGISTRY — FIGURE-AUTHORING TRANSCRIPT
Tradition: Basque (Euskal Herria)
Registered era vocabulary: mythic (only)
Author batch: weningerii@gmail.com — 2026-06-23

PURPOSE
-------
This transcript supplies the well-attested CORE of Basque (Euskal) mythology that
was still missing from the Pantheon Registry, complementing the six figures already
held (basque_mari, basque_sugaar, basque_basajaun, basque_eate, basque_ilargi,
basque_ortzi). All figures are drawn from the standard ethnographic corpus of
Basque mythology — chiefly José Miguel de Barandiarán's "Mitología vasca" (1960)
and "Diccionario ilustrado de mitología vasca," Julio Caro Baroja's "Los vascos"
(1949), "Algunos mitos españoles" (1941) and "Mitos vascos y mitos sobre los
vascos" (1985), and Wentworth Webster's "Basque Legends" (London, 1879). No figure
or citation here is invented; where a detail is uncertain or a single-witness claim,
it is flagged in the notes. Euskara terms are given only where they are real,
sourced words (lur "earth," eki/eguzki "sun," suge "serpent," gau "night," etc.).

FIGURES AUTHORED (8)
--------------------
1. basque_amalur   — Amalur / Lur / Ama Lurra, Mother Earth; the cosmic womb and
                     source of the Sun and Moon. Mother-of the existing
                     basque_ilargi and of the new basque_eguzki. Created the
                     eguzkilore (sun-thistle) to guard human doorways.
2. basque_eguzki   — Eguzki / Eki / Ekhi, the Sun; daughter of Amalur, "grandmother"
                     of the Basques and the eye of day, protector against night
                     spirits, sister of the Moon (basque_ilargi). Returns nightly
                     through Itxasgorrieta ("the Reddish Seas") into her mother's womb.
3. basque_mikelats — Mikelats, the malevolent twin son of Mari and Sugaar; spirit
                     of storm and hail; brother of Atarrabi. parentIds: Mari, Sugaar.
4. basque_atarrabi — Atarrabi, the benevolent twin son of Mari and Sugaar; escaped
                     the Devil's school, became a saved priest. parentIds: Mari, Sugaar.
5. basque_gaueko   — Gaueko, the Lord of the Night, of the oldest mythic stratum,
                     who punishes those who flaunt the dark ("the night for the one
                     of the night, the day for the one of the day").
6. basque_aatxe    — Aatxe / Aatxegorri / Beigorri / Etsai, the cave young-red-bull
                     spirit, a guise of Mari's chthonic domain, who emerges in storms
                     to drive the good home and punish wrongdoers.
7. basque_lamia    — The Lamiak (sing. Lamia/Lamina), river-and-spring nymphs with
                     duck/bird feet who comb their hair with a golden comb and build
                     bridges and dolmens overnight in exchange for offerings.
8. basque_tartalo  — Tartalo / Tartaro / Torto, the one-eyed cave giant (cyclops)
                     who devours shepherds; undone by his own crying ring.
9. basque_inguma   — Inguma (Mauma), the night-demon of nightmares and suffocation
                     who presses sleepers' throats; warded by a counting-charm.

(Note: "Lur" is NOT authored as a separate figure — in the sources Lur / Ama Lurra /
Lurbira is the bare and the maternal name of the very same Mother-Earth deity given
here as basque_amalur, with "Lur" recorded as an alt name. Mari and Amalur are
sometimes conflated in the literature; they are kept distinct per the existing
basque_mari and the explicit mother-of-Sun-and-Moon genealogy attached only to Amalur.)

RELATION WIRING NOTES
---------------------
- Parent→child edges use the corpus's directed kinds "mother-of" / "father-of";
  child→parent edges use "mother" / "father"; these are non-symmetric.
- Amalur is wired mother-of the EXISTING basque_ilargi and the new basque_eguzki.
- Eguzki and Ilargi are sisters: a symmetric "sibling" edge from Eguzki toward the
  existing basque_ilargi requires a reciprocal on Ilargi (flagged below).
- Mikelats and Atarrabi are twin brothers (reciprocal "twin sibling" within this
  batch) and are children of the EXISTING basque_mari and basque_sugaar.
- Aatxe is a guise/aspect of Mari; expressed as a directed "aspect-of" edge toward
  the existing basque_mari (non-symmetric; informational, no reciprocal required).
