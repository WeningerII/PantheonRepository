REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added three genuinely individuated, deified-historical benzhu that Bai scholarship documents beyond the existing set: Li Mi (李宓), the Tang general annihilated in the 754 Tianbao War and propitiated at the General's Cave near Xiaguan; Xinuluo (细奴逻), the seventh-century Mengshe founder of the Nanzhao line enshrined as ancestor and mountain lord at Weibaoshan; and Zheng Hui (郑回), the captured Tang official who became Nanzhao's chief councillor and was deified as a patron of statecraft and learning (wired as subordinate-to the royal house Xinuluo founded). Each rests on at least two real scholarly works (Backus 1981; Bryson 2015 and 2016; Liang 2021). I deliberately excluded Zhang Lejinqiu (White Mywa/Baizi Guo chief), Duan Jianwei (Tianbao-War general), and the Nanzhao kings Piluoge/Geluofeng: all are historically attested and plausibly venerated, but I could not confirm a specific, individuated benzhu cult for them in the accessible scholarship, and padding with uncertain names would be worse than a tightly-cited set of three.
