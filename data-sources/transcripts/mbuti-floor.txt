REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the three individuated figures the attested record still supported: Baatsi, the Efe first man kneaded from clay under the tahu-tree prohibition (explicitly deferred by the earlier authoring pass; Schebesta 1936; Scheub 2000), now tied to his attested maker Arebati; Masupa, the hidden smith-creator of Schebesta's Efe narrative whose withdrawal down the river brings toil, childbirth-pain and death (Schebesta 1934/1936; with Sawada 2001 recording the caution that the unified Efe creator is partly Schebesta's monotheistic construction); and Kalisia, the Ituri lord of forest and game with a concrete hunters' dream-oracle and offering-hut cult (Roome 1934; Zuesse 1979 'ndura nde Kalisia'; Jordan 1993). Each entry preserves the corpus's standing caution that these god-names are band-variants of one forest divinity rather than a tidy pantheon, so no symmetric equations were asserted against existing entries. Excluded after source-checking: Gor the thunder-elephant and the chameleon (Khonvoum's messengers, already recorded on his entry and tertiary-only), Mugasa/Mungu (Bantu loan god-names with no separable Mbuti myth, per the corpus's Muungu precedent), Epilipili (name-only equation already carried as an Arebati alt), and 'Matu'/'Muri-Muri' (a named mother of the fire-theft god circulating only in fan compendia such as TV Tropes and unverifiable in Schebesta, Scheub or any reachable scholarship, so Tore's mother remains unnamed and unauthored).
