REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added one genuinely individuated, well-attested being missing from the corpus: Pönege (Pünegusse), the man-eating forest ogre who is the recurring monstrous antagonist of the It'a/Icha tale cycle, documented in the Encyclopaedia of Uralic Mythologies vol. 4 (Tuchkova, Kuznetsova, Kazakevich et al. 2010), Pelikh's Sel'kupskaya mifologiya (1998), and Maloney's 2022 Hamburg study of Selkup folklore. It is left solitary because the sources give it no genealogical tie into the divine family; the directed relation kinds available do not accurately capture "adversary slain by It'a" (Icha is the slayer, not the slain, and symmetric enemy relations may not point at existing corpus ids), so the antagonism with the already-registered hero Icha is documented in prose. I deliberately did NOT add several checked candidates: the "Earth Old Woman" (Chvechyt Paya/Paya) is described in the sources as merging with — i.e. a southern regional aspect of — the already-registered life-giving Ylynta Kota; the forest-mistress and per-lake/river water-spirits are unnamed loz classes (the loz class is already registered); sun, moon and thunder are treated as aspects/voice of Num/Nom; and the cosmogonic earth-diver bird is not individuated by name. The attested individuated record beyond the existing five figures is therefore close to exhausted, with Pönege the one solid addition.
