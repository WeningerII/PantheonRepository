================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added seven attested figures: the subterranean grandmother goddesses Yak Takel and Yak Kalcheng, the divine grandchild Begjag, and the goddess Jalang with her kin Ag-ag the crow and Klang the hawk (all documented in Evans, The Negritos of Malaya, 1937, ch. XIV "The Negrito Deities", the Jalang family at p. 149, as transmitted in the Encyclopedia Mythica digests of that chapter), plus the Batek creator god Tohan (Endicott, Batek Negrito Religion, 1979; Ramle Abdullah et al. 2014). I deliberately excluded Ple, Skeat & Blagden's (1906) Kedah demiurge who made the earth and mankind under the thunder god Kari, as too likely a doublet of the existing Ta Pedn, whose recorded role it mirrors. Likewise excluded were the Batek underground "Grandmother" Ya' and the serpent names Dranuk and Baji (Endicott 1979; Tacey 2013; Tacey & Riboli 2014), which the scholarship treats as local forms of the existing Manoid-type earth grandmother and the earth-serpent Naga', and the name "Karpgen", which appears only in AI-generated tertiary material and could not be verified. The cenoi flower-spirits and hala' asal were left aside as unindividuated spirit-classes.
