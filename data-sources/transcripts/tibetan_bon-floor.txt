REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added five distinctly Bön (g.Yung-drung Bön) figures absent from the corpus, each attested in mainstream Tibetological scholarship: the creator/demiurge Sangpo Bumtri, the light-god Shenlha Ökar, the wisdom-mother Satrig Ersang (Sherab Chamma), the supreme wrathful protectress Sipe Gyalmo, and the Zhang-zhung tutelary Welchen Gekhö (Zhang Zhung Meri). The three primordial lords (Shenlha Ökar, Satrig Ersang, Sangpo Bumtri) are wired together as co-members of the Four Transcendent Lords, and Sipe Gyalmo is linked as the wrathful emanation of Satrig Ersang, following Kvaerne, Snellgrove, Karmay, Norbu and Bellezza. I deliberately excluded the founder Tönpa Shenrab (already registered under the separate 'Bön' tradition) and the Tibetan Buddhist protectors, and left generic yul-lha/nyen classes to the existing figures; the named, individuated Bön divine beyond these five is essentially exhausted in the cited record.
