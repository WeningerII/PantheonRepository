================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added three individuated figures securely attested in the core Senufo ethnographies: the ancestress Nerejao, revered head of the Poro society (Glaze 1981; Gagliardi 2014); Fo, the primordial python who carries diviners' questions to the madebele bush spirits (Glaze 1975, 1981; Goldwater 1964; Britannica); and Porpianong/Kasingele, the primordial hornbill "first ancestor" who scattered the seeds of life at creation (Goldwater 1964; Glaze 1981; Gagliardi 2014). Nerejao is wired to the existing Maleeo (Poro works for the Ancient Mother) and Fo to the existing madebele. I examined and excluded kafigeledjo (an oracle-object class, not an individuated being), the unnamed tugubele/kuubele spirit classes, and alternate creator names (Yirigefolo, Nyehene, Koulotiolo variants) that are aliases of the existing Kolotyolo/Maleeo pair; beyond these, the scholarly record for Senufo religion is largely non-individuated.
