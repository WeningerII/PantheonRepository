================================================================================
THE GAP — THE CORPUS HELD HAYK AND NOT BEL
================================================================================

A 68-name check across the WHOLE registry returned 28 hits and 40 absences.

**Hayk is in the registry. Bel is not.**

The founding act of the Armenian nation, as Movses Khorenatsi tells it, is a
single arrow. Hayk refuses to submit to Bel, the tyrant of Babylon; Bel follows
him north with an army; Hayk draws a wide bow and shoots him through the breast;
and the people take their name — *Hay* — from the man who fired it. The corpus
held the archer and not the target.

That is this programme's recurring finding once more, and in the form it has
taken in eleven traditions running: **the registry holds the famous name and not
the thing it is defined against.**

It also held **Vahagn Vishapakagh**, "Vahagn the Dragon-reaper", and no dragon.

================================================================================
THE PANTHEON WAS HELD AT FOUR
================================================================================

The `pantheon-era` bucket contained **Aramazd, Anahit, Astghik and Vahagn** —
and nothing else. Absent:

    Mihr          god of light, heaven and the sun; the temple at Garni is his
    Nane          goddess of motherhood, war and wisdom, with spear and shield
    Tir           god of learning, writing and the sciences, and the interpreter
                  of dreams; the messenger of the gods
    Barsamin      the weather and sky god
    Vanatur       the New Year god of Navasard, and by one account the supreme
                  god of the pantheon BEFORE Aramazd displaced him
    Spandaramet   god of the underworld
    Tork Angegh   the giant who hurls boulders across the sea and scratches
                  pictures into rock with his fingernails
    Grogh         "the writer", who records what a person does and settles what
                  becomes of them

**Vanatur is the one that costs most.** A tradition whose pantheon the corpus
held four of was missing the god that pantheon is said to have replaced.

================================================================================
AND A REGISTERED ERA WITH NOBODY IN IT
================================================================================

The Armenian era vocabulary registers five eras. Two of them held **nothing**:

    pantheon-era               4 records
    haykazuni-mythohistorical 12 records
    arsacid-historical         0
    medieval-sasna-tsrer       7 records
    modern-collection          0

Lane 19 found six empty registered eras in the Welsh vocabulary and lane 20
found one in the Finnish. `arsacid-historical` is the third case, and it is the
span of the **Vipasankʻ** — the epic Khorenatsi quotes from and says the
minstrels of his own day were still singing: Artashes lassoing the Alan princess
Satenik with a red cord and a golden ring, the rain of gold at the wedding, the
feast Argavan sets as a trap, and Artashes cursing his own son.

**That era now holds four records.** `modern-collection` still holds none, and
this batch declines to fill it, for the reason lane 20 gave about Elias Lönnrot:
it is the era of **Garegin Srvantdziants**, who wrote the Sasna Tsrer down in
1873, and a collector is not a figure of the tradition.

================================================================================
WHAT THIS BATCH DOES NOT INGEST
================================================================================

The **aralez** — the winged dog-like spirits who come down to lick a dead hero's
wounds, and whom Shamiram calls on for Ara — are a **collective of unnamed
beings**, and are rejected on the ground that rejected the Cwn Annwn in lane 19
and the Matronae in lane 22, even though they are the most distinctive thing in
Armenian mythology and the corpus already holds both figures of the story they
belong to. The **vishaps** are likewise a class, and the **shahapet** of the
hearth is a class. Garni, Bagaharich, Ashtishat and Masis are **places**.
Navasard and Vardavar are **festivals**.
