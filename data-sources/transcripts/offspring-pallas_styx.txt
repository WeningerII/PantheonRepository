Tradition: Greek (Hesiodic theogony)
Era (registered, exact): "titanomachy"
Cluster: children of Pallas (greek_hesiod_pallas_titan, deity) by the Oceanid Styx

Author pass: the winged enforcer-spirits of Zeus — the personified abstractions

================================================================================
RESEARCH SUMMARY AND AUTHORING DECISION
================================================================================

The locus classicus is Hesiod, Theogony 383–403: "And Styx the daughter of Ocean
was joined to Pallas and bore Zelos (Emulation/Rivalry) and trim-ankled Nike
(Victory) in the house; and she brought forth Kratos (Strength) and Bia (Force),
wonderful children. These have no house apart from Zeus, nor any dwelling nor path
except where the god leads them, but they dwell always beside Zeus the loud-
thunderer." When Styx was first of the immortals to come with her children to aid
Zeus in the war against the Titans, Zeus honoured her by keeping her offspring at
his side forever (Theog. 389–403). The four are thus the personified war-powers
and winged attendants/enforcers of the throne of Zeus.

Two of the four (Kratos and Bia) are the named agents who open Aeschylus,
Prometheus Bound (1–87): sent by Zeus, they drag the captive Prometheus to the
Scythian crag and compel the reluctant Hephaestus to fetter him. Kratos
(Strength/Power) speaks and embodies brute, pitiless authority; Bia (Force) stands
by in silence. Apollodorus, Bibliotheca 1.2.4 repeats the Hesiodic parentage
(Pallas + Styx → Nike, Kratos, Zelos, Bia).

Standard modern scholarship used here:
  • M. L. West, Hesiod: Theogony, Edited with Prolegomena and Commentary (Oxford:
    Clarendon Press, 1966), on lines 383–403 (the Styx genealogy and the war-
    powers as throne-attendants of Zeus).
  • Timothy Gantz, Early Greek Myth: A Guide to Literary and Artistic Sources
    (Baltimore: Johns Hopkins University Press, 1993), pp. 26–28 (children of
    Pallas and Styx).
  • A. F. Garvie / Mark Griffith, ed., Aeschylus: Prometheus Bound (Cambridge:
    Cambridge University Press, 1983), on the prologue (Kratos and Bia).

THREE firmly-attested children are authored here: ZELUS (Zelos), KRATOS (Kratos/
Cratus) and BIA. NIKE is NOT re-authored — she already exists as
greek_hesiod_nike — and is wired as their sibling (one-way, since the reciprocal
must be added on the existing Nike entry).

TYPE (by descent). Both parents are deities — Pallas (greek_hesiod_pallas_titan,
divinity fraction 1.0) and Styx (greek_hesiod_styx, 1.0). The average is 1.0
(> 0.75), so each child is a DEITY. They are personified-abstraction deities and
are deathless throne-attendants of Zeus (vitalStatus living; no death/transform
event, so no lifecycle array is authored, matching the existing Nike entry which
likewise omits lifecycle).

WIRING.
  - parentIds: ["greek_hesiod_pallas_titan", "greek_hesiod_styx"]
    parentRoles: { greek_hesiod_pallas_titan: "father",
                   greek_hesiod_styx: "mother" } (Theog. 383–385; Apollod. 1.2.4)
  - sibling → each of the other two new children (reciprocated WITHIN this batch)
  - sibling → greek_hesiod_nike (existing; one-way here — reciprocal to be added
    on the Nike entry)
