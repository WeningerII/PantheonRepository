================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the two remaining individuated figures of the documented Krachi corpus: Owuo, the giant personification of Death from the famous Krachi origin-of-death tale (Cardinall 1931; Abrahamsson 1951; Scheub 2000; Lynch 2010), and Asase Ya, the named Mother Earth on whom Wulbari lay in the Krachi cosmogony (Cardinall 1931; Radin 1952; Beier 1966; Leeming 2005), wiring Asase Ya to the existing Wulbari as consort. Bruku of Shiare and Akonedi of Larteh, though ritually networked with the Dente shrine, were excluded as deities of the neighbouring Adele and Larteh-Guan traditions rather than of Krachi proper (Maier, Priests and Power, 1983). The remaining actors in Cardinall's Krachi tales — the pestle-pounding old woman, the death-bringing bird, and the first men and women lowered from the sky — are unnamed and therefore not individuated.
