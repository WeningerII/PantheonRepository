Figure-authoring batch — smallest-first sweep, installment 1: the Etruscan and
Hittite pantheons.

Two of the smallest traditions in the corpus are also among the best documented.
Etruscan carries only Tinia, Uni, Menrva and Turms; Hittite carries only three
historical Great Kings and not one deity (its Kumarbi-cycle gods are filed under
Hurrian). This installment adds the major missing deities of each, sourced to
primary epigraphy and text: for Etruscan, the Liver of Piacenza, the Pyrgi
tablets, Martianus Capella's god-list, engraved bronze mirrors, the painted
tombs, and Cicero; for Hittite, Hoffner's edition of the Hittite Myths
(Telipinu CTH 324, Illuyanka CTH 321), the prayers to the Sun-goddess of Arinna
(CTH 376), and the Yazılıkaya reliefs. The Hittite storm-god and sun-goddess are
wired to their children by parentIds, forming the state-cult family.
