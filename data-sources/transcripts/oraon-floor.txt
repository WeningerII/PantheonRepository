================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added three genuinely individuated, well-attested Oraon powers absent from the existing set: Anna Kuari, the grain-and-wealth goddess famous from Frazer and Dalton for her former human sacrifices; Chandi, the local Oraon goddess of the hunt documented in the Encyclopedia of World Cultures and Roy; and Darha, the chief tutelary/village-guardian bhut central to Roy's classification of Oraon village spirits. Each is wired into the pantheon as subordinate to the existing supreme creator Dharmes, consistent with the attested Oraon hierarchy of spiritual beings. I deliberately excluded Chala Pachho, which Roy explicitly identifies as an alias of the already-registered Sarna Burhi ("Chala Pachcho, or Sarna Burhia"), and I omitted generic classes such as Khunt bhuts and unnamed ancestral spirits, which are not individuated named figures. Remaining minor village names (Pat Raja, Desauli, Mahadania) were left out because their Oraon-specific individuation is thin or conflated with neighbouring Munda/Ho usage in the sources I checked (Roy 1915/1928, Dalton 1872, Frazer 1922, Encyclopedia of World Cultures vol. 3).
