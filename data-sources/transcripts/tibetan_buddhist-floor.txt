REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added six well-attested, individuated Tibetan Buddhist protector deities absent from the corpus: Begtse (war god, Heller 1992; Nebesky-Wojkowitz 1956), Tsiu Marpo (Samye's protector, Bell 2006), Dorje Legpa and Za Rahula (two of the three Nyingma Dzogchen guardians, cross-linked as allies; the third, Ekajati, was left out as a pan-Buddhist Tara-related figure closer to the corpus 'Buddhist' tradition), Tashi Tseringma (chief of the Five Long-Life Sisters, Chang 1962), and Dorje Shugden (included with explicit treatment of the controversy per Dreyfus 1998). Deliberately skipped: Nyenchen Thanglha (already in the corpus as tibetan_nyenchen_tanglha under Tibetan Bön) and Dorje Drakden (documented in the corpus inside the Pehar entry as his emanation/minister, so authoring him separately would risk duplicating an aspect of an existing figure). All citations are real scholarship, anchored on Nebesky-Wojkowitz's Oracles and Demons of Tibet (1956).
