REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added four well-attested individuated Salish figures: Fox (Interior Salish animal-person and Coyote's reviving elder brother; Teit 1912, Boas 1917), Scomalt (giant woman chief of the Okanagan island-flood origin narrative; Clark 1953, Erdoes & Ortiz 1984), Xá:ls the Coast Salish Transformer (Carlson 2001, Hill-Tout 1978, Jenness 1955), and Dohkwibuhch the Snohomish creator-changer (Clark 1953, Haeberlin & Gunther 1930). I deliberately excluded Amotken because his name literally means "the old one (who sits on the mountain top)" and he overlaps with the existing salish_old_one/Great Chief Above entry; likewise I authored no generic spirit-classes (sxwixwiyam ancestors, unnamed stl'áleqem beings). Fraternal ties between Fox and the pre-existing Coyote could not be expressed because sibling/companion kinds are symmetric-only, so Fox is documented in solitary instead.
