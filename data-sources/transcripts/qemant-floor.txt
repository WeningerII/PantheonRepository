REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the six named qedus (angel-like intermediaries) of Qemant traditional religion documented in Gamst's 1969 ethnography and repeated in derivative reference literature: Jakaranti (the most respected), Mezgani and Anzatatera (next in rank), and the lesser Kiberwa, Aderaiki, and Shemani; each is wired to the existing sky god Mezgana via a subordinate-to relation. I deliberately excluded Saytan (the pan-Ethiopian devil figure, not individuated to this tradition), the generic zar and other spirit classes, and the moiety labels Keber/Yetanti, which name descent groups rather than clearly individuated ancestor figures beyond the existing Anayer. Note that primary-source fetches (archive.org, Wikipedia) were proxy-blocked, so attestation rests on Gamst 1969 as reported consistently across multiple search-corroborated tertiary summaries; a reviewer with library access should verify exact page numbers in Gamst 1969 (religion chapter) and the spelling Aderaiki vs. Aderaika.
