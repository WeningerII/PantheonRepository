REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added five well-attested individuated Bathouism deities beyond Bathoubwrai and Mainao: the household god Song Raja, the tiger king Bura Bagh Raja, and the three chief Kherai attendants Agrang (general), Khoila (messenger) and Ailong (earth/bodyguard), each wired to Bathoubwrai via a directed subordinate-to relation. Attestation rests on Sidney Endle, The Kacharis (1911), Kameswar Brahma, A Study of Socio-Religious Beliefs, Practices and Ceremonies of the Bodos (1992), and Sekhar Brahma, Religion of the Boros and Their Socio-Cultural Transition (2006). I deliberately excluded the schematic list of eighteen divine couples (Si Bwrai/Si Burwi, Bwlli Bwrai/Bwlli Burwi, etc.), which the sources present as paired name-classes rather than individuated figures with independent mythology, and generic named consorts (Song Rani) attested only as pair-fillers.
