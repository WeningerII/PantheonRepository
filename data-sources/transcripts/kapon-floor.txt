REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added four well-attested individuated Kapon figures: Pia, twin brother of Makonaima and first shaman (Roth 1915; Brett 1880); Sigu, the Akawaio flood hero and son of Makonaima (Brett 1880; Roth 1915); Piai'ma, the named mountain ogre and archetypal master of shamanic power (Butt 1977; Whitehead 2002; Roth 1915); and Abel, the historical prophet-founder of the Hallelujah/Areruya religion (Butt Colson 1960). Generic spirit classes such as kanaima assassins and imawali forest spirits were deliberately excluded as non-individuated. Sigu is genealogically linked to the existing Makonaima entry; Pia's twin relationship is documented in prose because symmetric links to pre-existing entries are disallowed.
