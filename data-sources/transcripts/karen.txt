In the myth Y'wa gave writing to each of his children; the Karen elder brother, careless or unlucky, lost his book, which was burnt or devoured by fowls and swine, leaving the people illiterate and diminished, while the younger brother preserved his and sailed away across the sea, promising one day to bring the book back.
