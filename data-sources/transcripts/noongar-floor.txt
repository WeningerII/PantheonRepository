================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added three individuated, well-attested Noongar figures beyond the existing Wagyl: Yondock, the ancestral saltwater crocodile killed by the Waugal at the Swan River mouth (Walley's Derbal Nara narrative; City of Fremantle Whadjuk significance study; Collard, Westerly 54); the Charnok Woman, the Milky Way spirit woman documented by Whadjuk elder Noel Nannup (Moondang-ak Kaaradjiny, 2008) and City of Joondalup heritage material; and Mulka, the cross-eyed giant of Mulka's Cave, whose legend is recorded in Gunn's Records of the Western Australian Museum study (2006). I deliberately excluded spirit classes (djanga, woodarchi/bulyit, charnok beings generally) and thinly attested names such as Motogon (a Salvado-era report surviving mainly via the Dictionnaire Infernal, with no corroborating ethnography) and the Charnok Man and Balyet, whose documentation I could not verify to a scholarly standard. Relations to the Wagyl (its killing of Yondock, its river valley crossed by the Charnok Woman) are described in prose and sources, since no validator-approved directed relation kind expresses being slain by an existing figure.
