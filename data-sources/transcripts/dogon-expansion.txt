DOGON PANTHEON EXPANSION — verify-first authoring transcript
=============================================================

Scope: well-attested Dogon (Mali, West Africa) figures NOT already in the corpus.
Already present (NOT re-authored): dogon_amma, dogon_lebe, dogon_nommo, dogon_ogo.

Core authorities cited at WORK level (no invented page numbers):
  - Marcel Griaule, Dieu d'eau: entretiens avec Ogotemmêli (1948) / Conversations with Ogotemmêli (Eng. ed. 1965)
  - Marcel Griaule & Germaine Dieterlen, Le renard pâle (Paris, 1965)

VERIFICATION SUMMARY (WebSearch, 2026-06):
  * The Smith / Master of Fire (le Forgeron): the first ancestor who, having received Amma's
    granary, stole a coal of the Sun from the celestial smithy, hid it in his bellows, fled the
    Nommo's lightning/thunderbolts and slid down the rainbow to bring fire and the crafts to earth;
    the granary fell after him and burst, scattering people, animals and seeds. Distinct from Nommo
    and from the slain seventh ancestor (the Smith is the agent who instigated the seventh's death).
    [Griaule, Conversations with Ogotemmêli (1948/1965)]
  * The Eight Ancestors: four twin couples (eight beings, also called Nommo) drawn from the body of
    the sacrificed Nommo, sent down in the ark with all that humankind would need; ancestors of the
    eight families / four lineages (Dyon, Ono, Arou, Domno). NOT individually named as a stable set
    in the popular sources beyond the four "Sérou" eponyms — authored as ONE collective figure.
    [Griaule, Conversations with Ogotemmêli (1948/1965); Griaule & Dieterlen, Le renard pâle (1965)]
  * Dyongou Sérou (Dyougou Sérou): named first ancestor; received the first/primitive Word; by
    speaking the language of one world in the other (and by an incest/transgression motif) he broke
    the order and became the first to enter DEATH; his death is re-enacted in the great Sigui rite,
    origin of the masks. Founder of a lineage. [Griaule & Dieterlen, Le renard pâle (1965)]
  * Binou Sérou (Binu Seru): named immortal ancestor "of the time before death"; eponym of the Binu
    totemic cult; founder of the Ono lineage; manifests to descendants as the clan totem animal and
    is served at painted Binu shrines with millet and blood offerings.
    [Griaule & Dieterlen, Le renard pâle (1965); Dieterlen, on the Binu/clan-ancestor cult]
  * The Yeban (Yéban): underworld earth-genies — small beings with big heads, discoloured bodies and
    frail limbs — engendered (incestuously, like himself) by Ogo the Pale Fox; they hid in holes of
    the earth; they in turn engendered the Andoumboulou. [Griaule & Dieterlen, Le renard pâle (1965)]
  * The Andoumboulou: imperfect/failed first little humans, smaller than the Yeban, dwellers in
    earth-holes who could turn into serpents; the Dogon trace the origin of the masks and of the
    Sigui to them. [Griaule & Dieterlen, Le renard pâle (1965)]

REJECTED: "Dada" (not attested as a named Dogon figure); a separate "Yurugu" (= dogon_ogo, who BECOMES
the Pale Fox — would duplicate); a separate "Lébé Sérou" (= existing dogon_lebe); "Amma Sérou" as a
standalone (thinly distinct from creator Amma and name-colliding — folded into the Eight Ancestors).

KINSHIP NOTE: Le renard pâle has the eight ancestors drawn from the body of the sacrificed Nommo;
the Sérou ancestors (incl. the existing Lébé Sérou = dogon_lebe) are therefore set as children of
dogon_nommo here and as siblings of one another and of dogon_lebe. The Yeban descend from dogon_ogo;
the Andoumboulou descend from the Yeban.
