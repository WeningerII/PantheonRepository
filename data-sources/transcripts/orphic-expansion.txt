PANTHEON REGISTRY — FIGURE-AUTHORING TRANSCRIPT
Tradition: Orphic
Era (registered, exact): "Orphic traditional religion; continuing"
Author pass: Lane 1 — Orphic held 11 records and was UNCLAIMED. This adds the
             9 figures that are distinctively Orphic: the first principles of
             the theogonies, the deities named on the gold leaves, and the
             obscure goddesses known from the Hymns and from Anatolian stone.

================================================================================
SCOPE — WHY ONLY NINE
================================================================================

The existing 11 records already carry the Orphic core: Phanes (with Protogonos,
Erikepaios, Metis and Eros as aliases), Chronos, Ananke, Nyx, Zagreus, the
Orphic Zeus, Persephone, Dionysos, Mnemosyne, Melinoe and Orpheus.

This registry's practice is to keep an Orphic record beside the Greek one where
the Orphic material treats the figure distinctively — `orphic_nyx` stands
apart from `greek_hesiod_nyx`, `orphic_persephone` from
`greek_hesiod_persephone`. That practice could be used to justify an Orphic
record for every deity addressed in the 87 Orphic Hymns, which would mean
dozens of near-duplicates of Greek gods differing only in which hymn addresses
them.

That is deliberately NOT done here. The bar applied is: **the figure's
existence or character comes from the Orphic material itself**, not from a
Greek god happening to receive an Orphic hymn. Nine candidates clear it:

- **First principles** the Orphic theogonies name and Hesiod does not —
  Hydros, Thesis, Aither, Physis.
- **Gold-leaf deities** — Eukles and Brimo, named on the inscribed tablets
  buried with initiates.
- **Anatolian hymn-goddesses** — Hipta and Mise. These are the exact peers of
  Melinoe, already in this registry: all three were known ONLY from the Hymns
  until inscriptions from western Asia Minor confirmed their cult. Admitting
  Melinoe and excluding the other two would be inconsistent.
- **Musaeus**, the addressee of the proem that heads the Hymns.

Deliberately excluded: an Orphic Chaos, Gaia, Ouranos, Kronos, Rhea or Eros.
Each already exists in the Greek tradition, and the Orphic material gives them
a place in a different theogony rather than a different character. Adding them
would inflate the corpus without adding knowledge.
