Added two individuated, well-cited figures missing from the corpus: Oshe, the male moon of the Shipibo huito-stain incest myth (Margery Peña 1998; Roe 1982; Loriot et al. 1993), and Cori Inca, the beneficent "Gold Inca" hero of order paired against the existing Yoashico Inca (Bertrand-Rousseau 1984, Anthropologica 2(2); Roe 1988 in Hill, Rethinking History and Myth; Morales Chocano & Mujica Baquerizo 2019). Symmetric relation kinds may not target pre-existing ids and no directed kind (parent, consort, slayer, etc.) fits Cori Inca's rivalry with Yoashico Inca or Oshe's story, so both are documented as standing alone. Candidates rejected as non-individuated or under-attested: the chaikonibo (invisible perfected people), the ibo spirit-owners, the Wishmabo/Pleiades children (all classes or collectives), and "Wiso Ino" (black jaguar, attested only on practitioner websites).
