================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added two genuinely individuated figures documented in the standard ethnographic literature beyond the existing set. Aiwel Longar (Aiwel), the semi-divine culture hero born of a mortal woman and a river-divinity, is the archetypal first Master of the Fishing Spear and founder of the spear-master priesthood, wired to Nhialic (subordinate-to) and to the divinity Flesh (creator-of). Flesh (Dinka ring) is the named, hereditary clan-divinity of the spear-masters that Aiwel conferred, tied to Nhialic as one of its refractions. I deliberately excluded the many clan-divinity classes Lienhardt lists (Grass/Mon, Fibre, various snake and lion divinities) and the colwic (people struck by lightning), since these are species- or category-associated spirit-classes rather than named individuals; the well-attested individuated Dinka pantheon appears essentially exhausted by the existing five plus these two. Sources checked: Lienhardt, Divinity and Experience: The Religion of the Dinka (1961); Francis Mading Deng, Dinka Cosmology (1980) and The Dinka and Their Songs (1973); Encyclopaedia Britannica 'Aiwel'; and the Wikipedia/encyclopaedic summaries of Dinka religion.
