REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added five well-attested Crow (Apsáalooke) figures beyond the Old Man Coyote pair: the Sun (Áxxaashe), a principal deity per Lowie's Religion of the Crow Indians (1922); his son the monster-slaying hero Old Woman's Grandchild, identified with the Morning Star in McCleary's The Stars We Know (1997); the ogress Red Woman (Hisshishtawia), attested in Lowie 1918; and the twin heroes Thrown-behind-the-Curtain and Thrown-into-the-Spring of the Crow 'Lodge Boy and Thrown Away' cycle (Lowie 1918; Thompson 1929). Genealogy and slayer relations follow the recorded narratives; symmetric twin-sibling links are reciprocated within the new set. Remaining Crow narrative characters (e.g., unnamed ducks in the earth-diver myth, generic star beings) are not individuated named figures and were excluded.
