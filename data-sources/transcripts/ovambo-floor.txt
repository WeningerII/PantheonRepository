================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the three individuated ancestral figures of the Aawambo origin tradition: Kamangundu (Mangundu), the first human created by Kalunga or emerging from the Omumborombonga tree, and his sons Nangombe ya Mangundu (progenitor of the Aawambo) and Kathu (progenitor of the Ovaherero), attested in Williams (1991), Werner (1933), Scheub (2000) and Aarni (1982). Kamangundu is wired to the existing ovambo_kalunga as creator-parent. Beyond these, the scholarship on Ovambo religion (Aarni 1982; Hiltunen, Witchcraft and Sorcery in Ovambo, 1986) documents only Kalunga, Musisi, and unnamed spirit classes (aathithi, oompwidhuli), so no further individuated figures qualified.
