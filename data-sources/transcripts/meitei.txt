Pantheon Registry — research transcript
Tradition: Manipuri (Meitei / Sanamahi — the indigenous Sanamahism of the Kingdom of Kangleipak / modern Manipur, NE India and adjacent Meitei diaspora)

Scope: the well-attested CORE of the Sanamahi / Umang-Lai pantheon, which the existing corpus was missing. Authored against the standard scholarship — Saroj Nalini Arambam Parratt, *The Religion of Manipur* (1980; rev. as *The Religion of Manipur: Beliefs, Rituals and Historical Development*) and T. C. Hodson, *The Meitheis* (1908) — supplemented by the Meitei puya tradition and modern Meitei (Sanamahi-laining) reference sources. Older Meitei orthography (Meitei Mayek) is given where the existing corpus already records it.

DEDUPLICATION NOTE (Atingkok vs. the existing manipuri_salailen).
The task asked whether Salailen (Soraren) is distinct from, or an aspect of, the creator Atingkok. The scholarly and traditional consensus is that they are ONE supreme being under different theological titles. Parratt states plainly: "the supreme god is Atiya Guru Sidaba or Sidaba Mapu, and the Manipuri puranas make Sanamahi a son of Guru Sidaba and Leimaren Sidabi, and elder brother of Pakhangba." Multiple Meitei reference sources reconcile the names explicitly — "Salailen is also known as Soraren, Atiya, Atinga, or Atingkok ... all these terms are meant for a single God." The existing figure manipuri_salailen ALREADY carries "Atingkok Maru Sidaba (alternate-tradition equivalent)" in its name.alt and its notes already state it is "the same supreme-creator-figure under different titles." Therefore I do NOT author a separate manipuri_atingkok figure (that would duplicate manipuri_salailen). Instead, the new figures are wired to the existing manipuri_salailen as the creator/father (Atingkok = Salailen). The creator slot in the requested list is thus satisfied by the pre-existing figure, per the "do not duplicate" rule.

What is authored below (9 figures, all new): Leimarel Sidabi (earth-mother / creator's consort); Lainingthou Sanamahi (eldest son, household god); Pakhangba (younger son, ancestral dragon / royal deity); Panthoibi (warrior-love goddess) and Nongpok Ningthou (her consort, the eastern god); and the four cardinal Umang Lai "guardians of the directions" (Maikei Ngakpa Lai) — Thangjing (SW, Moirang), Marjing (NE), Wangbren / Wangpulen (SE), Koubru / Koupalu (NW).

Key wiring: manipuri_salailen (= Atingkok, pre-existing) ⇄ manipuri_leimarel as spouse; Sanamahi & Pakhangba as reciprocal siblings, both sons of Salailen + Leimarel (parentIds + father/mother); Nongpok Ningthou ⇄ Panthoibi as spouse; the four directional gods cross-linked as co-guardian companions of the Maikei Ngakpa Lai. Panthoibi is additionally recorded as a divine incarnation of Leimarel Sidabi (reciprocal equated-with).

The variant where Leimarel is Sanamahi's FOSTER-mother (and Pakhangba alone is her birth-son) is noted in the figures; the dominant Parratt/purana line — both are her sons — is used for the structural genealogy.
