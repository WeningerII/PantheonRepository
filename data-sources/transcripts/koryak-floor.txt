REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added three genuinely individuated, well-attested figures beyond the existing Raven-family set. Cloud-Man (Yahal/Yahalan) is a named Koryak sky deity, son of the Supreme Being and patron of marriage, who weds Yiniaaneut in the Raven cycle — wired to koryak_universe as father and to koryak_yiniaaneut as consort. Illa and Kilu are the only two named members of the younger generation of Big-Raven's kin in Koryak oral tradition (nephew and niece, or in one variant the creator's children); they are wired into the pantheon through their canonical cousin-marriages to the existing Yiniaaneut and Ememqut respectively and to each other as siblings. I deliberately excluded Raven-Man and Little-Bird-Man because their clearest attestation is Chukchee (Bogoras) rather than securely Koryak, and I treated kalau/kamak spirits as an unnamed class rather than individuated figures; each added figure carries two primary ethnographic citations (Jochelson 1908/1904, Bogoras 1917).
