REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added four individuated, well-attested figures beyond the existing four, each wired into the pantheon: Pumo Hniyyr, the weaving virgin-mother of the hero Zhyge Alu (mother-of yi_zhyge_alu); Qobu Jjumu, the ancestral patriarch and father of the flood survivor (father-of yi_jjumu_vuvu); and the bimo pair Asu Lazzi (Ashy Lazzi), an eminent ancestral priest, and his daughter Lazzi Shysi, the one woman remembered to have practiced as a bimo in male disguise. All rest on the University of Washington Press translation of the Book of Origins (Bender, Aku Wuwu, Zopqu 2019), Bender's "Tribes of Snow" (2008), Bamo Qubumo's Oral Tradition study (2001), and the Liangshan bimo ethnographies of Bamo Ayi (2001; Fieldwork Connections 2007) supported by Kraef (2014). I deliberately excluded the Torch Festival wrestlers Atilaba and Sireabi: although the wrestling contest is attested, the surviving accounts of those named champions are Sinicized/pan-Yi and lack the two clean Nuosu-specific scholarly citations this dataset requires, so padding with them would have lowered rigor.
