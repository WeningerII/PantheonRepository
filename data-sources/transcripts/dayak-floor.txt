================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added six well-attested Ngaju Dayak figures: the first human couple Manyamei Tunggul Garing and Kameloh Putak Bulau, their three sons Maharaja Sangiang, Maharaja Sangen, and Maharaja Bunu (ancestors respectively of the spirit beings, the primeval heroes, and mortal humankind), all documented in Hans Schärer's Ngaju Religion (1963) and Anne Schiller's Small Sacrifices (1997); and the trickster hero Sangumang from the folktale corpus of Klokke-Coster, Klokke and Saha (1976). The new figures are wired to the existing corpus through subordinate-to relations with dayak_mahatala. Remaining Ngaju supernatural categories (sangiang, gana, generic ancestor spirits) are spirit classes rather than individuated figures and were excluded.
