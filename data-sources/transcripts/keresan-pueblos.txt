In the eastern Keresan pueblo of Sia she appears as Sussistinnako, the spider, the very first being, who draws lines of sacred meal and sings until living creatures come into existence. Because thought and word are the instruments of her creation, later writers, and the Laguna author Leslie Marmon Silko, have made her the emblem of storytelling itself: what she thinks and names becomes real.
