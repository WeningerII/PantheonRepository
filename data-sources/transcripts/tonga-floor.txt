================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the two genuinely individuated, named figures that Zambian/Zimbabwean (Gwembe Valley) Tonga scholarship documents beyond Leza and the existing mortals: Nyaminyami, the serpentine Zambezi river god (Chikozho & Mubaya 2015; McGregor 2009; Tremmel 1994), and Monze, the named basangu rain spirit of the plateau rain-shrine cult (Colson 1969, 2006; O'Brien 1983; Kaoma 2017). Both are wired to the existing high god Leza via cited subordinate-to relations reflecting the documented Tonga spiritual hierarchy. Other candidates were rejected as non-individuated: basangu and mizimu are spirit classes, sikatongo and mulela are ritual offices, and figures such as Mweemba are chiefly titles whose attestation as named spirits is too thin; Nyaminyami's wife is unnamed in the scholarship and was therefore excluded.
