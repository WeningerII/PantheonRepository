REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added eight individuated, named Kulin beings verified against primary ethnography and modern scholarship: Karakarook/Karatgurk the fire-keeper of the Pleiades and Karween the crane (both in Smyth 1878, vol. 1), Mindye the punitive great serpent (Smyth 1878; Broome 2005), Balayang the bat and Bunjil's two hawk avengers Djurt-djurt and Thara (Howitt 1904, pp. 128-130; Mudrooroo 1994), Bellin-bellin the wind-keeping musk crow (Howitt 1904; Smyth 1878), and Loo-errn, the Boonwurrung guardian of Wilsons Promontory also claimed by the Gunaikurnai as Loan/Lohan. Bunjil's remaining four young men (Yukope, Dantum, Tadjeri, Turnung) are attested by name in Howitt 1904, p. 128 but only as list members without individual narratives, so I left them out; a reviewer could defensibly add them. Stanbridge's 1857 star beings (Marpean-kurrk, Neilloan, etc.) were excluded as Boorong/Wergaia rather than Kulin. Direct fetching of source texts was blocked by the sandbox egress policy, so attestations were confirmed through search-indexed passages of Smyth and Howitt on Wikisource plus secondary literature; page numbers for Smyth vol. 1 (443-446, 453, 458-459) reflect those indexed passages.
