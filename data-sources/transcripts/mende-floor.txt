REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added five individuated, well-attested Mende figures missing from the corpus: the water genius Tingoi (Njaloi) and the bush genius Ndogbojusui, the two named dyinyinga documented by Harris & Sawyerr (1968), Little (1951), and Gittins (1987), both wired as subordinate to the existing creator Ngewo; the Sande clown Gonde (Phillips 1995; Boone 1986), subordinate to the existing Sowo; the one-armed Poro warrior spirit Falui (Siegmann & Perani 1976; Sierra Leone Heritage), subordinate to the existing Gbeni; and the unaffiliated satirical masker Gongoli (Anderson, Africa 2018; Siegmann & Perani 1976). Other candidates were excluded as unindividuated spirit classes (generic ngafanga ancestors, dyinyinga as a category, society medicines such as those of the Njayei and Humoi) or as aliases of existing entries (Goboi/Gbini under mende_gbeni; Leve under mende_ngewo).
