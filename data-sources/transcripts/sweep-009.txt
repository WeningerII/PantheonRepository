Sweep 009 — strict-alphabetical family-completion sweep, batch 9.
Batch of 50 ids (dogon_ogo … egyptian_ramesses_ii) read IN ORDER, every tradition
treated as equal. All 50 were already present in the corpus; this sweep is a
verification-and-wiring pass over the Heliopolitan Ennead, the Theban and Memphite
triads, and the Ramesside / Thutmosid royal families.

OUTCOME

The Ennead spine (Atum → Shu+Tefnut → Geb+Nut → Osiris/Isis/Set/Nephthys) and the
Ramesside / Thutmosid royal genealogies are densely and correctly wired by
parentIds. The Dogon (Amma→Nommo/Ogo/Lebe, the Smith, the Yeban→Andoumboulou),
Edo (Osanobua→Olokun/Ogiuwu), and Anishinaabe (E-bangishimog→Nanabozho and his
brothers by Wiininwaa) lines are already complete. No symmetric edge among batch
figures or to existing corpus figures was found broken by the validator.

TWO genuinely NAMED-BUT-NO-ENTRY attested divine kin were found and are authored
below. Both are direct kin anchors named inside an existing batch figure:

1. egyptian_mut — the great Theban mother-goddess, consort of Amun and mother of
   the moon-god Khonsu; second member of the Theban Triad. She is named explicitly
   and repeatedly inside the existing Khonsu entry ("Theban moon god of healing;
   son of Amun and Mut"; "third of the Theban triad with Amun and Mut") yet had no
   entry and was the missing maternal parent of Khonsu. Authored and wired:
   spouse → egyptian_amun (RECIPROCAL needed on the existing Amun entry), mother-of
   → egyptian_khonsu (Khonsu's parentIds should add egyptian_mut alongside
   egyptian_amun).

2. egyptian_nefertem — the lotus-god of Memphis, son of Ptah and Sekhmet and third
   member of the Memphite Triad. He is named explicitly inside the existing Ptah
   entry ("Husband of Sekhmet, father of Nefertem") yet had no entry. Authored and
   wired: parentIds [egyptian_ptah, egyptian_sekhmet]; father → egyptian_ptah,
   mother → egyptian_sekhmet (both already exist; parent→child is modelled by
   parentIds in this corpus, so no reciprocal directive is strictly required, but a
   father-of / mother-of back-edge on Ptah and Sekhmet would complete the triad).

WIRE-EDGES on EXISTING corpus figures (no new entry; recorded for the maintainer to
apply to app/data.js — the transcript json holds new figures only):

  The Heliopolitan Ennead sibling/parent matrix is incomplete for Osiris and Isis.
  Set and Nephthys already carry parentIds [egyptian_geb, egyptian_nut] and the
  Set↔Nephthys sibling pair, but Osiris and Isis have empty parentIds and no sibling
  edges, although their own prose states the relationships ("brother-husband of
  Isis ... brother and victim of Set"; "Wife and sister of Osiris"; Nephthys "sister
  of Isis"). The canonical Ennead (Ennead, s.v.; Britannica; Wikipedia "Nephthys")
  makes all four full siblings, children of Geb and Nut. Required edges:

    egyptian_osiris   --sibling-->   egyptian_nephthys

    egyptian_isis     --parentId-->  egyptian_nut
    egyptian_isis     --sibling-->   egyptian_osiris

    egyptian_isis     --sibling-->   egyptian_nephthys
    egyptian_set      --sibling-->   egyptian_osiris   (reciprocal of the above)
    egyptian_set      --sibling-->   egyptian_isis     (reciprocal of the above)

    sweeps; materializing them now would be re-authoring outside scope.

    entry only as a contrastive DISAMBIGUATION ("Distinct from ... the slain seventh
    ancestor"), not asserted as kin of any batch figure; no kin edge to wire.
  - The other three of the four Anishinaabe winds (besides E-bangishimog the West

  - greek_memnon, the Greek/Canaanite/etc. interpretatio targets, and Astarte: all
    already wired (interpretatio/equated-with) or held as externalRef per the
    registry's deity-as-required-anchor-only principle; not kin to author.
