================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added three individuated, well-attested figures from the Talamancan (Bribri-Cabécar) creation cycle that individuate the existing set: Nãmãitãmĩ (the Tapir-woman, sister of Sibö and mother of the earth-girl Irìria), her mother Namásia / Makeur Siau (the underworld grandmother and counsellor), and Dukúr Bulú (the first great bat whose blood-drinking and revelation of the hidden path enable the earth's creation). Each is wired into the existing pantheon via parentage and directed relations to Iriria and Sulá, forming the maternal underworld lineage behind Iriria's transformation. All three are documented in Jara & García's 'Diccionario de mitología bribri' (2003), Constenla Umaña's 'Laca majifijca' (1993), Bozzoli de Wille (1979), and González Chaves & González Vásquez (1989). I deliberately excluded the omen-monsters born from Sórkura's dismembered body (wáyuk, Ùruksura, Àiáksura) as derivative portent-signs rather than individuated divine or ancestral beings.
