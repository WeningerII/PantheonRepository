Having killed Sórkura, Sibö ordered the earth into its present shape, brought the girl-child Iriria up from the underworld and made the fertile soil of her body, and planted the human clans as kernels of maize — the foundational myth of Bribri-Cabécar identity, in which descent groups are literally 'seed-stock' (ditsö̀).
