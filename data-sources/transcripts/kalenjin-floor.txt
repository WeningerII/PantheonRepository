================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added four genuinely individuated, named celestial deities from the Pokot (Suk) branch of the Kalenjin cluster, all documented in Mervyn Beech's 1911 ethnography "The Suk: Their Language and Folklore" (recorded from the elder Tiamolok) and reproduced in standard mythology dictionaries (Jordan 2004; Scheub 2000): the winged creator sky-god Tororut, his Pleiades-wife Seta, their firstborn son Arawa (the moon), and their firstborn daughter Topogh (the evening star). These are wired into the existing pantheon: Tororut is given as father of the already-registered Ilat/Ilet (the rain reckoned his son in the Suk account), and the new figures are cross-linked by spouse, parent, and sibling ties. I deliberately excluded the Kokel (stars) and the oyik (ancestral dead) as unindividuated spirit-classes, and the anonymous "Dorobo/Okiek" first ancestor of the Nandi origin myth as unnamed; the sun Asis, moon, and thunder are either already in the corpus or folded into these entries, so the individuated record beyond this set appears exhausted.
