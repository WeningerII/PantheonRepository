================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added six core, individually named Ifugao figures missing from the corpus: the chief god Maknongan, the Skyworld mediator Liddum, the Skyworld benefactor Ampual, the flood-ancestor and harvest pair Wigan and Bugan, and the bulul-origin deity Namtogan. Attestation rests on Barton (1946, 1955), Beyer (1913), Jocano (1969), Eugenio (1993), and Dulawan (2001). Kabigat was deliberately excluded to keep the Bontoc corpus distinct, and generic classes (bulul figures as objects, unnamed ancestors, the numerous minor named baki deities of Barton's thousand-strong lists that lack individuated narratives) were not authored. Wigan and Bugan carry reciprocal spouse and sibling relations per the deluge myth; Maknongan is wired to Bugan as consort per Jocano's creation account.
