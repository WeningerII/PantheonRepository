================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added two individuated Yapese figures beyond the existing sky-gods: Lug (Müller's Lugalang), the second great sky god who devours the "soul-that-dies," and Rumang, the toll-keeping gate-guard who judges the dead on the road to the afterworld and is wired to the existing chief god Yanolop. Both are drawn from the classic Yap ethnographies of Müller (1917) and Furness (1910), which document the Yapese pantheon and the soul's journey. I deliberately excluded Marespa and his soul-catching companion Mirrou (Ulithian apotheosis, a separate Carolinian tradition studied by Lessa) and the fisher-hero Motikitik (pan-Micronesian/Chuukese) as belonging to other corpus traditions rather than Yap proper. Attestation was constrained by loss of full-text access to Müller, Furness, and Lingenfelter during this session, so both figures are weighted "medium" and no dubious names were added to pad the set.
