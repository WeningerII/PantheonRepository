REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added five well-attested individuated Cheyenne figures: Aktunowihio the Wise One Below (Grinnell 1923; Mooney 1907; Dorsey 1905), the paired sacred couple Esceheman (Grandmother Earth) and Nonoma (Thunder) from Schlesier's Massaum reconstruction (1987) with Powell (1969) and the Cheyenne Dictionary (2006), their daughter Ehyophstah the Yellow-Haired Woman who brought the buffalo (Grinnell 1926; Schlesier 1987), and the star-born monster-slaying hero Falling Star (Grinnell 1926; Kroeber 1900). Heammawihio was excluded as an alias of the existing Maheo entry, and generic spirit classes (maiyun, listeners-above/below) were excluded as non-individuated. New figures are wired to the existing Maheo entry via directed subordinate-to relations and to each other via attested parentage.
