================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added two strongly individuated figures absent from the existing set: Eka Abasi, the primordial Great Mother ("Mother of God") who in Ibibio esoteric teaching is the true head of the pantheon, and her son-and-consort Obumo (Abasi Obumo), the thunder god regarded by ordinary worshippers as the chief deity. Both are richly documented in the two classic Ibibio ethnographies of P. Amaury Talbot (1923) and D. Amaury Talbot (1915), which explicitly describe their mother/spouse relationship and cosmogonic role; I wired Obumo to Eka Abasi via parentage and a reciprocal spousal relation. I deliberately excluded candidates that are merely aspects or epithets of already-registered figures (e.g. Abasi Enyong, the sky aspect of the supreme Abasi; Abasi Isong the earth goddess; and the generic ndem/eka-abasi child-spirit class), and treated the fertility "spirit mother" gloss as subsumed under Eka Abasi proper. Beyond these two, the attested Ibibio record is dominated by spirit-classes (ndem, ekpo) already covered or non-individuated, so I stopped at the two securely named divine individuals.
