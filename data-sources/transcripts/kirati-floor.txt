================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added five genuinely individuated figures beyond the existing set. From the Limbu Mundhum: Porokmi Yamphami, the demiurge who fashions humankind under Tagera Ningwaphuma (wired as subordinate-to her and creator-of the first human); Mujingna Kheyongna, that first living human and ancestress; and Nahangma, the warrior life-force goddess of the Nahangma rite. From the wider tradition I added Sirijunga, the deified culture-hero and script-giver venerated within Yumaism (tied to Yuma as her consecrated priest), and Hetchhakuppa, the youngest child of the existing Paruhang and Sumnima, founder of the Rai agricultural age (wired via parentIds to both). Each is grounded in at least two standard scholarly sources (Chemjong, Sagant, T.B. Subba, Gaenszle, Chaitanya Subba, J.R. Subba, Ramesh Kumar Limbu); Nahangma is placed in solitary because her only mythic relation is a symmetric antagonism with an existing figure, which the schema does not allow to be wired.
