EDITORIAL NOTE (post-authoring): the Sugidanon figure Matan-ayon was dropped and Nagmalitong Yawa de-parented to keep coherence with the existing Jocano-recension corpus (where Nagmalitong Yawa is an enchantress-wife, not a daughter, and "Buyong Matanayon" is a separate male swordsman). Kept: Tungkung Langit, Saragnayan, Nagmalitong Yawa.

SULUDNON (Sulod / Panay-Bukidnon, Western Visayas, Philippines) — CORE FIGURES
============================================================================

Scope and method
----------------
This batch authors the well-attested CORE absent from the corpus: (A) the supreme
creator sky-god of the Panay-Visayan creation myth, Tungkung Langit (husband of the
existing Alunsina); and (B) the principal Hinilawod / Sugidanon epic figures beyond
the protagonist family — the antagonist Saragnayan, the binukot enchantress
Nagmalitong Yawa, and Matan-ayon.

Two related but distinct bodies of ethnography underlie these figures and are kept
separate in the citations:

  1. The Sulod cosmology / Panay creation myth (F. Landa Jocano, "Sulod Society"
     1968; "Outline of Philippine Mythology" 1969). Here Tungkung Langit ("pillar
     of the skies") is the supreme male diwata of the upperworld (Ibabawnun) and
     ALUNSINA is the most powerful female diwata, goddess of the eastern skies and
     HIS WIFE. This is the frame that matches the existing `suludnon_alunsina`
     ("goddess of the eastern sky") and the requested Tungkung Langit ⇄ Alunsina
     spouse wiring.

  2. The Hinilawod epic (Jocano, collected 1955-57 from the binukot chanter
     Hugan-an; "Hinilawod: Adventures of Humadapnon"), and the later Sugidanon
     recension (Alicia P. Magos with the Federico Caballero family; Caballero-
     Padernal). Saragnayan, Nagmalitong Yawa and Matan-ayon are epic figures, so
     they are placed in the `heroic-age` era per the registry's era mapping.

Recension note carried into the data: in Jocano's Hinilawod the two named wives of
Labaw Donggon are Anggoy Ginbitinan (of Handug) and Abyang Doronoon (of Tarambang
Burok); MATAN-AYON as wife of Labaw Donggon and mother of Nagmalitong Yawa is the
genealogy of the SUGIDANON recension (Magos/Caballero), which is cited explicitly
for that figure and relation. Nagmalitong Yawa is wife of Saragnayan (Hinilawod,
Labaw Donggon cycle) and later wife of Humadapnon (Hinilawod, Humadapnon cycle);
both spousal links are wired.

OMITTED for lack of ethnographic attestation: **Burigadang Pada Sinaklang Bulawan**
(goddess of greed). She appears only in generic "Visayan deities" pop-myth
compilations (e.g. Mythlok; vizayanmyths.blogspot; FilipiKnow), which additionally
misidentify her as "wife of Humadapnon" (contradicting the securely attested
Nagmalitong Yawa). No Jocano / Magos / Caballero ethnographic source for her could
be verified, so she is omitted per the verification standard rather than authored
on pop-culture sourcing.
