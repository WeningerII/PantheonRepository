REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added five individuated Miwok figures verified against the two core primary ethnographies, C. Hart Merriam's The Dawn of the World (1910) and E. W. Gifford's Miwok Myths (UCPAAE 12(8), 1917), with support from Kroeber's Handbook (1925) and Gifford & Block (1930): Mol'-luk the Condor (father of the existing Wek'-wek the Falcon and son of Coyote-man, wired in via parentIds and a father-of relation), Ke'-lok the North Giant whose death caused the world fire, the Rock Giant Che-ha-lum'-che, the cannibal giant Yayali, and Oo-soo'-ma-te the Grizzly Bear woman. Other Merriam names (e.g., minor Hoi-ah'-ko tale characters, unnamed rock-giant classes, Ho-ha'-pe mermaids) were excluded as spirit classes or insufficiently individuated.
