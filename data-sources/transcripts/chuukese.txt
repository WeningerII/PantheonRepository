Chuukese (Trukese) pantheon — figure-authoring transcript

SCOPE AND METHOD
================
The registry already holds the attested male sky-genealogy of Chuuk: chuukese_anulap
(Anulap / Enúúnap, the uncreated high creator-spirit of magic and knowledge),
chuukese_lugeilan (Lugeilang / Lukeilang, "Middle of Heaven," his son and bringer of
the useful arts), and chuukese_olifat (Olifat / Iolofath, the trickster fire-bringer,
eldest son of Lugeilang). The task was to add any well-attested, individuated CORE
figures still missing, drawing on Laurentius Bollig, Die Bewohner der Truk-Inseln
(Anthropos-Bibliothek, 1927); Roger E. Mitchell's Micronesian folktale corpus
(Micronesian Folktales, Nagoya, 1973, and his Indiana folklore dissertation); Ward H.
Goodenough & Hiroshi Sugita, Trukese-English Dictionary (1980); Thomas Gladwin & Seymour
Sarason, Truk: Man in Paradise (1953); and William A. Lessa's overview of Micronesian
religion in the Encyclopedia of Religion, which synthesizes Bollig and the Carolinian
record. Chuukese cosmology is deliberately compact, so the goal was specific named gods,
not spirit-classes (the aniti / énú collectives, the deceased who possess mediums, the
reef- and nature-spirits) — those are NOT entered as figures.

AUTHORED (2 figures)
====================
1. chuukese_inemes — INEMES, the goddess of love, whom Bollig (1927: 6) called "the
   Venus of Chuuk." The Encyclopedia of Religion ("Micronesian Religions: Mythic
   Themes," Lessa) names her among the most popular and most-often-supplicated deities,
   "imprecated in the love magic that has survived even down to the present day." She is
   a genuinely individuated, named goddess with a distinct domain (love/sexual desire)
   and a living cult of love magic, so she is core. Her worship sits inside the wider
   Chuukese spirit-cult in which a medium (sou awarawar / wáátawa) was seated in the
   lineage meeting-house — over which hung a model double-hulled canoe that served as
   the vehicle for the spirit's descent — and called the spirit down; that ritual
   apparatus grounds her materialCulture. No parentage, spouse, or sibling is securely
   attested for her in the sources, so she is flagged SOLITARY.

2. chuukese_semenkoror — SEMENKOROR ("Father of Wisdom"; the candidate listed as
   "Semenkoroas"), a named god recorded by Bollig (1927: 6-7) and repeated in the
   Encyclopedia of Religion as one of "a number of other figures that might be called
   gods." He is individuated by name, the epithet "Father of Wisdom," and a domain
   (wisdom / esoteric knowledge), which in Chuuk is the prized sphere of itang lore and
   the knowledge a benign spirit reveals through a medium. The attested detail is thin
   (name + epithet + sphere; no narrative cycle), but he is a specifically named,
   individuated deity rather than a spirit-class, so he is entered with exactly the
   detail the sources support. No kin is attested → SOLITARY.

CONSIDERED AND DELIBERATELY SKIPPED (with reasons)
==================================================
- LUKELONG / LUKEILANG as a separate creator above/beside Anulap: in the CHUUKESE (Truk)
  recension the supreme being is Anulap (Enúúnap), and "Lukeilang" is simply the local
  form of Lugeilang, already authored as chuukese_lugeilan (Bollig 1927; Lessa). The
  distinct figure "Lukelong / Luk who made the sky" with the genealogy Luk > Lugeilang >
  Olofat belongs to the CENTRAL CAROLINE / Woleai recension (Hambruch; Frazer 1924;
  Dixon, Oceanic Mythology 1916), not to Truk. Authoring it under tradition "Chuukese"
  would be a tradition error, so it is omitted.
- The well-behaved BROTHER of Olifat: Dixon (Oceanic Mythology, 1916) reports only that
  the trickster "stands in marked contrast to his brother or brothers, who are
  beneficent" — UNNAMED in the Truk/Bollig material. The one named good brother of the
  trickster, Khiou (alongside Iolofath as a son of Lugeilang), is attested for ULITHI,
  in William A. Lessa's Tales from Ulithi Atoll (1961), a different tradition. Since no
  individuated NAMED beneficent brother is attested specifically for Chuuk, none is
  authored (inventing or importing a name would violate the no-fabrication rule).
- ONOFET: found only as a spelling variant of Olofat/Olifat (already chuukese_olifat);
  no independent figure. Skipped.
- SINENAP ("Skilled One") and AREMEI ("Achiever"): real named gods in Bollig's list
  (1927: 6-7), but attested ONLY as a bare name plus a one-word epithet, with no
  genealogy, narrative, powers, or objects on record. Entering them would force empty
  faculties/materialCulture against the enrichment mandate; they are documented here as
  attested-but-too-thin rather than padded into the registry.
- LIGOBUBFANU / Ligoupup / Nikowupwuupw (the creator goddess paired with Anulap in
  popularized retellings): the genuinely ethnographic anchor (Bollig; Lessa) treats
  Anulap as the high being and does not give this consort a stable individuated cult in
  the Truk record; the pairing is strongest in secondary/popular sources. Held back as
  not firmly enough attested as an individuated Chuukese figure to author at core.

NATIVE-TERM AND ETYMOLOGY NOTES
===============================
Chuukese is an oral language recorded in Latin orthography, so where a native word is
given, term.script = "Latin" and rom = value. Per the hard rules I supply a Chuukese
term only where a real attested word exists. "Inemes": the sources gloss the deity but do
not securely segment the theonym; the leading element is consistent with Chuukese iin /
ine "mother" (< Proto-Micronesian *ina, Proto-Oceanic/Proto-Malayo-Polynesian *ina
"mother"; Goodenough & Sugita 1980; Bender et al. 2003), a common formative in female
names, but I do NOT assert a full etymology that the sources do not give. "Semenkoror":
transparently semen- (the relational/honorific "father of," cf. Chuukese seme- "father")
+ a wisdom element; the gloss "Father of Wisdom" is Bollig's. I leave domain/faculty
term.value empty wherever a specific attested Chuukese word is not in hand.

SOLITARY: chuukese_inemes — the goddess of love Inemes is attested as an individuated, much-supplicated deity (Bollig, Die Bewohner der Truk-Inseln 1927, p. 6, 'the Venus of Chuuk'; Lessa, 'Micronesian Religions: Mythic Themes,' Encyclopedia of Religion) but the sources give her no parentage, spouse, or sibling — a solitary love-goddess defined by her domain and her love-magic cult.
SOLITARY: chuukese_semenkoror — Semenkoror, 'Father of Wisdom,' is named among the Chuukese gods (Bollig 1927, pp. 6-7; Lessa, Encyclopedia of Religion) with an epithet and a sphere but no attested kin, consort, or descent; flagged solitary for kinship.

RECIPROCAL-NEEDED: (none) — neither authored figure asserts a symmetric relation toward an existing corpus figure, so no reciprocal edge needs to be added to chuukese_anulap, chuukese_lugeilan, or chuukese_olifat.
