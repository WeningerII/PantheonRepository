NEW FIGURES (3):
  - ainu_shiramba_kamuy — Ainu kamuy of wood, grain and vegetation; named brother of the
    hunt-goddess Hash-inau-uk Kamuy (batch entity ainu_hash_inau_uk_kamuy) and, with her,
    a sibling of Kamuy Fuchi. (Ashkenazi, Handbook of Japanese Mythology, 2003.)
  - aboriginal_waa — Waang/Waa the Crow, the Crow moiety-ancestor of the Kulin nation,
    brother of the eaglehawk creator Bunjil (batch entity aboriginal_bunjil); the
    eaglehawk-and-crow moiety pair. (Howitt 1904; R. B. Smyth 1878; Blake 1991.)
  - ambundu_nzua_dia_kimanaueze — Nzua dia Kimanaueze, the mortal Ambundu culture-hero
    who married the Daughter of the Sun and Moon and fathered the thunder-twins
    Sudika-Mbambi and Kabundungulu (batch entity ambundu_kabundungulu). Currently
    referenced only by externalRef from three existing figures. (Chatelain 1894; Werner 1933.)
