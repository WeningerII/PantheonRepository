He completes the shaping of the world begun by Earth Doctor, creates or perfects human beings, and gives the O'odham their songs, ceremonies, and desert way of life, including the saguaro-wine rain ritual. In the great chronicle he is slain through the sorcery of Buzzard and the earlier people, but he revives, descends into the underworld, and brings up from below the ancestors of the present O'odham, who emerge to overcome the Hohokam. Elder Brother is believed to live still, withdrawn within a labyrinthine cave on Baboquivari Peak, the axis of the O'odham homeland; the emblem known as the Man in the Maze depicts him at the mouth of the winding path of life. Sources differ on his exact relation to Earth Doctor, some casting the two as independent creators and others as kin."
