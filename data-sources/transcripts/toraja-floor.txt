================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the seven remaining individuated deities of the Aluk To Dolo cosmology documented in the standard scholarship (Van der Veen 1940/1965/1966; Nooy-Palm 1979/1986; Encyclopedia of Religion 1987; Waterson 2009; Budiman 2013; Darius and Zaluchu 2023): the theogonic line Gaun Tikembong - Usuk Sangbamban - Simbolong Manik that produces the existing Puang Matua; Puang Matua's consort Arrang di Batu; the death-lord Pong Lalondong; the earthquake goddess Indo' Ongon-Ongon, wired as consort of the existing Pong Tulak Padang; and the maro healing goddess Indo' Belo Tumbang. The well-attested to manurun noble ancestors (Tamboro Langi', Lakipadada) were deliberately excluded because they belong to a later mythic age that neither registered era key can honestly accommodate. The remaining seven beings forged on Puang Matua's bellows alongside the existing Datu Laukku' (ancestors of cotton, rain/wind, iron, rice, fowl, buffalo, poison) were also excluded because their individual names vary irreconcilably across sources.
