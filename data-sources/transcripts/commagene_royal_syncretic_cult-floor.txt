REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added one genuinely individuated figure: Mithridates I Kallinikos (r. c. 109–70 BCE), Antiochos I's father and founder-king of independent Commagene, who initiated the dynasty's Graeco-Iranian cult reforms and was posthumously deified with his own hierothesion and cult-foundation inscription at Arsameia on the Nymphaios — a distinct individuated cult recipient documented by Waldmann (1973), Dörner & Goell's excavation report (1963), Versluys (2017), and Sullivan (1977), and wired into the pantheon as father-of the existing Antiochos I Theos. No further figures qualified: the four great syncretic gods, Commagene the Fatherland, and Antiochos himself are already in the corpus, while the extensive Nemrud Dağı ancestor galleries (the paternal Achaemenid line from Darius I and the maternal Seleucid line, including Laodice VII Thea) are collective genealogical honorands rather than individuated deities with their own sanctuaries, so authoring them would violate the no-vague-classes rule.
