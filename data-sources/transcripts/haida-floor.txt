================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added six well-attested, individuated Haida figures absent from the existing set: Power-of-the-Shining-Heavens (Sîns Sganagwai), the supreme sky-power; Sacred-One-Standing-and-Moving (Ḵuuya Gyaag̱andal), the earthquake-causing world-bearer; the hero Stone-Ribs, slayer of the five-finned killer whale Qagawai; Property Woman (Skîl Jáadei), a wealth-bringing spirit; Konankada, Chief of the Undersea World; and Nanasimgit, hero of the woman-carried-away-by-killer-whales narrative. Each rests on at least two real scholarly sources (Swanton 1905 Contributions and Skidegate Texts, Barbeau 1953, MacDonald 1983, Stewart 1979, Bringhurst 1999, and Boelscher Ignace 2005), and new figures are wired into the existing pantheon where attested: Power-of-the-Shining-Heavens as source of the Sg̱áana beings, the world-bearer as subordinate within that hierarchy, and Stone-Ribs as slayer of the killer-whale Qagawai. I deliberately excluded Dzelarhons/Volcano Woman (already present as haida_djilaqons) and the Wasgo/sea-wolf, which the sources treat as a creature-type rather than a single named individual.
