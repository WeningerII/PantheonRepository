Tradition: Bactrian (Kushan numismatic pantheon)
Author: figure-authoring researcher
Date: 2026-06-26

PURPOSE
Fills the upper-Oxus core of the Central Asia gap with the Kushan dynastic pantheon attested on the coins of Kanishka I and Huvishka and in the Rabatak inscription — Nana the dynastic sovereign, the sun- and moon-gods Miiro and Mao, the Shiva-like Oesho, the wind-god Oado, the Oxus river-god Oaxsho, the fire-god Atsho, and the hypostatized powers Pharro (royal Glory) and Ardoxsho (Abundance). Iranian/Indic forms carry equated-with links to their originals.
