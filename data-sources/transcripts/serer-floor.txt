Author pass: floor-raising expansion — attested individuated figures still missing (5 added).

================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the five individuated figures the scholarship documents beyond Roog and the Pangool collective: the paired lesser gods Takhar (justice/vengeance) and Tiurakh (wealth), attested since Bérenger-Féraud (1879) and discussed by Gravrand (1990); and three individually named pangool — Mindiss, protector of Fatick and its sea arm with the sanctuary Mbind Ngo Mindiss; and the 'milk' pangool Njemeh of Languème and Moussa Sarr — grounded in Gravrand's La civilisation sereer: Pangool (1990), Thiaw (1991) and Kalis (1997). Kopé Tiatie Cac/Kokh Kox/Koox were excluded as Cangin-language names of the existing supreme being Roog, and the generic pangool/blood-pangool classes and unnamed spirits were excluded as non-individuated. All new figures are wired to the existing serer_roog via subordinate-to relations.
