REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added three genuinely individuated, well-attested figures absent from the existing set. Fekulen (the heavenly maiden) and her divinely-born son Bukūri Yongšon are the core actors of the canonical Manchu/Qing origin myth preserved in the Manzhou Shilu and analyzed by Crossley, Elliott, and Lin Sun; I wired Bukūri to Fekulen via parentIds and a mother-of relation. Monggaldai Nakcu is the named underworld gate-warden and soul-fetcher of the Tale of the Nišan Shamaness, which I subordinated to the existing figure Ilmun Han per Nowak & Durrant and Durrant's Signs analysis. I deliberately excluded the two barely-individuated bathing sisters (Enggulen, Jenggulen), the mortal plot-figure Sergudai Fiyanggo, and the Chinese-derived Guan-mafa, since scholarship does not develop them as distinct divine personalities beyond the figures given here.
