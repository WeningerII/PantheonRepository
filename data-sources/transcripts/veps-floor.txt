REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added three figures verified against scholarship on Veps folk religion: the supreme sky and weather god Jumou (dialect forms juma, d'umal, jumou, g'umou; thunder g'umalanguru 'god's thunder'), and the two attested female mistresses (emäg) of water and forest, wired as consorts of the existing water and forest masters, since Veps host spirits are documented as families in which the ižand as a rule had a wife-emäg. Primary anchor is I. Yu. Vinokurova's encyclopedia Mifologiya vepsov (2015) plus her 1994 calendar-rites and 2006 animal-worldview monographs and Uno Holmberg's comparative studies. I deliberately excluded bathhouse, threshing-barn and field masters and the figure Sünd because I could not confirm individuated native terms or adequate citations from the sources reachable in this session; post-Christian saints (Ilya, Mikola) absorbed weather functions but are not Veps deities.
