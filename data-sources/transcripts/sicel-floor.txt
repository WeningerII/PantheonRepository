Added five genuinely individuated figures within the registered Sikel-Sicani era: the eponymous ancestor-king Sikelos (Hellanicus, Philistus and Antiochus apud Dionysius Hal. 1.22 and 1.12.3; Sammartano 1998), the Sican king Kokalos of Kamikos (Herodotus 7.170; Diodorus 4.78-79; Dunbabin, PBSR 16, 1948), the Sican battle-hero Leukaspis (Diodorus 4.23.5; Syracusan drachms c. 405-400 BCE; Caccamo Caltabiano, LIMC VI.1, 1992; Dunst, BCH 88, 1964), the deified river Chrysas of Assorus (Cicero, Verr. 2.4.96; Head, Historia Numorum2, p. 127), and the collective Mothers of Engyon (Diodorus 4.79.5-80.6; Plutarch, Marcellus 20; Cicero, Verr. 2.4.97; Sfameni Gasparro 1973), the last authored as a single collective cult figure on the model of the existing Palici entry. Kokalos is wired into the wider registry by the attested directed relation slayer-of toward the existing greek_minos. Deliberately excluded as not further individuated or out of scope: the bare-name Sican generals of Diodorus 4.23.5 (Pediacrates, Buphonas and others), the historical Sicel kings Hyblon (Thuc. 6.4.1) and Ducetius (historical actors without attested cult), the mainland eponyms Italos and Morges, and Aitne, already covered as an alias of the existing sicel_thaleia.
