================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added one genuinely individuated figure, Meilitars, the deified Toda man whom the ethnographic literature consistently pairs with the already-registered Kwoten as one of the two 'deified Todas'; he tricked the gods into eating buffalo-calf flesh and is credited with founding the ceremonial calf-sacrifice (Rivers 1906; Walker, Encyclopedia of World Cultures 1992 and The Toda of South India 1986). Anthony Walker's reconstructed high-god genealogy (Anthropos 113, 2018) names further beings, On's wife Ti·ko·ner, the sons Pyu·f and Töw-fax, and ten hill-god sons of Teikirzi such as Kora-töw, but these are almost entirely bare genealogical names, and Ti·ko·ner occupies the same consort-of-On slot the corpus already fills with Pinakurs; authoring them would risk padding with undifferentiated names or re-authoring an existing figure under a variant, so I refrained. Meilitars is left solitary because his mortal parentage lies outside the divine roster and no source ties him by a specific relation to an existing corpus deity.
