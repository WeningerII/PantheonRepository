REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added four individuated, named Jukun divinities missing from the corpus: Kenjo (household war deity), Akwa (ancestral protector deity), Yaku-Keji (tutelary "mother of all creation" at Wukari, linked to the existing Aku Uka), and Achu Nyande (rain god of the upper skies, linked to the existing sky god Chido). All are attested in C. K. Meek's foundational ethnography A Sudanese Kingdom (1931) and corroborated by recent scholarship (Elawa 2022 in Studies in World Christianity; Ukaogo and Tanko 2020). Beyond these, the literature records only unnamed ancestor spirits and cult-classes, which fall outside scope.
