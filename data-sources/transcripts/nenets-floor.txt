================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added five well-attested, individuated Nenets figures beyond the existing set: Ilebyam'pertya (guardian of reindeer and game, deified benefactor to whom Num delegated authority), Yavmal (deity of the southern sky, sea's edge and winds, servant of Num and forefather of water-spirits), Pe-mal Khada (the 'Old Woman of the mountain's edge,' goddess of the Polar Urals), and the divine couple of Vaygach Island — Vesako (the 'Old Man,' a many-headed male idol-deity at the south) and Hadako (the 'Old Woman' at the north). Ilebyam'pertya and Yavmal are wired to the existing Num as subordinate deities per the sources; Vesako and Hadako are joined as a reciprocal spouse pair; Pe-mal Khada stands alone as attested. Each rests on real scholarship (Kharyuchi's Sibirica 2018 pantheon survey, Golovnev and Osherenko's Siberian Survival 1999, Khomich's Nentsy 1995, Lehtisalo's Jurak mythology 1924, Vallikivi's JEF study of the Vaygach idols 2011, and the 1855 Veniamin account). I deliberately excluded the mythic wind-eagle Minlei (a named creature rather than an individuated cult figure with firm scholarly citation) and the pan-Siberian 'White Old Man' Ser-Ne Iriko (likely a borrowing), preferring correctness over quantity.
