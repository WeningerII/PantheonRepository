================================================================================
RESEARCH SUMMARY AND AUTHORING DECISION
================================================================================

The standard ethnographic genealogy of the Tswana traces the senior merafe (chiefdoms) to
the sons of the eponymous ancestor Malope. The chief scholarship is Isaac Schapera, *The
Ethnic Composition of Tswana Tribes* (LSE Monographs on Social Anthropology 11, London:
LSE / Athlone Press, 1952), with his tribal histories of the Bakgatla and Bakwena; and
Leonard D. Ngcongco, "Origins of the Tswana," *Pula: Botswana Journal of African Studies*
2(1), 1982. Schapera's *A Handbook of Tswana Law and Custom* (1938) and the corpus entry
for Malope (citing Ngcongco 1982 and Schapera 1952) both record the descent: "after his
death his children Mohurutshe, Kwena, Kgatla, Ngwato and Ngwaketse founded the Bahurutshe,
Bakwena, Bakgatla, Bangwato and Bangwaketse."

FIVE figures are authored here, the eponymous founders of the five tribes:
  • MOHURUTSHE — eponym of the Bahurutshe, conventionally the SENIOR Tswana tribe; totem
    the baboon (tshwene), whence the alternate clan-name Batshweneng. (A widely recorded
    variant tradition makes Mohurutshe a DAUGHTER and senior house, with Kwena the senior
    son; the corpus cluster, following Malope's own registry entry and the "five sons"
    founder-list, registers Mohurutshe among the founder-sons. The daughter-variant is
    flagged in this figure's notes for scholarly honesty; sex is given as male per the
    cluster's founder-son framing, with the variant disclosed.)
  • KWENA — eponym of the Bakwena ("people of the crocodile"); totem the crocodile (kwena),
    settled at Molepolole. The Bakwena are the parent stock from which the Bangwato,
    Bangwaketse and Batawana royal houses branch (they share the crocodile totem).
  • KGATLA (Mokgatla) — eponym of the Bakgatla; totem the monkey (kgabo); the senior
    Bakgatla seat in the corpus's frame at Mochudi (Bakgatla-ba-Kgafela).
  • NGWATO — eponym of the Bangwato (Bamangwato); totem the duiker (phuti, a small
    antelope); settled at Serowe. In the lost-cow tradition Ngwato and his followers split
    from Kwena and moved north.
  • NGWAKETSE — eponym of the Bangwaketse; totem the crocodile (kwena, the shared royal
    Bakwena totem, marking the common origin); settled at Kanye.

TYPE / TIER DECISION (anti-drift). All five are legendary human tribal-founder ancestors,
not deities; per the authoring rule, tribal founders are authored as MORTAL regardless of
the descent fraction. Their father Malope is himself a mortal in the corpus, and no divine
parent is involved, so the mortal tier is also what the descent rule yields. No second
parent (mother) is registered in the corpus — Malope has no recorded spouse, and
verified-solitary.json confirms none of the sons (nor a mother) are registry figures — so
each child is wired with parentIds = [sotho_tswana_malope] (parentRoles {father}) only, not
to a guessed mother.

LIFECYCLE. As with Malope himself (who carries no lifecycle block), these eponymous
founders have no attested death/transformation myth that changes their type-status; they
are recorded as deceased legendary ancestors without a lifecycle array.

WIRING.
  - parentIds: ["sotho_tswana_malope"]  (parentRoles {father}) for all five.
  - sibling → each of the other four founder-sons, reciprocated within this batch
    (the Oghuz-sons pattern: all five are brothers).
  No already-existing sibling of this cluster is in the corpus (the only Sotho-Tswana
  figures are sotho_tswana_badimo, _malope, _modimo, _senkatana), so there is no one-way
  sibling edge to an existing figure to add.
