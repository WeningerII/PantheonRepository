Attestation notes:
- Mastorpaz: son of the sun-god Chipaz and the grain-mother Norov-ava in the Erzya
  "egg" cosmogony; god of the underworld. (Folklore vol. 17, Tartu 2001; Harva FFC 142.)
- Varmanpaz: the male wind-god, son of Chipaz, in the same Erzya egg-cosmogony
  (Chipaz begot Nishkepaz, the earth-keeper Mastoron-kirdi and the wind-god Varmanpaz).
- Brynhildr: valkyrie / shield-maiden, daughter of Budli; mother of Aslaug (and Svanhild)
  by Sigurd. (Volsunga saga; Ragnars saga lodbrokar ch.1.)
- Ragnar Lodbrok: legendary Viking king, husband of Aslaug, father of her sons.
  (Ragnars saga lodbrokar; Ragnarssona thattr.)
- Ivarr the Boneless, Bjorn Ironside, Sigurd Snake-in-the-Eye: sons of Ragnar and Aslaug.
  (Ragnars saga lodbrokar ch.4-9; Ragnarssona thattr.)
