================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added five individuated figures well attested for Phrygia beyond the existing Cybele-Attis-Agdistis-Men-Sabazios core. Two extend the Mother-of-the-Gods myth as told by Pausanias and Arnobius: the river-god Sangarios and his daughter, the naiad Nana, who conceived Attis from the miraculous almond (wired to Attis as mother and to Sangarios as father). Marsyas is the Phrygian silenos and aulete of Celaenae, remembered as composer of the aulos 'Song of the Mother' for Cybele's rites, and I tied him to Cybele accordingly. The legendary kings Gordias and Midas form the Phrygian royal line (Midas's mythic name preserving the historical Mita/Mita of Mushki), wired father-to-son and to Cybele. I stopped at these five: further Phrygian names (e.g. Papas, Lityerses) are either bare epithets of existing gods or too thinly individuated to author responsibly.
