Legends tell that she offered to build a stone bridge to the Korean mainland if the islanders would sew her an enormous pair of drawers, but the cloth fell short and the bridge was never finished. Sources differ on her death: the most common account has her wade into a fathomless pond to measure its depth and vanish beneath the water.
