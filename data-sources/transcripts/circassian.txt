PANTHEON REGISTRY — FIGURE-AUTHORING TRANSCRIPT
Tradition: Circassian (Adyghe), Northwest Caucasus
Registered era vocabulary: mythic ; mythic / Nart age
Author batch: weningerii@gmail.com — 2026-06-23

PURPOSE
-------
This transcript supplies the well-attested CORE of Circassian (Adyghe) mythology
that was still missing from the Pantheon Registry, complementing the seven figures
already held: circassian_tha (Tha/Theshxwe, sky/supreme god), circassian_shible
(Shible, thunder), circassian_tlepsh (Tlepsh/Lhepsh, divine smith), circassian_mezitha
(Mezitha, forest/hunt), circassian_sozeresh (Sozeresh, fertility/hearth), circassian_setenaya
(Setenaya/Satanaya, Mother of the Narts), and circassian_sosruquo (Sosruquo, fire-bringer
born of stone, son of Setenaya).

The figures here are drawn from the standard scholarly and ethnographic corpus of
Circassian myth — chiefly John Colarusso, "Nart Sagas from the Caucasus: Myths and
Legends from the Circassians, Abazas, Abkhaz, and Ubykhs" (Princeton UP, 2002) and his
study "Prometheus among the Circassians"; Georges Dumézil, "Légendes sur les Nartes"
(1930) and "Le livre des héros" (1965); and the Adyghe-religion ethnography synthesized
in Amjad Jaimoukha, "The Circassians: A Handbook" (Curzon, 2001) and "The Circassian
Pantheon," with corroboration from the standard surveys of Circassian paganism. No
figure or citation here is invented; where a detail is a single-witness or interpretive
gloss it is flagged in the notes. Adyghe/Kabardian words are given only where they are
real, sourced forms (psa "soul/life," tha/tḥə "god"); where the exact Cyrillic
orthography of a divine name could not be source-verified for this batch, the term-object
is omitted per the registry's "real-word-only" rule and the romanized name is kept.

SCOPE NOTE — what was VERIFIED-IN and what was deliberately SKIPPED
------------------------------------------------------------------
The Circassian agrarian/pastoral pantheon distributes the "herd" function across TWO
distinct deities, contrary to a single "god of cattle/herds": Amish (Amisch) is the god
of SHEEP and shepherds, while Akhin (Ahin) is the patron of CATTLE. Both are authored,
each in its correct, separately-attested role. Thagaledj (god of flora and crops /
harvest) and Psathha (Psetha, god of life and souls) are likewise firmly attested and
functionally distinct from the existing Sozeresh, and are authored. Two well-known Nart
heroes treated as semi-divine in the cycle — Warzameg (the "Great Nart," husband of the
existing Setenaya) and Pataraz (Khimishuquo Pataraz, the avenger) — are authored as
demigods.

DELIBERATELY SKIPPED (to avoid padding):
 • "Zheguthe / Wered," a putative patron of singers. No deity of this name is attested
   in the sources; the song-and-dance specialist of Adyghe society is the human roving
   minstrel, the dzheguako / jegwak'we (a social role, not a god). The musical/pastoral
   "song" theme that does exist in the pantheon attaches to Amish and his reed-flute,
   which is captured under circassian_amish. No singing-deity figure is created.
 • "Lady Nart / Adiyukh." Adiyukh ("the luminous-/white-armed") is a Nart lady with a
   tale in Colarusso, but her tier (deity vs. mortal heroine) and her kin-wiring are not
   cleanly attestable from the sources available for this batch; per the don't-pad rule
   she is omitted rather than authored on thin evidence.

FIGURES AUTHORED (6)
--------------------
1. circassian_amish     — Amish / Amisch / Yemish, god of sheep and of fauna; the first
                          shepherd, patron of shepherds, protector of the flocks; player
                          of the magic reed-flute that turns barren ewes fertile.
2. circassian_akhin     — Akhin / Ahin, patron of cattle; honored in the rite of the
                          sacred "self-walking" sacrificial cow (esp. among the coastal
                          Shapsug). Distinct from Amish (sheep).
3. circassian_thagaledj — Thagaledj / Thageledj / Theghelej, god of flora, crops and the
                          harvest; "Lord of wonder" invoked over the plough to multiply
                          the harvest. Distinct from the existing Sozeresh.
4. circassian_psathha   — Psathha / Psetha / Pseta, the god of life and souls (psa); a
                          cosmogonic "god without image" governing the breath/soul of the
                          living and the souls of the ancestral dead.
5. circassian_warzameg  — Warzameg / Wseraseg / Worzameg, the "Great Nart," leader of the
                          Nart host and husband of the existing Setenaya; son of Lady
                          Meghazash. Demigod (Nart).
6. circassian_pataraz   — Pataraz / Bataraz (Khimishuquo Pataraz), the avenger-hero;
                          son of the Nart Khimish (slain by jealous Narts for marrying the
                          water-sprite Lady Isp), he swears vengeance in the womb and is
                          "vengeance incarnate." Demigod (Nart).

RELATION WIRING NOTES
---------------------
- Warzameg is the husband of the EXISTING circassian_setenaya: a symmetric "spouse" edge
  toward Setenaya requires a reciprocal on Setenaya (flagged RECIPROCAL-NEEDED). NOTE:
  Setenaya is Warzameg's WIFE, not his mother — Warzameg's mother is Lady Meghazash (not
  in corpus), so his parentIds are [].
- The "four great Narts" of the Circassian cycle are Sosruquo, Warzameg, Pataraz and
  Sos*/She Bardinuquo; Warzameg and Pataraz are companions of each other (reciprocal
  "companion" within this batch) and each is a companion of the EXISTING circassian_sosruquo
  (symmetric "companion" toward an existing figure → flagged RECIPROCAL-NEEDED for both).
- Pataraz is the son of Khimish and the water-sprite Lady Isp (neither in corpus); his
  parentIds are [] and the genealogy is recorded in notes.
- The four deities Amish, Akhin, Thagaledj and Psathha are functional cosmic/patron gods
  with no parentage, consort or offspring attested in the sources; each carries a SOLITARY
  flag with a cited reason. (Amish/sheep and Akhin/cattle share the livestock-protector
  sphere but no narrative kin-bond between them is attested, so no symmetric relation is
  fabricated.)

- circassian_amish — Amish (god of sheep / first shepherd) is attested only as a functional pastoral deity, with no parentage, consort, or offspring named in the sources (Jaimoukha, The Circassians, 2001; Colarusso, 'Music of the Gods: Amisch and His Magic Flute'). He shares the livestock-protector sphere with the cattle-god Akhin, but no narrative kin-bond between them is attested, so no relation is fabricated.
- circassian_akhin — Akhin (patron of cattle) is attested only as a functional herding deity centred on the self-walking-cow sacrifice, with no kin named in the sources (standard survey of Circassian paganism / Religion in Circassia; Shapsug ethnography).
- circassian_thagaledj — Thagaledj (god of flora, crops and harvest) is attested only as a functional agrarian deity invoked in harvest-prayer, with no parentage, consort, or offspring named (standard survey of Circassian paganism; Jaimoukha, The Circassians, 2001).
- circassian_psathha — Psathha/Psetha (god of life and souls) is a cosmogonic 'god without image' with no kin attested in the sources (standard survey of Circassian paganism / Religion in Circassia).
