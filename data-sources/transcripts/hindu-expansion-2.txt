================================================================================
WHAT LANE 16 LEFT, AND WHY THIS IS THE NEXT PASS
================================================================================

Lane 16 took the conflicts — the Kauravas, the four adversaries the gods are
named after, the missing half of the Ramayana, and the lunar dynasty that makes
the two great houses kin. Its audit named what should come next, and this is it.

**THE NAVAGRAHA. The corpus held none of the nine as grahas.**

The nine planetary deities are the most widely worshipped set of figures in
living Hindu practice — a Navagraha shrine stands in most substantial temples in
southern India, and the whole apparatus of Vedic astrology is addressed to them.
The corpus held Surya and Soma for other reasons, and lane 16 added Shukra for
his part in two stories. It held **Mangala, Budha, Brihaspati, Shani, Rahu and
Ketu** not at all.

Rahu and Ketu are the sharpest case. They are one being cut in half: the asura
Svarbhanu drank the nectar in disguise, Vishnu took his head off before it could
pass his throat, and because the nectar had already reached him both halves are
immortal — the head is Rahu and the body is Ketu, and the eclipse is the head
swallowing the sun. The corpus held the sun, the moon and the discus and neither
half of the thing that eats them.

**THE CHURNING OF THE OCEAN.** Rahu's story is an episode of it, and the corpus
held no part of the churning either: not **Kurma**, the tortoise avatar who holds
the mountain up; not **Vasuki**, the serpent used as the rope; not
**Dhanvantari**, who comes out of the sea holding the pot; not **Mohini**, the
form Vishnu takes to get the nectar back. Lane 16 blocked Kurma for want of a
treatment; this pass has one.

**THE HOUSEHOLDS OF THE SKY.** The corpus held Surya and did not hold either of
his wives — **Sanjna**, who could not bear his heat, or **Chhaya**, the shadow
she left in her place, who is Shani's mother. It held Soma and not **Tara**,
whose abduction by him starts a war among the gods and whose son is Budha. Lane
16 named this as its own pattern: the corpus holds the god and not the household.

**THE DAKSHA CYCLE.** The corpus holds Shiva and Parvati and held nothing of what
connects them: **Daksha**, who holds a sacrifice and does not invite his
daughter's husband; **Sati**, who burns herself in the fire of it; and
**Virabhadra**, whom Shiva tears out of his own hair to go and behead him.

**THE SOLAR DYNASTY.** The corpus held the whole lunar line after lane 16 and
nothing of the solar one — so it held Rama and could not say that he is an
Ikshvaku. **Ikshvaku** himself, **Sagara**, **Bhagiratha** who brings the Ganges
down, **Harishchandra**, and **Kapila**, whose glance burns Sagara's sons to ash.

**SEVEN MORE VEDIC GODS**, in a corpus that holds Indra, Agni, Varuna, Vayu,
Surya and Ushas: the Adityas **Mitra**, **Aryaman**, **Bhaga** and **Amsha**,
plus **Pushan**, **Tvashtr**, **Dyaus Pita** and **Parjanya**.

And **two more of the Kurukshetra principals** whose treatments this pass could
retrieve: **Shalya**, who drives Karna's chariot while praising his enemy, and
**Sanjaya**, whose narration to the blind king IS the Bhagavad Gita.

================================================================================
WHAT THIS PASS STILL DOES NOT TAKE
================================================================================

Virata, Satyaki, Yuyutsu and Ekalavya stay BLOCKED: the searches that returned
Shalya and Sanjaya returned these four as names without treatments, and lane 16
already established that a record built from a list entry is a name with nothing
behind it. Parashurama and Kalki likewise. The Devi Mahatmya's other asuras
likewise. They are census rows here, as they were there.

================================================================================
ONE THING THIS PASS DELIBERATELY DOES NOT RESOLVE
================================================================================

Lane 16 escalated an era-vocabulary inconsistency in this tradition: the corpus
files Dhritarashtra at `epic-period` and his nephews the Pandavas at
`dvapara-yuga`, which is earlier. This pass meets it again from the other end.
`hindu_manu` is filed at `vedic`, and **Ikshvaku is his son** — so the founder of
the solar dynasty must be filed at `vedic` or later, while his descendant Rama
sits at `heroic-age`, which is earlier. The same inversion, in the same
vocabulary, reached by a different route.

This pass files Ikshvaku and his line at `vedic` because that is what keeps the
parent-before-child invariant the suite enforces, and it does not mutate Manu or
Rama to fix the underlying order. **Two lanes have now hit this from opposite
directions**, which raises it from an oddity to a thing the owner should decide.

NOTE ON THE ONE SYMMETRIC RELATION THIS PASS COULD NOT RECIPROCATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Brihaspati and Shukracharya are arch-nemeses — the guru of the gods and the guru
of their enemies — and that is symmetric in fact. Shukra was added by lane 16,
one commit ago, and is now a pre-existing record; a symmetric `rival` edge toward
him would be one-way, which the validator reports as RECIPROCAL-NEEDED, and
reciprocating it would mean editing a transcript from a batch already merged and
audited as "zero pre-existing records mutated".

The edge therefore uses `arch-nemesis-of`, which is the treatment's own phrase,
is non-symmetric, carries the full force of the claim and leaves no warning
behind. The fact that the underlying relation is mutual is recorded in the edge's
own notes, on the record, where a reader meets it.

This is worth naming as a general finding rather than a one-off. **The "never
mutate a pre-existing record" rule has a cost, and this is the shape of it:**
each lane's additions become the next lane's immovable objects, so genuinely
mutual relations discovered later can only be recorded one-way. Six lanes have
paid that cost silently by choosing directional kinds. It is cheap while the
directional kind is also the truer one — as it was for every edge in lanes 13
through 16 — and it starts to bite when it is not.
