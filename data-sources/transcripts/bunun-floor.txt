================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the four individuated actors of the Bunun deluge cycle — the river-damming giant serpent, the giant crab that slew it, the fire-bringing kaipis/haipis bird (the only one bearing a recorded Bunun name), and the fire-fetching toad — all attested in Ogawa & Asai's 1935 Bunun text collection, Ho Ting-jui's 1971 comparative study, Lu Yi-lu's 2002 flood-myth monograph, and Gauss (2020). Beyond this cycle, ethnographies of Bunun religion (e.g., Huang Ying-kuei's work and Yang Shu-yuan's studies) consistently describe a system of dihanin plus the hanitu spirit-class with no further personalized, named deities, so no additional god-figures qualified. The "Ina and Ino" supreme deities circulating on popular websites have no scholarly attestation and were deliberately excluded as fabrications.
