================================================================================
RESEARCH SUMMARY AND AUTHORING DECISION
================================================================================

The canonical roster of Erechtheus's children is Pseudo-Apollodorus, *Bibliotheca*
3.15.1, where Erechtheus by Praxithea (daughter of Phrasimus and Diogeneia) begets the
sons Cecrops, Pandorus and Metion and the daughters Procris, Creusa, Chthonia and
Oreithyia. Apollodorus there gives the daughters' marriages: Procris wed Cephalus son of
Deion(eus), Creusa wed Xuthus, Chthonia wed Butes. Four of these children are still
absent from the corpus and are authored here — Procris, Cecrops II, Pandorus and Metion —
each wired to the cluster parent greek_erechtheus and to the second parent greek_praxithea
(confirmed present in the corpus by grep, id greek_praxithea), with reciprocal sibling
edges among the four and one-way sibling edges to the two already-registered sisters
(greek_oreithyia, greek_creusa_athens).

Per-figure attestation:
  • PROCRIS (Πρόκρις) — third daughter of Erechtheus, wife of Cephalus son of Deioneus.
    Her tragic story is told at length in Apollodorus 3.15.1 (the quarrel; her flight to
    Minos in Crete, who gives her the unerring javelin and the hound Laelaps; her death
    when Cephalus, hunting, mistakes her in the thicket for game and kills her with the
    never-missing spear) and Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 7.690–865; also Hyginus, *Fabulae* 189,
    and Antoninus Liberalis 41. DIES — lifecycle authored. type mortal.
  • CECROPS II (Κέκροψ) — eldest son of Erechtheus; succeeded (in the Apollodoran/
    Pausanian dynastic scheme) to the Athenian kingship and was father of Pandion II.
    Apollodorus 3.15.1, 3.15.5; Pausanias 1.5.3 distinguishes this later Cecrops, "son of
    Erechtheus, son of Pandion, son of Erichthonius," from the first earth-born Cecrops,
    and 7.1.2 notes the Ionian-Euboean connection of the Erechtheid sons. DIES (mortal
    king) — lifecycle authored. type mortal.
  • PANDORUS (Πάνδωρος) — son of Erechtheus who, leaving Attica, founded Chalcis in
    Euboea. Apollodorus 3.15.1; Pseudo-Scymnus, *Periodos / Circuit of the Earth* 572–574
    ("Pandorus the son of Erechtheus crossed over from Athens and founded Chalcis, the
    greatest city on it [Euboea]"); Smith, *Dictionary of Greek and Roman Biography and
    Mythology* s.v. "Pandorus." Colony-founder hero. DIES — lifecycle authored. type mortal.
  • METION (Μητίων) — son of Erechtheus, ancestor of the Metionidae. His sons (the
    Metionidae — Eupalamus, Sicyon, and others) expelled their cousin Pandion II from
    Athens (Apollodorus 3.15.5; Pausanias 1.5.3); through his son Eupalamus he is the
    grandfather of the master-craftsman Daedalus (Apollodorus 3.15.8, "Daedalus … son of
    Eupalamus, son of Metion"; Diodorus Siculus 4.76.1; Pausanias 7.4.5, 9.3.2). In a
    Sicyonian variant (Pausanias 2.6.5) Metion's line is tied to Sicyon. DIES — lifecycle
    authored. type mortal.

TYPE BY DESCENT (anti-drift rule). The cluster parent Erechtheus is mortal (divinity
fraction 0) and the second parent Praxithea is registered as mortal (0); a child's
fraction = (0 + 0)/2 = 0 → mortal. Independently, the rule mandates authoring tribal/
royal founders as "mortal" regardless. All four children are therefore type = mortal,
vitalStatus = dead (the Greek-cluster convention used by greek_erechtheus and
greek_praxithea), schemaVersion 2 (the convention of the surrounding Greek figures).

WIRING.
  - parentIds: ["greek_erechtheus", "greek_praxithea"] for all four (both parents present
    in the corpus); parentRoles {greek_erechtheus:"father", greek_praxithea:"mother"}.
  - sibling (reciprocal, in-batch): the four children — Procris, Cecrops II, Pandorus,
    Metion — are wired as mutual siblings.
  - sibling (one-way, to existing figures): each of the four carries a one-way sibling edge
    to greek_oreithyia and to greek_creusa_athens (the already-registered sisters); the
    reciprocals belong on those existing figures.
  - additional attested relations: Procris ↔ Cephalus is recorded as a spouse edge with
    personId "greek_cephalus" — NOTE this figure is NOT yet in the corpus (grep returned
    only greek_apollod_thespiad_procris, a different Procris, and no greek_cephalus); the
    spouse edge is therefore documented in NOTES and NOT emitted as a relation, to satisfy
    the hard rule that relation.personId must resolve to an existing/in-batch figure.
    (Cephalus, Daedalus, Eupalamus, Pandion II, Xuthus, Butes, Chthonia are named in prose
    where relevant but not wired, as they are absent from the corpus and outside this batch.)

NOT AUTHORED (already exist — flagged for wiring, not re-authored):
  - greek_oreithyia, greek_creusa_athens (existing daughters of Erechtheus; reciprocal
    sibling edges to the four new children should be added on these existing figures).
