================================================================================
THE GAP
================================================================================

A corpus-wide name check, over canonical names and aliases with Unicode
normalisation, found the following absent from the WHOLE registry:

- **Ninurta**, the warrior god of Lugal-e, and **Ashur**, head of the Assyrian
  pantheon. The corpus carried Assyrian kings NAMED after them — Ninurta-apal-Ekur,
  Ninurta-tukulti-Ashur, Ashur-dan I — but not the gods.
- **Ninlil**, wife of Enlil; **Damkina**, wife of Enki; **Ningal**, wife of
  Nanna. The three most important consorts in the pantheon.
- **Anshar, Kishar and Lahamu**, the primordial pairs of Enuma Elish. Lahmu was
  present without Lahamu, which is a pair split in half.
- **Enkidu.**
- **Anzu, Pazuzu, Lamashtu, Humbaba, Asag** — the whole monstrous register.
- **Gula, Nisaba, Nanshe, Ninkasi, Uttu, Ninshubur, Namtar, Nusku, Ishum,
  Ninazu, Ningishzida, Ishtaran, Ningirsu, Zababa, Nammu, Enmesharra.**

================================================================================
ONE IDENTITY DECISION UP FRONT — THE FLOOD HERO
================================================================================

Utnapishtim, Atra-hasis and Ziusudra are **one figure under three names** in
three languages: the reference literature states plainly that the Sumerian
Ziusudra becomes Atra-hasis in Akkadian and Utanapishtim in the Epic of
Gilgamesh, the names meaning respectively "he of long life" and "he who found
life". Creating three records would be exactly the duplicate-transliteration
error this programme forbids. **One record** is authored, primary name
Utnapishtim, with Atra-hasis, Ziusudra and Xisouthros as aliases and the
three-version history stated in `variants[]`. Atra-hasis and Ziusudra are
dispositioned ALIAS in the census.

================================================================================
AUTHORED FIGURES — III. THE EPIC CYCLE AND THE MONSTROUS REGISTER
================================================================================

The Epic of Gilgamesh was represented in this registry by Gilgamesh, Ninsun and
Lugalbanda alone. Enkidu — half the epic — was absent, and so were Humbaba,
Siduri, Shamhat, Utnapishtim and the Bull of Heaven. The demons were absent
wholesale: Anzu, Asag, Pazuzu, Lamashtu. Adapa and Etana, the two other great
Akkadian legends of a mortal reaching for something the gods keep, were absent
as well.

Relations toward Gilgamesh use non-symmetric kinds throughout. His record is
part of the seed core, which the generator does not rebuild, so a symmetric
relation pointed at him could not be reciprocated and would leave the corpus
one-legged.
