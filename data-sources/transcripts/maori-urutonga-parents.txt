PANTHEON REGISTRY — FIGURE-AUTHORING TRANSCRIPT
Tradition: Māori
Era (registered, exact): "mythic-prehistoric"
Author pass: genealogy completion — the parents of Urutonga (wife of Hemā, mother of Tāwhaki and Karihi).

================================================================================
RESEARCH SUMMARY AND AUTHORING DECISION
================================================================================

Urutonga (polynesian_urutonga) is the wife of Hemā and mother of Tāwhaki and
Karihi in the Tāwhaki cycle (Grey, Polynesian Mythology, 1855). The modern
reference tradition names her parents as UA (father) and KAHILINAI (mother);
both are known only genealogically and play no independent narrative role in
the recorded myths.

Both figures are therefore authored as MORTAL genealogical ancestors (the
sources record no divine role for either), and the parentage is recorded via
directed father-of / mother-of relations on the parent side. Urutonga's own
entry is deliberately left untouched: writing the pair into her lineage as
formal parents would mechanically re-derive the divine descent of the whole
canonical Tāwhaki line, and that kind of re-derivation is reserved for a
human-reviewed pass, following the registry's existing practice for the line
of Tros of Troy.

Phonological note kept in the figure notes: the form "Kahilinai" contains /l/,
which is absent from the Māori phoneme inventory — the name evidently entered
the record from a cognate (likely Hawaiian) genealogical source, as with other
figures of the pan-Polynesian Hemā/Tāwhaki cycle.
