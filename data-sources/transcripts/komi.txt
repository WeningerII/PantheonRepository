Figure-authoring batch — the Komi (Zyrian) nature-spirit core.

The corpus held only the dualist creation pair (komi_jen, the good demiurge En/Jen;
komi_omol, the adversary Omöl/Kul') and the north-wind god (komi_voipel). This batch
adds the well-attested "owner"/master spirits and individuated figures that fill out
the middle and lower world of the Komi-Zyrian cosmos: VÖRSA (Вöрса), the master of
the forest; VASA (Васа), the master of the water; YOMA (Ёма), the cannibal forest /
swamp witch of Komi folktale; and ZARNI AN (Зарни ань), the "Golden Woman," the
Permian goddess-idol of chronicle fame. Nature-classes that lack a clearly
individuated named figure are deliberately NOT entered: the olys'/olysya hearth-spirit
(a domovoi-type household genius), the field-spirits, and the undifferentiated kul'
water-demons are described in the sources only as classes, so they are skipped to
avoid padding. The epic strongman Pera-bogatyr is omitted: he is firmly attested but
overwhelmingly as a Komi-PERMYAK legendary hero (epic, not the Zyrian mythological
core), and the cosmological/solar elements sometimes attached to him are not securely
attested for the Zyrian branch.

Sources are the standard reference literature on Permian/Zyrian religion, verified to
exist as real works: Uno Holmberg (Harva), "Finno-Ugric [and] Siberian Mythology"
(The Mythology of All Races, Vol. IV, Boston 1927), and his earlier "Permalaisten
uskonto" (Helsinki 1914); N. D. Konakov et al., "Komi Mythology" (Encyclopaedia of
Uralic Mythologies, Vol. I; eds. Siikala, Napolskikh & Hoppál; Akadémiai Kiadó & SKS,
Budapest–Helsinki 2003); and Károly Rédei, "Zyrian Folklore Texts" (Akadémiai Kiadó,
Budapest 1978) for the folktale corpus (the Yoma tales). Tertiary syntheses (the Komi
mythology survey on Wikipedia / Mythosphere / Encyclopedia.com, drawing on these works)
are cited where they carry the specific wording.

The Komi cosmos is a Jen–Omöl dualism with a three-tier structure (luminous upper
world, the earthly middle world of humans and forests, the shadowy underworld); the
nature-masters police hunting and fishing morality. Vasa, the water-master, belongs to
the malevolent water sphere of Omöl/Kul' (Holmberg: he "could be malicious, like Kul'";
the kul' water-demons are Omöl's spawn), so he is wired as an ally of the existing
komi_omol, with a RECIPROCAL-NEEDED flag. The forest-master Vörsa, the witch Yoma, and
the Golden Woman Zarni An have no individuated kin in the sources and are flagged
SOLITARY with cited reasons.

Native Komi (kpv) orthography is given in Cyrillic with romanization. Etymologies:
vör 'forest' + -sa 'inhabitant/of' (Вöр-са, "the forest one"); va 'water' + -sa
(Ва-са, "the water one"); zarni 'gold' + ań 'woman/mother' (Зарни ань, "Golden Woman").
