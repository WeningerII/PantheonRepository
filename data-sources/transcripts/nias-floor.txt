================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added two genuinely individuated, well-attested figures missing from the corpus. Sirao (Sirao Uwu Zihönö) is the keystone divine progenitor-king of the golden heaven Teteholi Ana'a and father of the nine ancestral sons; I wired him into the pantheon as father-of the existing Hia (named in Suzuki as Sirao's son) and father-of Lowalangi (the son Luo Mewöna who kept the upper world), per Suzuki 1959, Loeb 1935, Schröder 1917, and Beatty 1992. Buruti Siraso, the heavenly Seed-Maiden (Putri Bibit) who brought cultivated seed down to Tanö Niha, is the Nias agricultural culture-heroine documented in Mendröfa's Fondrakö Ono Niha (1981) and Suzuki 1959; because her attested parent (Balugu Silaride Ana'a) and twin (Silogu Mbana) are not in the corpus and she has no fixed genealogical tie to the existing figures, she is listed as solitary. I deliberately withheld the primeval tree-born couple (Tuhamöra Ana'a / Buruti Rao Ana'a), the god Sihai, and the individual clan-eponym sons (Gozo, Daeli, etc.), whose names are either spelling-unstable, thinly cited, or generic clan-founder ancestors rather than robustly individuated deities.
