================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added three further individuated cosmic deities that the standard Mithraic scholarship (Clauss 2000, Beck 2006, Vermaseren's CIMRM, Cumont 1903, Merkelbach 1984) documents as named presences on the monuments beyond the existing core five: Luna, the moon-goddess set opposite Sol at the top of the tauroctony and descending in her chariot; Oceanus, the reclining primordial-waters god of the marginal and rock-birth scenes; and Caelus, the personified vault of heaven bending over the zodiac-ringed celestial sphere. Each is wired into the pantheon as subordinate to Mithras, the cult's cosmocrator, matching how the existing torchbearers are placed. I deliberately did NOT author a separate Saturn/Kronos figure, since the reclining time-and-power elder overlaps the already-registered leontocephaline Arimanius, whose alternate names explicitly include Aion and note the Kronos-Saturn identification; likewise I excluded non-individuated classes (the seven planetary grade-gods, the four winds and seasons, the unnamed primal bull) as out of scope. The attested individuated record beyond these additions appears exhausted.
