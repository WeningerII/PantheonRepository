REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added six individuated, well-attested figures from the recorded Fulɓe initiatory corpus of Amadou Hampâté Bâ and Germaine Dieterlen: Njeddo Dewal (the sorceress 'mother of calamity') and her nemesis Bâgoumâwel (the wonder-child) from the Njeddo Dewal tale; Hammadi, the wisdom-winning initiate of Kaïdara; and, from the Koumen cosmogony, Silê Sadio (the first silatigi/master-initiate), Ilo son of Yaladi (the first herdsman and brother of Tyanaba), and Ndurbeele (the primordial hermaphrodite cow). Each is wired into the existing pantheon by attested directed relations — Njeddo Dewal and Ndurbeele to Guéno, Silê Sadio to Koumen and Foroforondou, Hammadi to Kaïdara, Ilo to Tyanaba — with the Njeddo Dewal/Bâgoumâwel enmity reciprocated between the two new figures. I deliberately excluded non-individuated or minor elements (Njeddo Dewal's seven unnamed daughters, a spirit-class; and the greed-blinded foils Hamtoudo and Dembourou), since the task calls for named divine, ancestral or heroic beings rather than literary bit-players.
