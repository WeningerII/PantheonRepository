REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the well-attested individuated core of the Bön pantheon beyond Tönpa Shenrab: the other three of the Four Transcendent Lords (Satrig Ersang 'the Mother', Shenlha Ökar 'the God', Sangpo Bumtri 'the Procreator'), the chief protectress Sipé Gyalmo, and the Zhang-zhung tutelary deity Gekhö. Attestation rests principally on Per Kværne, The Bon Religion of Tibet (1995), Samten Karmay's Treasury of Good Sayings (1972) and The Arrow and the Spindle (1998), Snellgrove's Nine Ways of Bon (1967), and Bellezza's Zhang Zhung (2008); web verification confirmed the tetrad structure and Sipé Gyalmo's identity as the wrathful form of the wisdom mother. Shenlha Ökar is wired to the existing Tönpa Shenrab entry via a creator-of (emanation-source) relation; Nyenchen Tanglha and Yul-lha under the sibling 'Tibetan Bön' label were not duplicated.
