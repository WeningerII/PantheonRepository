Tradition: Lardil
Era (registered, exact): "Lardil traditional religion; continuing"
Author pass: floor-raising expansion — attested individuated figures still missing (3 added).

================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added three genuinely individuated, well-attested Lardil ancestral beings absent from the existing set: Nyaranbi the Dingo ancestor, named repeatedly alongside Thuwathu as a co-founder of Lardil law, totems and kinship (McKnight 1999; Memmott 1979); Gidegal the Moon Man, who presided over the first initiation ceremony and guards the passage to the afterlife (Roughsey 1971; McKnight 1999); and Kaltharr the Yellow Trevally ancestor, credited with creating the celebrated Damin ceremonial language taught at the second (warama) degree of initiation (Hale & Nash 1997; Fleming 2017). Each stands alone in the record because Lardil sources present these Dreaming ancestors as co-equal totemic figures without attested genealogy, so I placed them in solitary rather than fabricate kin links; they connect thematically to the existing pantheon (especially Thuwathu) but no directed genealogical relation is documented. I deliberately excluded generic totem-classes (shark, seagull, barramundi Dreamings, and the unnamed first-initiate sign register) that lack an individuated named protagonist, so the attested individuated record beyond these additions appears effectively exhausted.
