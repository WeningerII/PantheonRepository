================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added seven individuated Hattic-substrate deities verified against current Hittitological scholarship (Taracha 2009; Haas 1994; Klinger 1996; Soysal 2004, plus figure-specific studies by Starke 1979, Archi 2013, Torri 2019 and Pecchioli Daddi 1987/2014): the throne-goddess Hanwasuit/Halmasuit, Telipinu's bride Hatepuna (recorded as his consort), Katahha the 'Queen' of Ankuwa, the paired fate-spinners Istustaya and Papaya, the divine smith Hasamili, and the wild-nature goddess Teteshapi. The other obvious Hattic candidates were deliberately not authored because the corpus already covers them elsewhere: Taru, Inar, Mezulla, Zintuhi, Wurusemu and Hannahanna exist in or as aliases of Hittite-tradition figures (hittite_tarhunna, hittite_inara, hittite_mezzulla, hittite_zintuhi, hittite_arinna, hittite_hannahanna), while Kattahzipuri is an alias of luwian_kamrusepa and Sulinkatte an alias of hattic_wurunkatte. A few genuinely attested but more weakly individuated local figures (Zashapuna of Kastama, the mountain-god Zaliyanu, the hearth-god Zilipuri, Kammamma, Tahattanuiti) were left for a possible future pass rather than padded in with thin documentation.
