================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added one genuinely individuated, missing figure: Owo, the God-given personal guardian spirit / soul (also associated with destiny and with rain) that scholarship consistently names as a distinct element of Idoma cosmology alongside the already-registered Owoicho, Aje, Alekwu, Anjenu, and Egli-Enyanwu. It is wired to the pantheon as subordinate to Owoicho, who apportions it and to whom it returns at death. I deliberately excluded "Ejembi," which appears in Wikipedia-derived popular listings as "the guardian spirit" but which I could not corroborate in any independent scholarly (book or journal) source and which appears to duplicate the Owo guardian-spirit concept; per the correctness-over-quantity standard I would rather omit it than pad with an uncorroborated name. Sources consulted include Armstrong (1955), Anizoba & Johnson (2021, Filosofia Theoretica), Allam (2018, GNOSI), and Kasfir on Anjenu; these confirm the tripartite Owoicho–Aje–Alekwu framework plus Anjenu, Egli-Enyanwu, and Owo as the attested named divine beings, so beyond Owo the individuated record for this tradition appears essentially exhausted.
