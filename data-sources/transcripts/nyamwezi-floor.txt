================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Beyond the four attested names of the one High God already in the corpus, the ethnographic record yields exactly two further individuated beings, both added here: Lyangombe, remembered in Unyamwezi as founder of the uswezi (Buswezi) possession cult and identical with the interlacustrine hero-spirit Ryangombe (Nyanto, History Research 6(2), 2016; Cory, American Anthropologist 57(5), 1955; Cohen, Journal of African History 9(4), 1968, on the Ryangombe-Cwezi cult carried from Unyamwezi to Katanga by the Yeke), and Limudimi, the named 'guardian of the bushes' of Unyamwezi cosmology (Nyanto 2015 and 2016). I excluded as non-individuated classes the swezi/maswezi possession spirits, the migabo/migawo spirits and the ancestral masamva/mizimu; excluded Katabi/Katavi, whom Nyanto and the regional literature attach to Ukimbu, Ukonongo and the Bende-Pimbwe country rather than to the Nyamwezi proper; and excluded Mulungu, Kube and Likubala, which Scheub (A Dictionary of African Mythology, 2000) records as further titles of the already-authored High God, barred as aliases. Additional sources checked (Bösch 1930; Abrahams 1967 and 1981; Tcherkézoff 1985 and 1987) document no further named, individuated Nyamwezi figures. Note that Limudimi's attestation rests on Nyanto's two publications alone, which the figure's notes acknowledge.
