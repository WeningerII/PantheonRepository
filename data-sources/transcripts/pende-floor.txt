================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added four well-attested, individuated named Pende mask-spirits as numen — Mbangu (the sorcery-afflicted, paralysed man), Tundu (the licensed clown-transgressor), Giwoyo/Kiwoyo-Muyombo (the bearded mask of the honoured dead), and Gitenga (the solar-disc mask and elder of the minganji) — following the corpus's own precedent of treating specific named masks (Gipogo, Pumbu) as figures. Each is documented across de Sousberghe's L'Art Pende (1959) and Strother's Inventing Masks (1998) and Pende (2008). I deliberately excluded generic spirit-classes the sources do not individuate: the collective ancestors (mvumbi), the unnamed minganji fibre masks as a group, and unnamed nature spirits, since the task forbids padding with non-individuated classes. The high-god names (Nzambi, Kalunga, Mawese) are already in the corpus, and I found no attested named founding-hero or culture-hero beyond the mask repertoire, so the additions are confined to the securely documented named masquerade personages.
