================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the five individuated non-pantheon figures of Ugaritic narrative and royal-ancestor ritual, which fit the Levantine label as constituted by the existing figure Ammurapi of Ugarit while avoiding the Canaanite deities proper: the legendary kings and heroes Kirta, Danel, Aqhat, and Pughat (KTU 1.14-1.16 and 1.17-1.19; Parker, Ugaritic Narrative Poetry, 1997; Wyatt, Religious Texts from Ugarit, 2002; Margalit, The Ugaritic Poem of AQHT, 1989), and the deified dynastic ancestor Ditanu of the funerary liturgy KTU 1.161 performed under Ammurapi himself (Pardee, Ritual and Cult at Ugarit, 2002; Pitard, BASOR 232, 1978). Secondary epic characters (Hurriya, Yassib, Yatpan, Shataqat) and the collective Rapiuma were excluded as insufficiently individuated or as spirit-classes.
