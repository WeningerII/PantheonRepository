================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added five epigraphically attested Cantabrian-Asturian deities missing from the corpus: Cabuniaeginus (Monte Cildá altar, Palencia), Epane (Cantabrian form of Epona at Monte Bernorio), Salus Umeritana (the Otañes patera near Flaviobriga), Nimmedus Aseddiagus (Ujo altar, Mieres), and Vagodonnaegus (La Milla del Río altars, publicly dedicated by Asturica Augusta) — the latter two Asturian, matching the corpus precedent of Aernus. Each rests on real epigraphic scholarship (Iglesias Gil 1976/2014, Diego Santos 1959/1986, Blázquez 1975, Olivares Pedreño 2002/2005, Marco Simón 2005, Peralta Labrador 2003, García y Bellido 1949). I excluded Tabaliaenus of Grases because the fragmentary reading '[Lug]ovio Tabaliaeno' is generally taken as an epithet of Lugus, and excluded unnamed collective cults (Matres) per the individuation rule. All five are solitary because the inscriptions record no genealogies or divine pairings; all use the registered era key 'Roman period', the AD 161 altar key belonging to the existing Erudinus record.
