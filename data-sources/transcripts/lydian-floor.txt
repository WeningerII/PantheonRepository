================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added three individuated, well-attested Lydian deities absent from the existing set: Qλdãns/Pldãns (the chief male god of the inscriptions, wired to the existing Artimus as her attested consort), Baki/Pakiš (the native wine-and-frenzy god cognate with Bakkhos), and Malis/Maλiš (a vegetation goddess continuing Anatolian Maliya, recovered via a Lydian–Greek bilingual equating her with Athena). Each rests on at least two real scholarly sources (Gusmani 1964; Greenewalt 2010; Sasseville & Euler, Kadmos 2019; Yakubovich 2024; Payne & Sasseville 2023; Payne & Wintjes 2016). I deliberately excluded the Marivda/"Dark Gods," since these are attested only as an unindividuated collective class rather than a named person, and imported figures such as Kore, whose Lydian standing is too thin to individuate. The corpus's native theonymic record for Lydian is otherwise largely exhausted by these plus the pre-existing entries.
