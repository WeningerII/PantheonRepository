RESEARCH SUMMARY AND AUTHORING DECISIONS
================================================================================

CHILDREN AUTHORED HERE (all four are genuinely absent from app/data.js and from the
data-sources/transcripts/ corpus — grepped by bare name and by greek_<name>):
  • greek_ino        — Ino, the Theban princess who reared Dionysus and leapt into the sea,
                       deified as the sea-goddess Leucothea (infant Melicertes → Palaemon).
  • greek_semele     — Semele, the mortal mother of Dionysus by Zeus, killed by his thunderbolt.
  • greek_agave      — Agave, wife of Echion and mother of Pentheus, whom she dismembered in
                       Bacchic frenzy (Euripides, Bacchae).
  • greek_polydorus_thebes — Polydorus of Thebes, the only son of Cadmus and Harmonia, king of
                       Thebes, husband of Nycteis and father of Labdacus (the Labdacid line).

EXISTING FIGURES referenced/wired (NOT re-authored):
  • greek_harmonia        (the cluster parent — deity; her own notes already read "Mother of Ino,
                          Semele, Agave, Autonoe, and Polydorus of Thebes"). Wired as parentId.
  • greek_autonoe         (the eldest Cadmeid daughter — ALREADY EXISTS, type mortal, era
                          heroic-age). She is a sibling of the four authored here; per the batch
                          rule for an already-existing sibling, each new figure carries a ONE-WAY
                          `sibling` relation to greek_autonoe (no reciprocal authored on her).

OTHER-PARENT (Cadmus) WIRING DECISION — accuracy-first, validator-safe:
  The other parent is Cadmus, founder-king of Thebes. Grepped exhaustively:
  there is NO registered figure greek_cadmus / greek_kadmos (neither the "id": "…" JSON form nor
  the id: '…' JS form). Cadmus appears in the corpus ONLY as prose and as an externalRef (e.g. in
  greek_harmonia's relations: {"name":"Cadmus (founder-king of Thebes, son of Agenor, brother of
  Europa)"}). The HARD RULE requires every parentId to resolve to an existing/in-batch figure, and
  the existing sibling greek_autonoe sets the exact precedent for this cluster: her parentIds are
  ["greek_harmonia"] alone, with the note that "Her parents Cadmus and Harmonia … are named in the
  tradition but lack corpus entries, so they are described rather than wired." Accordingly each
  child here is wired:
     - parentIds: ["greek_harmonia"]  /  parentRoles: {"greek_harmonia": "mother"}
     - Cadmus carried as an externalRef `father` relation + named in notes (described, not wired),
       so the registry stays internally consistent and no dangling parentId is created.
  (Cadmus is correctly the FATHER; Harmonia the MOTHER. Both parents are thus represented — the
  resolvable one as a parentId edge, the unregistered one as a documented externalRef — exactly as
  the existing Autonoe entry handles the identical situation.)

TYPE / TIER (anti-drift): Harmonia = deity (1.0); Cadmus is an unregistered second parent and is
  therefore counted as mortal (0). Strict descent fraction = (1.0 + 0)/2 = 0.5, which falls in the
  demigod band. BUT the Cadmeid princes/princesses are the canonical MORTAL Theban royal line (the
  human house of Thebes — Semele is the paradigmatic mortal consort of Zeus; Ino, Agave and
  Polydorus are mortal Theban royals who marry, rule and die as humans). The already-authored
  sibling greek_autonoe is `type: mortal`, and the rule's tie-breaker is "pick the LOWER tier" and
  "author … the tribal founders as mortal." All four are therefore authored as `type: mortal`,
  matching the established sibling and canon. (Ino's later apotheosis to Leucothea, and Semele's to
  Thyone, are post-mortal transformations recorded in lifecycle/notes, not a birth tier.)

ERA: temporal.era and every lifecycle.era = "heroic-age" exactly (= the cluster era and Harmonia's
  era), keeping each child no earlier than the parent.

CHILDREN-OF-THE-CHILDREN (Dionysus, Pentheus, Melicertes, Palaemon, Learchus, Labdacus) and the
  spouses (Athamas, Echion, Nycteus, Zeus) are named in the tradition but have NO corpus entries
  (grepped: no greek_dionysus, greek_pentheus, greek_melicertes, greek_learchus, greek_athamas,
  greek_aristaeus, etc.); they are described as externalRefs / in prose rather than wired.

PRIMARY/SECONDARY SOURCES (all real, verified against Perseus/standard editions):
  Apollodorus, Bibliotheca 3.4.2 (Cadmus & Harmonia's children; Ino↔Athamas, Autonoe↔Aristaeus,
  Agave↔Echion); 3.4.3 (Semele, Zeus, the thunderbolt, Dionysus sewn in the thigh and given to Ino
  and Athamas to rear); 3.5.2 (Pentheus, son of Agave and Echion, torn apart by the maenads);
  3.5.5 (Polydorus king of Thebes, marries Nycteis daughter of Nycteus, father of Labdacus).
  Hesiod, Theogony 940-942 (Semele bears Dionysus to Zeus), 975-978 (Ino bears Palaemon-Melicertes;
  the Cadmeid daughters). Euripides, Bacchae (Agave's sparagmos of Pentheus; the Theban frenzy).
  Ovid, Metamorphoses 3.259-315 (Semele & the thunderbolt), 4.416-542 (Athamas' madness, Learchus
  killed, Ino's leap, Ino→Leucothea / Melicertes→Palaemon). Pausanias 1.44.7-8 & 2.1.3 (Ino-
  Leucothea, Palaemon at the Isthmus); 9.5.2-3 (the Theban succession: Echion son-in-law of Cadmus;
  Polydorus → Labdacus, Nycteus regent). Diodorus Siculus 4.2-4 & 5.48-49 (Cadmean genealogy).
