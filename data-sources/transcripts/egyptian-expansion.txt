Author pass: Egyptian held 76 records, but about forty of those are pharaohs,
             queens and royal children — Ramesses II through VIII, Tutankhamun,
             Akhenaten, Nefertiti, Hatshepsut, Meritaten, Meketaten and the
             rest. The pantheon itself was carrying roughly thirty-six records
             for one of the largest attested god-lists in the ancient world.

================================================================================
THE GAP
================================================================================

A corpus-wide name check, over canonical names and aliases with Unicode
normalisation and across the WHOLE registry rather than the Egyptian slice,
ran 87 candidates. Seven were already present. **Eighty were absent**, among
them:

- **Apep**, the serpent Ra fights every night. The registry held Ra, Maat and
  the whole solar apparatus, and not the thing the apparatus exists to resist.
- **The Ogdoad of Hermopolis.** Nun and Amun were present; Naunet, Amaunet,
  Heh, Hauhet, Kek and Kauket — six of the eight — were not. An eightfold
  creation account was in the registry as a quarter of itself.
- **The Four Sons of Horus**, the personified canopic jars. Not one of them.
- **Sokar**, of the Memphite necropolis, and **Khenti-Amentiu**, who was the
  god of the dead at Abydos before Osiris took his title.
- **Seshat**, who does the writing and the measuring; **Hu** and **Sia**,
  utterance and perception, who crew the solar barque; **Ammit**, who is the
  other outcome of the weighing of the heart.
- **Montu**, the Theban war god three kings were named after; **Anhur**;
  **Satis** and **Anuket**, two-thirds of the Elephantine triad, with Khnum
  present alone.
- **Apis, Mnevis and Buchis**, the three great bull cults, and **Serapis**.
- **Wepwawet**, **Anput** and **Kebechet** — the rest of Anubis's household.
- **Shai, Renenutet and Meskhenet**, the three who decide a life at its start.

================================================================================
THREE DECISIONS UP FRONT
================================================================================

**Hapy is two gods, and the registry had the other one.** `egyptian_hapi`
is the androgynous Nile-inundation god, and it carries "Hapy" among its
aliases. The Hapy of the Four Sons of Horus is a different deity — a
baboon-headed guardian of the lungs — who happens to share the name. He is
authored as `egyptian_hapy_son_of_horus`, with the collision stated on the
record, exactly as Uttu was separated from Utu in the Mesopotamian pass.

**Naunet is added although Nun's record lists "Nunet (fem.)" as an alias.**
The Ogdoad is four *pairs*; listing the female half as an alias of the male
collapses one of the eight into nothing. She is authored with
`counterpart-of` pointing at Nun and a note saying the alias is there.

**Haroeris and Harpocrates are records, not epithets.** This programme
forbids ingesting transparent epithets that are only titles. Neither of these
is one: Haroeris (Horus the Elder) has his own half of the double temple at
Kom Ombo, and Harpocrates (Horus the Child) is a separately worshipped deity
across the Greco-Roman Mediterranean with an iconography of his own. Both
carry `counterpart-of` to `egyptian_horus` and a `variants[]` entry recording
that other treatments read them as forms rather than as gods.

                        SONS OF HORUS
================================================================================

The registry held Anubis and nobody who worked with him. Added: Wepwawet, who
opens the way ahead of the procession; Anput, his consort; Kebechet, his
daughter, who cools the dead with water; and the four gods who are the canopic
jars.

guardian goddess attached to each (Isis, Nephthys, Neith, Serqet) is stated on
the record rather than given an edge, because the pairing is a tutelage of a
kind the relation vocabulary here does not have.

Meskhenet the brick, Renenutet the milk and the secret name, Shai the span of
years — and none of the three was in the registry. Added with them: Heqet, who
breathes the life in; and the small gods of the processes a temple economy
actually ran on — pressing, threshing, weaving.

AUTHORED FIGURES — VI. BULLS, COWS, STARS, AND THE LATE SYNTHESES
================================================================================

Three great bull cults ran in Egypt for two thousand years, each with one live
animal at a time, identified by its markings, giving oracles, mummified and
buried in a catacomb; none of the three was in the registry. Added with them:
the cow goddess Hathor absorbed, her son, the ram of Mendes, the two stars
that make the calendar, the two Horuses that are not the Horus already here,
and the Ptolemaic god assembled to be worshipped by everybody at once.
