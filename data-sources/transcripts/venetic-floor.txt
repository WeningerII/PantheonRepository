================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the only two further individuated, securely attested Venetic divine figures found in scholarship: Altno-, the male titular god of the Altino (Fornace) sanctuary recovered from Venetic votive epigraphy by Marinetti (Marinetti & Prosdocimi 2006; Marinetti 2009, and the Roman-phase continuation into Jupiter per Cresci Marrone 2009), and Aponus, the healing god of the Euganean hot springs near Padua, whose Palaeovenetic lake sanctuary at San Pietro Montagnon (Dämmer 1986) precedes his Roman-era naming in Claudian, Martial and Suetonius (Lazzaro 1981). Per the task notes, Śainati and Pora were verified to be epithets already carried as alternate names on Reitia and Trumusiatis, so they were not authored as separate deities; plural or generic dedications (e.g. boundary gods) were excluded as non-individuated, and Belenus was excluded as Celtic rather than Venetic.
