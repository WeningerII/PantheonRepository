Tradition: Zoroastrian

Author pass: Zoroastrian held 33 records. This adds 37.

================================================================================
THE GAP
================================================================================

A 95-name check across the WHOLE registry returned 13 hits and 82 absences.
Six of the thirteen hits were aliases already carried on existing records —
Jamshid on Yima, Zahhak on Azhi Dahaka, Fereydun on Thraetaona, Garshasp on
Keresaspa, Bahman on Vohu Manah, Anahita on Aredvi Sura Anahita — and two were
false: "Indra" matched the Hindu god rather than the Zoroastrian daeva of the
same name, and "Vata" matched hindu_vayu.

**The registry did not hold Zarathustra.**

It held Ahura Mazda, Angra Mainyu, all six Amesha Spentas, nine yazatas and
fifteen figures of the Iranian legendary cycle, and it did not hold the prophet
the religion is named after — the man whose hymns are the oldest layer of the
Avesta and who is the speaker of the Gathas the whole tradition is built on.

That is not the only structural hole, and the others have the same shape:

- **The six Amesha Spentas were present and all six of their opponents were
  absent.** The Vendidad sets an arch-daeva against each: Aka Manah against
  Vohu Manah's good thought, the daeva Indra against Asha Vahishta's truth,
  Saurva against Khshathra Vairya's dominion, Nanghaithya against Spenta
  Armaiti's devotion, Taurvi against Haurvatat's wholeness, Zairich against
  Ameretat's immortality. Six for six. The registry held one side of a
  dualist religion's central opposition.
- **Tishtrya without Apaosha.** The corpus held the star-yazata whose one myth
  is a horse-fight against the drought demon for the waters of Vourukasha, and
  not the demon.
- **No creation account at all.** No Gayomart, the first man; no Mashya and
  Mashyana, the first couple who grow out of his body; no Gavaevodata, the
  sole-created ox whose death is the origin of animal life. The registry had
  Ahura Mazda and the being he creates against, and nothing either of them made.
- **No eschatology.** No Saoshyant, no Hushedar, no Hushedar-mah — the three
  saviours born a thousand years apart from the prophet's seed, who bring the
  Frashokereti. In a religion whose distinctive contribution to the history of
  ideas is a linear history with an end.
- **Aeshma absent — while the corpus holds Asmodeus.** The demon of wrath, the
  most prominent daeva of the older texts, was missing from the tradition he
  belongs to, in a registry that already held the figure he is generally taken
  to have become on the other side of a transmission.
- **The Shahnameh held from the middle.** Rostam, Sohrab, Zal, Rudaba, Kay
  Kavus, Kay Khosrow, Siyavash — and not Sam, who is Zal's father; not the
  Simurgh, who raised him; not Tahmineh, who is Sohrab's mother; not Afrasiab,
  who is the antagonist of the entire cycle; not Kaveh, whose apron on a spear
  is the Iranian flag for a thousand years; and not Salm, Tur and Iraj, the
  three sons whose quarrel is the reason Iran and Turan are at war for the rest
  of the book.

================================================================================
METHOD NOTE — THE SWEEP WAS RUN TWICE
================================================================================

Lane 14 established that a corpus sweep tests the registry against the author's
recall, and that re-running it after the first batch is in place surfaces what
the first pass crowded out. That is applied here from the start: the 95-name
list was built in two passes before any record was written, and it will be run
again after generation.

================================================================================
WHAT THIS BATCH DOES NOT INGEST
================================================================================

**Classes and collectives.** The fravashis (guardian spirits, always plural),
the daevas as a category, and the Amesha Spentas as a group are not records;
the named members are. The Chinvat Bridge is a place, not a person.

**Aliases already carried.** Jamshid, Zahhak, Fereydun, Garshasp and Bahman
are alt-names on records the corpus already holds, and this batch does not
duplicate them under their Persian forms.

================================================================================
AUTHORED FIGURES — III. THE DAEVAS
================================================================================

The registry held all six Amesha Spentas and none of the six arch-daevas the
Vendidad sets against them, one to one. In a dualist religion that is not a
gap in the margins; it is half the system.

A textual contradiction is carried rather than smoothed: the enumerations of the
six do not agree. One list gives Aka Manah, Indra, Saurva, Nanghaithya, Taurvi
and Zairich; another substitutes Nasu. Both readings are recorded on the
records they affect, and Nasu is ingested on his own footing.
