================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added one figure: Kamangarunga, the First Woman who emerged from the omumborombonga tree as wife of the first man, attested in Vedder (1928), Irle (1906), and Scheub's Dictionary of African Mythology (2000), and wired to the existing herero_kamangundu via a directed consort-of relation. The other divine names documented for the Herero (Karunga, Musisi, Kaheua, Nguvitjita, Karivangera, Kapurua) are recorded only as alternate designations of the supreme being already covered by herero_ndjambi and herero_mukuru, and Karunga is explicitly an alias in the existing Ndjambi entry. Herero religion is otherwise ancestor-focused (unnamed ovakuru and the okuruwo holy fire), with no further individuated named figures in the scholarship checked, so the attested record is essentially exhausted with this addition.
