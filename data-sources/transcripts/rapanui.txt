Figure-authoring batch — NEW tradition "Rapa Nui" (Easter Island), authored
from scratch. The Rapa Nui pantheon was entirely absent from the corpus; this
adds its firmly-attested core (8 figures). ERA_ORDER['Rapa Nui'] and
ERA_DATES['Rapa Nui'] are already registered with exactly the three eras used
here: primordial, mythic, mythic-prehistoric.

Sources are the three standard ethnographies of the island plus Thomson's
foundational 1891 record: Alfred Métraux, Ethnology of Easter Island (Bishop
Museum Bulletin 160, 1940); Katherine Routledge, The Mystery of Easter Island
(1919); Sebastian Englert, La Tierra de Hotu Matu'a (1948); and W. J. Thomson,
Te Pito te Henua, or Easter Island (1891).

Verified scope and genealogy wired WITHIN this batch:
- Makemake — supreme creator god, maker of mankind (Métraux 1940: "procreated
  red flesh from a calabash of water, mounded up some earth, and formed three
  males and one female"; offspring Tive, Rorai, Hova, and the noblewoman
  Arangi-kote-kote), and chief god of the tangata-manu (birdman) cult at Orongo.
- Haua — Makemake's COMPANION (Haua-tu'u-take-take, "chief of the eggs"); the
  two together drove the nesting sea-birds to the islet Motu Nui and instituted
  the offering "Take for Haua, for Makemake!". (reciprocal companion ↔ Makemake)
- Uoke (Uvoke) — the giant/god who levered up the lands with his great pole until
  it broke, leaving only the fragment that is Easter Island (hence Te Pito o te
  Henua, "the fragment of the earth", Barthel 1974). SOLITARY.
- Tangaroa — the ancestral god who came to Rapa Nui in the shape of a seal (with a
  human face and voice; the seal would not cook, proving him a chief of mana).
  Father of Rongo and brother of Hiro; a divine ancestor of Hotu Matu'a (Métraux
  1940:127). (reciprocal: father-of ↔ Rongo; sibling ↔ Hiro; ancestor-of Hotu Matu'a)
- Rongo — son of Tangaroa in the Rapa Nui royal genealogy; with Tangaroa, the
  divine ancestor from whom the ariki claimed descent (Métraux 1940:127).
- Hiro — god of wind and rain, whose tears are the rain ("E te uá, matavai-roa a
  Hiro-é" — "O rain, great tears of Hiro"), summoned with the stone trumpet Pu o
  Hiro; brother of Tangaroa.
- Hotu Matu'a — the founding ariki mau (paramount ancestor-king) who led the
  migration from the homeland Hiva and settled the island at Anakena; descendant
  of the gods Tangaroa and Rongo (Métraux 1940:127); his line continues through
  his firstborn son Tu'u Maheke. (type mortal; ancestor edge from Tangaroa & Rongo)
- Nuahine — the old woman in/of the moon (the Rapa Nui form of Hina). SOLITARY.

Only the widely published ethnographic record is used. The aku-aku (spirits) are
a CLASS, not authored as a figure; tangata manu is a cult ROLE, not a deity, and
is likewise not authored.
