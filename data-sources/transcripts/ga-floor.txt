================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added four individuated, well-attested Ga divinities missing from the corpus: the war and lagoon god Sakumo, the wealth goddess Ashiakle (daughter of the existing sea god Nai, linked via parentIds), the Korle lagoon goddess Naa Korle, and the smith-thunder god Gua. All are documented in M. J. Field's Religion and Medicine of the Gã People (1937), Marion Kilson's kpele studies (1971, 2013), John Parker's Making the Town (2000), and Lynch's African Mythology A to Z (2004). Further Ga religious beings (dzemawodzi, town gods such as Lakpa of La) are largely covered by the existing collective ga_kpele entry or are localized aspects rather than distinct individuated figures, so they were not duplicated.
