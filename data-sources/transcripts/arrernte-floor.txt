================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added four well-attested Arrernte figures beyond the recently authored creator core: Karora, the sleeping bandicoot creator of Ilbalintja (Strehlow 1947; Eliade 1973); the Ntjikantja serpent brothers who became the Magellanic Clouds and originated death-wailing (Strehlow 1947, 1971; Johnson 1998); Twanyirika, the initiation spirit voiced by the bullroarer (Spencer and Gillen 1899, 1927; Lang 1905); and the Yeperenye caterpillar ancestors of Mparntwe (Brooks 1991; Wilkins 1989), a named collective Dreaming parallel in kind to the existing Ungambikula entry. Weaker candidates were rejected: Malpunga (Roheim's wild-cat ancestor) could not be confirmed as specifically Arrernte rather than Pitjantjatjara, and Erintja could not be verified as an individuated being rather than Roheim's generic demon class. Beyond these, the ethnographic record (Spencer and Gillen; Strehlow; Roheim) individuates mainly totemic ancestor classes rather than further named beings.
