================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added two well-attested individuated figures: Magalim, the Telefol master bush spirit that Jorgensen (1998, Social Analysis) identifies alongside Afek as one of the two central cosmological figures of Telefolmin, and Yomnok, the androgynous echidna/fruit-bat ancestor and sibling of Afek in Bimin-Kuskusmin cosmology documented by Poole (1981, 1996). I considered and excluded Tibulam, which Jorgensen (Paideuma 2001) treats as a late-1970s myth narrative rather than a traditional individuated being, and generic spirit classes (bisel, sinik, usong) as non-individuated. Beyond these, the Mountain Ok literature (Barth 1975; Craig and Hyndman, Children of Afek, 1990) centres overwhelmingly on Afek and Umoim, and no further securely named individual figures emerged.
