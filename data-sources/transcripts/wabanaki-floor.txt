================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added five genuinely individuated, well-attested Wabanaki figures absent from the existing set. From the Mi'kmaq creation cycle recorded by Stephen Augustine and Ruth Holmes Whitehead I added Nukumi (the grandmother formed from stone) and Netawansum (Gluskap's foam-born nephew), wiring both to the tradition's creator (Tabaldak/Kisulk) and to each other, plus Apistanewj the Marten, Gluskap's shapeshifting helper-brother from Rand and Leland (subordinate to Gluskap). From Leland and Speck I added two personified nature-powers subdued by Gluskap: Wuchowsen the Wind Bird (distinct from the existing Pamola) and Aglebemu the giant water-hoarding frog. Other recurring names in the corpus (Pukwudgies, Chenoo, the frog/wind classes) are spirit-classes rather than individuals, and the sorcerer Winpe and beings like Coolpujot are too thinly and locally attested to individuate confidently, so I stopped at these five.
