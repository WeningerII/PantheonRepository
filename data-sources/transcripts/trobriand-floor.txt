REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added eight Malinowski- and Senft-attested individuated figures: Topileta, guardian of the dead on Tuma (Malinowski 1916; Seligman 1910); Bolutukwa, virgin mother of the existing culture-hero Tudava, and Dokonikan, the ogre he slew (Malinowski 1926); Toweyre'i and Na'ukuwakula of the Kudayuri flying-canoe myth, wired to the existing Mokatuboda (Malinowski 1922, ch. XII; Tambiah 1983); Inuvayla'u of the Kwaybwaga legend (Malinowski 1929); and the lovers Imdeduya and Yolina, documented in five variants by Senft (2017). Details for the Kudayuri sister rely on Argonauts ch. XII and are kept conservative since full-text verification online was blocked; remaining named Trobriand beings (tauva'u, tokway, generic baloma) are spirit classes rather than individuals and were excluded, as was Kasabwaybwayreta, whose myth Malinowski localizes at Tewara outside the Trobriands proper.
