================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added four figures attested in the Viena/Karelian rune, charm and tale corpus and absent from both the Karelian and Finnish traditions: Antero Vipunen (the dead giant sage of SKVR I), Syöjätär (ogress and mythic mother of the snake, especially prominent in Karelian charms and wonder tales), Hiisi (demonized master of the sacred grove, maker of the demon elk), and Iku-Turso (the sea monster of the Sampo-voyage runes, bound by Väinämöinen and wired to karelian_vainamoinen accordingly). I deliberately skipped Kalevala-core figures already held by the Finnish tradition (Louhi, Tapio, Ahti, Ilmatar, Tuoni family, Pellervoinen, etc.) and vague spirit-classes such as haltiat. Citations rest on Harva (1948), Haavio (1952, 1967), Siikala (2012), Kuusi/Bosley/Branch (1977), Oinas (1985) and the SKVR corpus.
