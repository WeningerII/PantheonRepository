Era (registered, exact): "Baule traditional religion; continuing"
Author pass: expansion of the Baule pantheon — well-attested individuated deities still MISSING.

EXISTING FIGURES (NOT re-authored; referenced by id):
  - baule_nyamien  (Nyamien / Nyamye — the supreme, remote sky-god and creator)
  - baule_asie     (Asie / Asye — the earth-god/goddess, active power over the inhabited world)
  - baule_mbotumbo (the monkey-god; the figure also/widely called "Gbekre")

================================================================================
RESEARCH SUMMARY AND AUTHORING DECISION
================================================================================

The Baule are an Akan people of central Côte d'Ivoire; their religion is comparatively
thinly documented and is centered far less on a developed roster of named high gods than
on the earth-god Asie, the nature/power-spirits (asye usu, amwin/amuin), the ancestors,
and the otherworld (blolo). The principal scholarship is Vincent Guerry, *Life with the
Baoulé* (1975; orig. *La Vie quotidienne dans un village Baoulé*, INADES, 1972); Susan
Mullin Vogel, *Baule: African Art, Western Eyes* (Yale, 1997); and Pierre Étienne's
ethnographic work on Baule village society. The genuinely INDIVIDUATED divine core is
small and is already largely covered by the three existing figures.

ONE new firmly-attested individuated deity is authored here:

  • ANANGAMA (Anangaman / Anangaman Nyamien) — the creator who ordered the world.
    Across Baule popular religion and ethnography the supreme creator of the firmament is
    named "Anangaman Nyamien," and the formula "Anangaman Boli-Alliè" is glossed "Dieu le
    créateur de l'univers" (God the creator of the universe). The name designates the
    active creator who ordered (set in place) the three-tiered Baule cosmos and to whose
    sky-realm / to the otherworld (blolo) the souls of the dead return. Sources treat
    "Anangaman" and "Nyamien" sometimes as a single compound name for the one supreme
    creator and sometimes (in the ethnographic/scholarly framing the registry follows,
    after Guerry and Étienne) as the creator-demiurge aspect set just below the remote,
    intangible high god Nyamien. The figure is therefore authored as a deity and wired to
    baule_nyamien with a DIRECTED subordinate-to relation (no reciprocal required), with
    the dual reading documented in the notes so the registry does not overstate a
    separation the sources leave open.

FIGURES DELIBERATELY NOT AUTHORED (with cited reasons):

  • GBEKRE (Gbekre / Aboya) — although the task flagged a possible "rat-headed judge of
    souls," the attested figure called Gbekre is the MONKEY-god, i.e. the SAME being
    already in the corpus as baule_mbotumbo (which lists "Gbekre" among its alt names and
    already carries the underworld-judgment / judge-of-the-dead domain and the
    cup-bearing monkey power-figure). Godchecker ("GBEKRE — the Baule God of Judgment"),
    ArtTribal, and Vogel all identify Gbekre with the cup-bearing monkey power-object;
    Vogel/Delafosse note that the word *gbekre* literally means "mouse/rat" (the source of
    the "rat-headed" confusion), but the cult figure is iconographically the monkey-god.
    Authoring Gbekre separately would duplicate baule_mbotumbo, so it is NOT authored.

  • ALOUROUA (Aluröa) — listed by Godchecker as a "Baule God of Creation" ("all-powerful
    but extremely shy; no one knows what he looks like"). In the sources this is understood
    as another NAME for the same remote supreme creator already in the corpus as
    baule_nyamien, not an independent individuated deity; NOT authored, to avoid a
    duplicate of Nyamien.

  • KAKAGYE (Kaka Guié) — appears in popular deity-list compilations (Godchecker) as a
    "Baule God of Funerals," but it is not securely individuated in the standard
    ethnographies (Guerry, Vogel, Étienne) and is not corroborated by a scholarly source;
    NOT authored on the standard of "firmly-attested individuated deity."

  • blolo bian / blolo bla (otherworld spirit-spouses), asye usu (bush spirits), amwin /
    amuin (power-beings/objects), zuly (protective spirits) — these are CLASSES of spirits,
    not individuals; per the brief they are NOT authored as figures.

CONCLUSION: With Nyamien, Asie and Mbotumbo already present, the attested individuated
Baule core is nearly complete; the one well-attested addition is ANANGAMA. Returning a
single figure is the correct, evidence-bounded outcome here.
