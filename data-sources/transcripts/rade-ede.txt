When rival chiefs, among them Mtao Grứ and the wealthy Mtao Mxây, carry off his wives, he pursues and kills them and rises to become the richest and most feared mtao of the highlands, his greatness proclaimed by resounding gong-sets. Restless and defiant, he fells the sacred smuk tree of his wives' lineage; the wives die and he climbs to the sky-lord Aê Diê to win the remedy that revives them.
