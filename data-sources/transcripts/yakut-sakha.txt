PURPOSE
Extends the existing Yakut (Sakha) tradition with two genuinely-new figures of the Lena-basin pantheon: the birth-goddess Ayısıt (the northern counterpart of Umay) and the dangerous thunder-and-fire lord Uluu Toyon, distinct from the already-registered Ürüng Aiyy Toyon, Ai Toyon and Abaasy.
