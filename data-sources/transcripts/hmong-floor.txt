================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added six individuated, well-attested figures: the death-lord Ntxwj Nyug (the documented counterpart of the existing Nyuj Vaj Tuam Teem, not a duplicate of him), the first shaman Siv Yis, the frog creator Nplooj Lwg whose dying curse explains human mortality, the household fortune spirit Xwm Kab, the child-soul goddess Niam Nkauj Kab Yeeb (a Guanyin syncretism), and the Dab Pog birth couple, each carrying two or more real scholarly citations (Tapp 1989; Chindarsi 1976; Lemoine 1986; Mottin 1984; Falk 1996; K.-L. Yang 2006; D. Cha 2003; Lee and Tapp 2010). Siv Yis and Ntxwj Nyug are tied into the existing pantheon through Saub (who commissioned the first shaman) and through their attested enmity. I deliberately excluded the sun-maiden and moon-lord pair (Nkauj Hnub, Nraug Hli) and the thunder lord Xob, whose individuated attestation proved chiefly popular-folkloric or to belong to the distinct Hmu (Guizhou Miao) Butterfly Mother epic cycle rather than to Hmong tradition proper, and I excluded non-individuated spirit classes such as poj ntxoog forest spirits and zaj dragons.
