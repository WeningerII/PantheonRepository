REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added six individuated, primary-ethnography-attested Pomo figures: Thunder Man, Coyote's son charged with thunder and the salmon runs (Loeb 1926; Barrett 1933); the monster-bird Kilak/Gilak (Barrett 1933; Curtis 1924; de Angulo 1930); the bird-hero Falcon/tciya, Coyote's grandson (Barrett 1906, 1933; de Angulo & Freeland 1928); Obsidian Man of Mount Konocti (Barrett 1933); Frog Woman of Frog Woman Rock, Coyote's wife and Obsidian Man's mother (Clark & Williams 1954; Hudson ms c. 1892 via California Historical Landmark 549 documentation); and Calnis, the eastern world-end companion impersonated alongside the existing Kuksu (Barrett 1917; Loeb 1926, 1932). Excluded as non-qualifying: "Dasan," documented as a Northern Pomo name of the existing Kuksu, and "Makila," a tertiary-dictionary thunder god not traceable in primary ethnography whose niche the attested Thunder Man occupies; likewise the bagil water-monsters and olayome rock-people, which are spirit classes rather than named individuals. Sun-man and the Sun-people appear only at index level in Barrett 1933 without recoverable individuated detail, so they were left out; note that direct fetching of full texts was proxy-blocked, so verification relied on search-indexed content of Barrett 1906/1917/1933, Loeb 1926/1932, Curtis vol. 14, JAF 41 (1928), American Anthropologist 32 (1930), and the landmark documentation.
