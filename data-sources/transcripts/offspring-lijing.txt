================================================================================
RESEARCH SUMMARY AND AUTHORING DECISION
================================================================================

The locus classicus for the three brothers is the Ming vernacular novel *Fengshen Yanyi*
(*Investiture of the Gods*, 封神演義, attributed to Xu Zhonglin / Lu Xixing, c. 1567–1620),
which makes Jinzha (金吒), Muzha (木吒) and Nezha (哪吒) the three sons of the Chentang-Pass
commander Li Jing (李靖) by his wife Lady Yin (殷夫人). The brothers leave home to study under
Chan-Daoist immortals of the Chan ("Explanation") sect and return to fight for the Zhou
against the Shang under Jiang Ziya (姜子牙). Muzha is carried further into *Xiyouji*
(*Journey to the West*, 西遊記, Wu Cheng'en, c. 1592), where as Guanyin's disciple Hui'an
(惠岸) he helps subdue Sun Wukong and the demon Red Boy. The standard modern scholarship on
the family and its Buddhist substrate is Meir Shahar, *Oedipal God: The Chinese Nezha and His
Indian Origins* (Univ. of Hawai'i Press, 2015); the corpus already cites Shahar for Li Jing
(= Vaiśravaṇa) and Nezha (= Nalakūbara). The brothers' Buddhist afterlives — Muzha as Guanyin's
attendant Hui'an, and the wider identification of the trio with the bodhisattva-disciples of
the Chan masters (Wenshu/Mañjuśrī, Puxian/Samantabhadra, Cihang/Guanyin) — are recorded in the
Ming "Three Religions" pantheon material and summarized in the reference literature.

WIRING DECISION. Both figures are wired as sons of the cluster parent Li Jing
(parentIds: ["chinese_li_jing"], parentRoles {father}). Their mother, Lady Yin (殷夫人), is
NOT a registered figure (no id resolves in the corpus), so per the brief she is left
unregistered and is not added to parentIds; she is named in notes/etymology only. The two are
wired as siblings of each other (RECIPROCAL within this batch) and each carries a one-way
sibling edge to the already-existing youngest brother chinese_nezha (chinese_nezha presently
has no `relations` array, so the reciprocal on Nezha is left for the orchestrator to add).

TYPE BY DESCENT. Li Jing is a deity (divinity fraction 1.0); the second parent, Lady Yin, is
an unregistered mortal (0.0). Average = (1.0 + 0.0) / 2 = 0.5, which falls in the demigod band
(0.375–0.75). Both brothers are therefore authored as type = "demigod" (matching their
youngest brother's half-divine, immortal-disciple standing while respecting the descent rule;
where unsure the LOWER tier is taken, and demigod is the band value, not a promotion).

LIFECYCLE. Per the rule that lifecycle is recorded only for figures who die or transform:
  - MUZHA receives a lifecycle: he genuinely transforms across the two novels, translated from
    a Daoist disciple of Puxian Zhenren (Fengshen Yanyi) into the Buddhist attendant-bodhisattva
    Hui'an / Mucha, disciple of Guanyin (Journey to the West). Both lifecycle eras are exactly
    "mythic".
  - JINZHA receives NO lifecycle: in the sources he neither dies nor undergoes a
    death/transformation — he remains an immortal Daoist disciple of Wenshu Guangfa Tianzun and
    (already a cultivated immortal) is not even among the 365 invested on the Fengshen Bang. A
    lifecycle entry would be unwarranted, so it is omitted (as it is for chinese_nezha).

INHERITABILITY. None of the brothers' powers are blood-descent transmissions (they are
acquired by discipleship from immortals, not inherited from Li Jing/Vaiśravaṇa), so every
faculty is inheritability "none".
