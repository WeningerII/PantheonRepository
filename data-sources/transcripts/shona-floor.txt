================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added three individuated figures that scholarship documents beyond the existing set. Kaguvi (Kagubi) is the male mhondoro war-spirit and Nehanda's ritual consort, and Gumboreshumba is its historical medium hanged on 27 April 1898 — together they mirror the Nehanda/Charwe spirit-and-medium pairing already in the corpus, and are wired to those figures by directed consort-of and subordinate-to relations. Murenga Sororenzou (Pfumojena), the legendary elephant-headed founder-warrior whose name is the etymological root of "chimurenga," is added as a founding ancestor and wired as father-of the existing Chaminuka and Nehanda, following widely attested Shona genealogies. All three rest on standard scholarship (Ranger 1967; Lan 1985; Beach 1979/1986; Gelfand 1962; Pongweni 1982; Vambe 2004; Mutswairo 1996). I deliberately excluded generic ancestor-classes and figures with contested attribution (e.g. Mushavatu, the Mwari-shrine officiants, and the Tonga river-spirit Nyaminyami, which is not Shona), as they are either non-individuated or outside the tradition.
