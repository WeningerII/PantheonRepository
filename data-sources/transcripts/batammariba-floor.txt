================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added five named divinities individuated in the core ethnographies beyond the existing solar-earth-storm triad: the ancestral serpent Fawafa/Fawaafa (Sewane 2003/2004; UNESCO 2004 dossier), and the initiation divinities Fayenfe (war and death), Litakon (twins and fertility), Kupon (divination, served by the upon diviner; R. Blier in Peek 1991), and Fakuntifa (lizard deity of women's initiation), all documented in S. P. Blier, The Anatomy of Architecture (1987). Blier notes these initiation divinities are conceived as families with male and female instantiations; each nonetheless bears a distinct proper name, domain, and cult, so they are recorded as named deities with that caveat stated in their notes. All are wired as subordinate to the existing creator Kuiye, and Fawafa additionally to the earth goddess Butan.
