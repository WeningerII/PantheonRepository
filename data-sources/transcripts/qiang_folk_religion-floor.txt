REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added one securely attested, individuated figure: Abba Mula (阿爸木纳), the Golden Monkey God who serves as the tutelary "father god" of Qiang shamans, a Prometheus-like fire-thief and recoverer of the lost birch-bark scriptures. He is documented in David Crockett Graham's ethnography The Customs and Religion of the Ch'iang (1958) and Michael Oppitz's study of Qiang shamanic ritual objects (Res, 2004), and is wired beneath the existing supreme Heaven God Mubyasei as one of the lesser tutelary gods. I deliberately stopped there: the remainder of the documented Qiang pantheon consists of the already-registered great deities (Heaven God, Heavenly Maiden, Mountain God and Goddess, the earthly ancestor Dou'anzhu) and of function-gods named only by domain (god of fire, goddess of women, ox king, tree and river gods) or unnamed spirit-classes, none of which scholarship individuates as a named being. Padding with those domain-labels would misrepresent the record, so a single addition is the evidence-bounded result.
