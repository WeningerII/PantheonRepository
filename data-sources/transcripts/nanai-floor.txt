================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added two genuinely individuated named beings that the existing five-figure set omitted: Mameldi (Мамельди/Mamaldi), the Nanai female progenitress-demiurge who twists the earth out of nine serpent-streams and bears the first human from a drop of her blood, and Na Enduri (На эндури, 'Earth-Deity'), the aged earth-goddess of the muddy island whose soil the sky-god Boa Enduri forms into the continents. Both are attested across multiple scholarly sources (T. Yu. Sem's monograph and dissertation on the Tungus-Manchu pantheon, V. A. Avrorin's Nanai folklore corpus, I. A. Lopatin's Goldi ethnography, and A. V. Smolyak), and both wire into the existing pantheon — Mameldi as the maternal counterpart-consort of Khado, and Na Enduri as the lower-world complement of Boa Enduri. I deliberately excluded the three-suns hero "Guranta/Goronta" (already an alias of the existing Khado) and declined to add spirit-classes such as the dzhulin clan guardians, the ayami tutelary spirits, or the unnamed three suns, since scholarship treats these as categories rather than single named individuals.
