Greek — the House of Atreus / Pelopid line. Admissible now that the old
"no nymph/nereid/titan-descended figures" rule is RETIRED: Tantalus is son of
Zeus by the nymph Plouto (a 'numen'-class mother recorded as externalRef), so he
computes demigod and the whole Pelopid line cascades from him. Sources: Homer
Iliad/Odyssey; Hesiod Catalogue/Theogony; Pindar Olympian 1; Aeschylus Oresteia
(Agamemnon, Choephoroi, Eumenides); Euripides (Iphigenia at Aulis / in Tauris,
Electra, Orestes); Apollodorus Bibliotheca + Epitome 2; Pausanias.

Genealogy wired so divinity computes: Zeus → Tantalus(demigod, mother=numen
Plouto) → Pelops(quartigod) → Atreus/Thyestes(scion) → Agamemnon(scion, by
Aerope) / Aegisthus(by Thyestes' own daughter Pelopia). Clytemnestra is the
mortal daughter of Tyndareus & Leda (Helen's mortal-fathered sister); her
children by Agamemnon (Iphigenia, Electra, Chrysothemis, Orestes) are authored
mortal (scion father + mortal mother). Anaxibia (Atreus's daughter, mother of
Pylades) added as a scion sibling of Agamemnon.

NB: greek_iphigenia_helen_variant already exists (the Stesichorean Helen+Theseus
demigod reading); greek_iphigenia here is the dominant Agamemnon+Clytemnestra
mortal reading — distinct ids, cross-referenced. greek_pleisthenes already
exists in the corpus as a SON of Helen+Menelaus (not the Pelopid Pleisthenes),
so it is not re-authored or collided with here.
