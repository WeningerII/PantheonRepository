Scythian royal house (Herodotus, Histories book 4):
  - Gnurus: father of the sage Anacharsis and of King Saulios (Hdt 4.76).
  - Saulios: brother of Anacharsis, the king who shot Anacharsis; father of King Idanthyrsus
    (Anacharsis is named uncle of Idanthyrsus at Hdt 4.76).
  - Ariapeithes: Scythian king, father of Skyles (by an Istrian woman) and Octamasades;
    slain by Spargapeithes the Agathyrsian (Hdt 4.78).
  - Octamasades: brother of Skyles, set up as king by the Scythians, who beheaded Skyles
    (Hdt 4.78-80); son of a daughter of the Thracian king Teres.

Selk'nam Hain spirit:
  - Shoort: the husband of the underworld hag Xalpen, the most important and most feared
    of the masked Hain spirits (Gusinde 1931; Chapman 1982).
