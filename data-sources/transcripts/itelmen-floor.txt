Author pass: floor-raising expansion — attested individuated figures still missing (10 added).

================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the ten individuated Itelmen figures still missing from the corpus: the tale-cycle children of Kutkh and Miti (Ememkut, Sisilkhan, Sirim; Jochelson/Worth 1961, Menovshchikov 1974, Meletinskii 1979 and 1982) and the 18th-century pantheon recorded by Krasheninnikov (1755; Eng. trans. Crownhart-Vaughan 1972) and Steller (1774) and summarized by Sirina & Murashko (2010): Symskalin, Tyzhil-Kutkhu, Siduka, Gaech, Bilyukai/Pilyachuch, Tuil and Mitg. Pilyachuch is folded into Bilyukai because the sources equate the two names, and Krasheninnikov's Il'kkhum was deliberately not authored since she fills the same wife-of-Kutkh slot as the existing Miti record. Remaining attested names were judged too thin to individuate here and are noted only in prose: single-mention genealogical or weather figures (Amleia, Sidukamshich, Khutlyzhich, the sea-maker Utleigyn, the wind-man Balakitg and his wife Savina-Kugagt, the dog Kozei), minor tale characters (Kotkhanamtalkhan, Nyaa, Anaraklnavt, Chelkutkh, Marokl'navt), and unindividuated spirit classes (gamuly, ushakhchu, kanna).
