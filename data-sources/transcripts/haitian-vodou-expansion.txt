PANTHEON REGISTRY — FIGURE-AUTHORING TRANSCRIPT
Tradition: Haitian Vodou
Eras (registered, exact): Haitian Vodou traditional religion; continuing
Author pass: Haitian Vodou held 12 records. This adds 23.

================================================================================
THE GAP — THE CORPUS HELD PAPA LEGBA AND NOT KALFOU
================================================================================

The Haitian Vodou key held **twelve records**:

    Bondye                      the supreme God
    Papa Legba                  who opens the way at the crossroads
    Danbala Wedo, Ayida Wedo    the serpent and the rainbow
    Ezili Freda, Ezili Dantò    love, and the mother who fights
    Lasirèn                     the mermaid
    Agwe                        the sea
    Ogou Feray                  the warrior-smith
    Bawon Samdi, Manman Brijit  the graveyard and its mistress
    Gede Nibo                   leader of the spirits of the dead

Every one of those is a **headline Rada or Gede lwa**. What is missing is not a
handful of minor names — it is **an entire rite**.

**The Petwo nation is absent from the corpus completely.** The lwa divide into
nanchon, "nations"; the Rada are cool and sweet-tempered and are held to come
from the African homeland, and the Petwo are hot and volatile and are described
as born out of slavery and resistance in the New World. **Every one of the twelve
records is Rada or Gede. Not one is Petwo** — no Dan Petro, whose rite it is; no
Ti Jean Petro; no Marinette; no Bosou; no Kalfou.

**And the corpus held Papa Legba without Kalfou.** Papa Legba is the benevolent
opener of the way; Kalfou is his cousin-brother and his dark counterpart, the
literal embodiment of the crossroads, the spirit of dangerous crossroads, of
confusion and of blocked pathways. **Holding the one who opens the way and not
the one who blocks it** is this programme's recurring fault — Arawn without
Hafgan, Beowulf without Grendel, Kumarbi without Anu — arriving in a nineteenth
tradition.

It also held **no priest and no priestess**. Papa Loko Atisou and Manbo Ayizan
Velekete are the archetypal houngan and manbo, who confer the office of
priesthood in the kanzo rite and hand over the asson. **A religion whose entire
structure runs through initiation was in the corpus without the two lwa who
perform it.**

================================================================================
TWO OF THIS BATCH'S CANDIDATES TURNED OUT TO BE HIDING INSIDE AN ALIAS LIST
================================================================================

The sources give **Bawon** three aspects: **Bawon Samdi**, ruler of the
graveyard; **Baron La Croix**, guardian of the gravestone; and **Baron
Cimetière**, guardian of the grave. Three stations, three names, three jobs.

`vodou_bawon_samdi` carries **"Baron La Croix" and "Baron Cimetière" as
aliases.** So the corpus holds three guardians as one record, and a sweep for
either of the other two resolves onto Samdi and reports him present.

The same thing has happened to the **Gede**, who are the largest family of lwa in
the religion: `vodou_gede_nibo` carries the bare **"Gede"** and **"Ghede"** as
aliases, so the family name resolves onto one member of it. And to the **Ogou**:
`vodou_ogou`, primary "Ogou Feray", carries **"Ogou Badagri"** — a distinct lwa
of the Nago nation, a general in the armies of Ogou — as an alias of another.

**This batch does not create any of those three as records**, because they would
duplicate names the corpus already resolves, and it cannot fix the alias lists
without mutating pre-existing records. All three are censused as `EXISTING` with
the reason. **Lane 27 found that a false absence invites a duplicate; this is the
matching case — an alias that is really a separate figure makes a real absence
invisible.** Seventh lane to pay the never-mutate cost, and the first where the
cost is not a missing alias but a missing record.

================================================================================
THE RESEARCH BUDGET RAN OUT, AND THIS BATCH IS BOUNDED BY THAT
================================================================================

**This session's web-search budget was exhausted before this lane was authored.**
Every record here rests on material retrieved while the budget lasted, and the
batch stops where that material stops.

That is why this is **23 figures and not the 30-odd the tradition would support**.
Ezili Kokobe gets a name and a gloss because a name and a gloss is what the
retrieved treatment gives her, and the record says so. Nothing is padded out, no
attribute is supplied from general knowledge, and every figure the sources named
but did not describe is censused **BLOCKED — sourcing**, with the name recorded
so the next lane can start from it rather than rediscover it.

================================================================================
WHAT THIS BATCH DOES NOT INGEST
================================================================================

The **twenty-one nanchon** are a classificatory scheme, not figures; the Rada,
Petwo, Kongo and Nago **nations** likewise. **The asson** is a ritual object and
is named on Loko's and Ayizan's records rather than ingested. **Kanzo** is a rite.
**Dosou Agadja**, king of Dahomey 1708–1732, is named in the Bosou source as a
historical reign-marker and belongs to Dahomey, not to this key. The sources say
over a thousand lwa exist and at least 232 names are recorded; **a number is not
a roster**, and nothing is generated from it.
