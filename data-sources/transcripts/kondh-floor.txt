================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added four genuinely individuated, named Kondh divine figures beyond the existing five Pennu. Niyam Raja, the 'King of the Law' and supreme god-king of the Dongria Kondh of Niyamgiri, and Nirantali, the Kuttia Kondh creatrix who makes sun, moon, earth and the first humans, are both strongly attested (Elwin's Orissa myth collections; Padel & Das 2010; Behera & Padhi 2022) and represent the two major sub-tribal cosmologies distinct from the plains Bura Pennu pantheon. Dinga Pennu, the judge of the dead, is drawn from Macpherson's classic 1852 account and Tylor, and is wired to the creator pair as a second-order Pennu. Banka-Mundi, a hunting/fertility goddess, is included with a low weighting because her attestation rests mainly on comparative encyclopaedias (Michael Jordan) rather than primary ethnography. I deliberately excluded Dharni/Darni Penu (already an alias of the existing Tari Pennu) and the merely functional lesser gods (hill, stream, village, firstfruits, increase) that Macpherson and Tylor describe by role but for which no stable individual proper name is recoverable in the accessible sources.
