REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added four individuated figures completing the documented Macuxi cycle around the existing Insikiran: Makunaima (trickster-demiurge brother, Koch-Grünberg 1924; im Thurn 1883; Sá 2004), Wei the Sun as divine father (Koch-Grünberg 1924; Farage 1991), Anikê, the third brother named with Makunaima and Insikiran in the Macuxi erenkon songs (Farage 1991; Santilli 2001), and Piai'mã, the cannibal giant/'great shaman' adversary of the circum-Roraima corpus. Generic spirit-classes such as the canaimé were deliberately excluded as non-individuated. Sources checked: Koch-Grünberg's Vom Roraima zum Orinoco II, Farage's As Muralhas dos Sertões, Santilli's Pemongon Patá, im Thurn's Among the Indians of Guiana, and Sá's Rain Forest Literatures.
