REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

The individuated Swazi divine record is essentially exhausted by the existing three figures: Kuper (An African Aristocracy, 1947; The Swazi, 1963; the "Swazi Religion" entry in the Encyclopedia of Religion) and Marwick (The Swazi, 1940) name only the remote supreme being Mvelinqangi/Mkhulumnqande, his one-legged messenger Mlentengamunye, and the sky princess Nomkhubulwane; everything else is the collective ancestor cult (emadloti) and unnamed spirit classes (emandzawe, tinzunzu), which I excluded as non-individuated. I added one figure: Somhlolo (Sobhuza I), the individually named and venerated royal ancestor whose deathbed dream-vision of the book and the coin is a foundational sacred narrative of Swazi religion, well attested in Kuper, Matsebula (1972), and Mzizi (1995). Other historical kings appear in ritual only collectively, so no further additions qualified.
