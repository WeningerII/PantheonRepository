REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added eight individuated, double-cited figures that scholarship documents beyond the existing three: the cosmic ocean-force Kalunga (Fu-Kiau 2001; MacGaffey 1986), the nkisi-origin and twin-patron Funza wired as subordinate to Nzambi a Mpungu (Janzen and MacGaffey 1974; MacGaffey 2002; Laman 1962), the Woyo serpent pantheon of Ngoyo — Kuitikuiti, Mboze, Bunzi and Lusunzi, forming a cited genealogical chain (Knappert 1971; Volavka 1998; de Heusch 2000; Scheub 2000; Lynch and Roberts 2010) — the Loango rainbow serpent Mbumba Luangu (Bittremieux 1936; Hersak 2001), and the primordial androgyne Mahungu created by Nzambi (Fu-Kiau 1969; Janzen 1982). I slightly exceeded the typical range because the coastal Kongo record (Woyo, Vili, Yombe) is unusually rich and every figure carries at least two real scholarly citations. Deliberately excluded: Makanga and Mbatilanda (attested only as supporting actors inside the single Knappert-recorded tale), the thunder-lightning pair Nzazi and Lusiemo (individuation rests mainly on popular summaries rather than the ethnographic literature I could verify), the folktale trickster Moni-Mambu (heroic tale cycle rather than a divine or ancestral cult figure), and historical-legendary persons such as Lukeni lua Nimi and Kimpa Vita, who fall outside the registered primordial/mythic era keys.
