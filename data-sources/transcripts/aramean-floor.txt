================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added three epigraphically secure, distinctly Aramaean deities: Sahar, the moon god of Nerab and 'Lord of Harran' (KAI 218, 225-226); Šamš, the sun god of the Sam'al dynastic pantheon, the Katumuwa stele and the Sefire and Nerab curses (KAI 214; Pardee, BASOR 356, 2009); and Iluwer, the storm-god patron of Zakkur of Hamath at Tell Afis (KAI 202). Nikkal and Resheph were deliberately excluded because they already exist in the corpus under the Canaanite tradition, Nusku because he is a Mesopotamian import at Nerab rather than a distinctly Aramaean figure, and Baalshamin (of the Zakkur stele) because this corpus files him under Palmyrene; El and Kubaba likewise belong to the Canaanite and Luwian spheres. Beyond these, the Iron Age Aramaean epigraphic record (surveyed via Lipiński 2000 and Niehr 2014) yields chiefly compound manifestations of Hadad and non-individuated god-list members.
