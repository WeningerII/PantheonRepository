Vietnamese and Cambodian courts long treated the Fire King and the Water King as sovereigns of the interior, exchanging periodic gifts recorded from the eighteenth century onward. Jacques Dournes, whose Potao (1977) remains the fullest study, read the institution as a Jarai theory of power in which authority is deliberately held apart from coercion.
