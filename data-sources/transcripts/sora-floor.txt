================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added four individuated, multiply-attested Sora powers beyond the existing core: Jalia, the wandering sickness-deity (Fawcett 1888; Thurston 1909, with rich cult detail); Rattud/Ra'tud, the third great collective sonum category alongside the already-registered Sun- and Earth-Sonums (Vitebsky 1993 and 2017; Fawcett's 'Rathu' in Thurston 1909); and the village deities Jodisum (Patel, Orissa Review 2005; INTACH 2018) and Manduasum (INTACH 2018; Odisha State Tribal Museum/SCSTRTI documentation; Lanjia Saora field documentation 2019). Excluded as insufficiently individuated in verifiable scholarship: the bare-name sonums Karuni-sum, Rude-sum, Lurni-sum, Sandi-sum and Janangla-sum, which appear in SCSTRTI/INTACH/OSTM lists without functional description; Gasadasum, because the guardian posts are called 'Gasadasum or Kitungsum' and thus overlap the existing Kittung entry; Vitebsky's remaining death-Experience classes (Leopard, Convulsion, Smallpox), whose native names could not be verified from accessible sources; and Biswabasu/Jara of the Jagannath origin legend, which belongs to Odia temple tradition rather than Sora religion. Elwin (1955) catalogues 174 Saora gods, but the full text (archive.org copies) proved inaccessible for verbatim confirmation, so only figures corroborated across independently accessible sources were authored.
