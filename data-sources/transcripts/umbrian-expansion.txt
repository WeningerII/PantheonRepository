================================================================================
SOURCING NOTE — what was consulted, and how
================================================================================

This session has no direct outbound web access: the environment's egress policy
blocks attalus.org, en.wikipedia.org, tertullian.org, perseus.tufts.edu,
persee.fr, keytoumbria.com and archive.org alike (verified; all fail at the
proxy). Research for this batch was therefore carried out through server-side
web search, which returns the substance of those pages without fetching them.

Consequently the citations below are scrupulous about provenance:

- Tablet/face/line references for the Iguvine Tablets (TI) are carried forward
  from the Lane 1 Umbrian census, where the previous pass recorded them directly
  from Poultney's English translation as published at attalus.org. They are
  cited here as tablet references, with the census named as the proximate
  record. No page of the printed 1959 commentary is cited as if it had been read.
- Ritual detail (which victim, which gate, which cake or libation) is cited to
  the reference works that state it and that were actually consulted in this
  pass — principally the Oxford Classical Dictionary entry on the tabulae
  Iguvinae and the standard encyclopedic treatments of the Tablets.
- Etymological claims are attributed to the scholar who made them (Poultney on
  Vestiçe/vestiçia, the Proto-Italic *spek-tōr derivation for Speture) and are
  marked as proposals, not settled results.
- Comparative identifications (Fisus Sancius ~ Semo Sancus / Dius Fidius,
  Puemonus ~ Pomona, Spector ~ Culśanś, Cupra ~ Bona Dea) are recorded in prose
  and in `variants`, never as identity relations. The census disposition for
  these candidates is explicit that resemblance is not identity, and the corpus
  precedent (Umbrian Jupiter Grabovius, Roman Jupiter and Etruscan Tinia are
  kept as separate records) is preserved.

Relations authored here are confined to co-invocation attested in the same
ritual passage. Six figures receive no relation at all, because none is
attested; each is recorded in data-sources/verified-solitary.json with its
reason, per the corpus's "every name is verified" invariant.
