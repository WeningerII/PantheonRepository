PANTHEON REGISTRY — FIGURE-AUTHORING TRANSCRIPT
Tradition: Haitian Vodou
Eras (registered, exact): Haitian Vodou traditional religion; continuing
Author pass: Lane 28 found three figures hiding inside other figures' alias
lists and could not create them. The owner has now ruled that a lane may edit an
alias list on a pre-existing record. This adds 4 and edits 3.

================================================================================
THE FIRST LANE IN THIS PROGRAMME THAT DELIBERATELY MUTATES PRE-EXISTING RECORDS
================================================================================

Eight consecutive lanes have reported **zero pre-existing records mutated**, and
that was not a boast — it was a constraint, and lane 28 found the exact case
where the constraint was doing damage.

`vodou_bawon_samdi` carried **"Baron La Croix"** and **"Baron Cimetière"** in its
alias list. The sources give those as two *other* aspects of Bawon with two other
stations: Bawon Samdi rules the **graveyard**, Baron La Croix guards the
**gravestone**, Baron Cimetière guards the **grave**. Three stations, one record.

`vodou_ogou`, primary "Ogou Feray", carried **"Ogou Badagri"** — a distinct lwa
of the Nago nation whom the same source calls **a general in the armies of
Ogou**. A relation between two figures, stored as two names for one.

`vodou_gede_nibo` carried the bare **"Gede"** and **"Ghede"** — the name of the
**largest family of lwa in the religion**, resolving onto a single member of it.

**Lane 27 established that a false absence invites a lane to ship a duplicate.
This is the mirror image and it is worse: an alias that is really a separate
figure makes a real absence invisible.** No sweep would ever report Baron La
Croix missing. No lane would ever be prompted to add him. A lane that tried would
be told he was already there.

**So this lane reports `mutated pre-existing: 3`, and that is the point of it,
not a defect in it.** The count is stated plainly in the audit and the PR rather
than folded into a total.

================================================================================
WHAT WAS EDITED, EXACTLY
================================================================================

Four deletions, all in `data-sources/transcripts/haitian-vodou.txt`, which is the
source the records generate from — **nothing is hand-edited in `app/data.js`**:

    vodou_ogou        alt: removed "Ogou Badagri"
    vodou_bawon_samdi alt: removed "Baron La Croix", "Baron Cimetière"
    vodou_bawon_samdi epithets: removed "Baron Cimetière / Baron of the Cemetery"
    vodou_gede_nibo   alt: removed "Gede", "Ghede"

**No other field of any pre-existing record is touched** — not a name, not a
domain, not a relation, not a source, not a note.

**One of those four is a judgement call and is flagged rather than slipped
through.** The owner authorised editing *alias lists*; the fourth deletion is an
**epithet**. It read "Baron Cimetière", which is a name for a figure this batch
establishes is somebody else, so it is a name-bearing field doing an alias's job
and the same reasoning applies. It is recorded in the census as its own row so
the owner can reverse that one deletion without touching the other three.

**`vodou_bawon_samdi`'s own notes are left exactly as they were**, including the
sentence "He is often paired or conflated with Baron Lacroix and Baron
Cimetière." That sentence is *true* — the conflation is real and the sources
report it — and the edit removes the corpus's participation in it, not the
record of it.

================================================================================
NO RECORD IS CREATED FOR THE GEDE
================================================================================

The bare "Gede" and "Ghede" are removed from Gede Nibo and **nothing is created
in their place**. The Gede are a **family**, and a family is a class of spirit,
which this programme does not ingest — the rejection applied to the nāgas, the
tsen, the gyalpo and the mamo in lane 27 and to the nanchon in lane 28.

The effect is that a sweep for "Gede" now returns **nothing**, where before it
returned Gede Nibo. **That is an improvement and not a regression**: the corpus
holds no figure simply called Gede, and it should say so rather than answer with
one member of the family.

================================================================================
TWO RECORDS THIS PROGRAMME WROTE ARE CORRECTED
================================================================================

Lane 28 wrote two notes that this lane makes false, and both are rewritten:

- **`vodou_ogou_balendjo`** said "NO RECORD IS CREATED FOR OGOU BADAGRIS … the
  census carries him as EXISTING with the reason." He now has one.
- **`vodou_kalfou`** said no edge was drawn to Bawon "because which Baron the
  source means is exactly what the alias list has made unanswerable." It is now
  answerable, and the edge is drawn from the Bawon record.

Both are records this programme authored one lane ago, so correcting them is not
the mutation the header is about. **Leaving a corpus that contradicts itself
would have been the worse outcome.**
