In the myth she comes with other cassowaries to bathe, shedding her feathered skin to take the form of a woman; a man watching in hiding steals the skin and so keeps her as his wife. She bears him many children of alternating sex, from whom the various peoples and languages of the earth descend, the Arapesh among them holding a place of mythic seniority.
