PANTHEON REGISTRY — FIGURE-AUTHORING TRANSCRIPT
Tradition: Greek (the house of Neleus at Pylos; the Trojan-War generation)
Era (registered, exact, used for every figure here): "trojan-war"
Author pass: the missing sons of Nestor of Pylos — Antilochus, Thrasymedes, and
Peisistratus — children of the existing figure greek_nestor.

EXISTING FIGURES (NOT re-authored; referenced by id):
  - greek_nestor   (Nestor, Gerenian horseman of Pylos; type=demigod, era=trojan-war,
                    parentIds greek_neleus + greek_apollod_chloris). His prose notes
                    already name "Antilochus and Thrasymedes (Trojan-War contingent) and
                    Peisistratus" as his sons, so these three close an explicit gap.

================================================================================
RESEARCH SUMMARY AND AUTHORING DECISION
================================================================================

PRESENCE CHECK (STEP 1). Grepped app/data.js and data-sources/transcripts/ for each child
under both the bare name and the greek_<name> id (Antilochus/greek_antilochus,
Thrasymedes/greek_thrasymedes, Peisistratus/Pisistratus/greek_peisistratus). NONE exists as
an authored figure. The only hits are (a) prose mentions inside greek_nestor and
greek_memnon ("Killed Antilochus son of Nestor at Troy"), and (b) an unrelated Pausanias
citation to a sculptor named Thrasymedes. All three sons are therefore genuinely absent and
are authored below.

SECOND PARENT (the mother). Nestor's wife is named in two strands:
  • Homer, Odyssey 3.451–452 — Eurydice, eldest daughter of Clymenus.
  • Apollodorus, Library 1.9.9 — Anaxibia, daughter of Cratieus, who bore Nestor the
    daughters Pisidice and Polycaste and the sons Perseus, Stratichus, Aretus, Echephron,
    Peisistratus, Antilochus, and Thrasymedes.
Neither mother is registered in the corpus (the existing greek_anaxibia_atreid is Agamemnon's
sister, and greek_anaxibia_bias is Pelias's wife — neither is Nestor's wife / a daughter of
Cratieus, and no Eurydice-daughter-of-Clymenus figure exists). The mother is accordingly an
UNREGISTERED second parent: she is recorded in parentRoles only via the registered father and
is named in prose, and for the descent-tier computation she counts as mortal(0).

TIER / TYPE (anti-drift, by descent). Nestor = demigod = 0.5 (per his registered type and the
cluster brief); unregistered mother = mortal = 0. Child divinity fraction = (0.5 + 0) / 2 =
0.25, which falls in the quartigod band (0.1875–0.375). All three sons are therefore authored
as type = "quartigod". (This is also internally consistent with the corpus, which already
records the *grandfather* generation, e.g. Acastus and the Peliades, as quartigods off a
demigod-or-better Poseidon-line parent by a mortal mother.)

ERA. temporal.era = "trojan-war" for all three, exactly as the cluster requires and exactly as
the parent greek_nestor carries; every lifecycle.era is likewise "trojan-war". This keeps each
son no earlier than the father.

LIFECYCLE. Authored ONLY for the son who dies: Antilochus, slain by Memnon at Troy. Thrasymedes
and Peisistratus both survive the war and return to Pylos (no death or transformation is
attested), so per the schema rule they carry no lifecycle array entries.

WIRING.
  - parentIds: ["greek_nestor"] for each (the mandated cluster-parent edge; the mother is
    unregistered so no second id is added — confirmed by grep that no Eurydice-of-Clymenus /
    Anaxibia-of-Cratieus figure exists).
  - parentRoles: { "greek_nestor": "father" }.
  - sibling relations are reciprocated WITHIN this batch among the three brothers (Antilochus
    ↔ Thrasymedes ↔ Peisistratus), each with its own source. No already-registered sibling of
    these three exists in the corpus (Pisidice, Polycaste, Perseus, Stratichus, Aretus,
    Echephron, etc. are unregistered), so there is no out-of-batch one-way sibling edge to add.

SOURCES. Primary citations are the Homeric poems (Iliad / Odyssey, by book.line), Apollodorus's
Library, the Epic-Cycle Aethiopis (Proclus's summary), Pindar's Pythian 6, and Quintus
Smyrnaeus's Posthomerica; secondary scholarship is Gantz, *Early Greek Myth* (1993) and the
standard commentaries (Heubeck et al., *A Commentary on Homer's Odyssey*; Richardson, *The
Iliad: A Commentary* vol. VI on Book 23). No reference is fabricated.

FIGURES CONSIDERED BUT NOT AUTHORED (with reasons):
  • The other Apollodoran sons (Perseus, Stratichus, Aretus, Echephron) and daughters
    (Pisidice, Polycaste) of Nestor — real names in Apollod. 1.9.9, but the cluster brief asks
    for exactly Antilochus, Thrasymedes, and Peisistratus; the rest are deferred to avoid
    padding, and are NOT wired here (so no dangling sibling edges are created).
