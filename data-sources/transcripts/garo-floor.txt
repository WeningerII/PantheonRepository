================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added five well-attested, individuated Garo figures that the corpus was missing, all documented in Playfair's foundational ethnography The Garos (1909) and corroborated by later scholarship (Sangma 1981; Rongmuthu 1967; Marak 2015). Two are primordial actors in the earth-diver creation myth — Machi, the second lesser demiurge who shapes the earth with Nostu-Nopantu at Tatara-Rabuga's command, and Chiching-Barching, the named beetle who dives into the primordial ocean and retrieves the clay for Nostu-Nopantu — each wired to its existing parent-figure. Three are named Songsarek deities of ongoing human and agricultural concern: Kalkame (guardian of human life, brother of the existing Goera), Chorabudi (crop-protector and servant of Tatara-Rabuga), and Rokkime/Mima Kiri Rokkime (mother-goddess of the rice-paddy, invoked at Agalmaka), all placed as lesser deities under the supreme creator. I deliberately excluded generic spirit-classes such as the unnamed crabs and the malevolent mite/nawang soul-devourers, and the thinly-attested epic culture-heroes (Dikki, Bandi), as not sufficiently individuated or documented to meet the bar.
