================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the four individuated clan-myth heroes documented for the Elema beyond the mask-spirit complexes: Oa-Laea (Brown 1977, 1988), Meavea Kivovia (Brown 1975, 1988; Miva, Oral History 1978), and the paired protagonists Marai and Eare (Brown 1988; Kekeo, Oral History 1978), all from the Elema lou-oharo (clan myths) recorded in Toaripi/Orokolo. The proposed sky-being Tairi could not be verified in any accessible scholarship and was excluded; Oa Rove (Marai) was also excluded because the attested tradition for that figure is Roro/Mekeo, not Elema. Web page fetching was blocked in this environment, so verification rests on bibliographic records (Slone's annotated Gulf Province folklore bibliography, ANU Pacific Linguistics records, Institute of PNG Studies publications) surfaced through search; claims were kept minimal accordingly.
