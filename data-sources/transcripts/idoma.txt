Frequently described as the enforcement officers of Owoicho, the Alekwu uphold the moral order: they guard marital fidelity, truthfulness, respect for property and life, blessing those who keep the community's norms and afflicting those who break them, so that ritual confession and appeasement are needed to restore balance.
