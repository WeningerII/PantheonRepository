REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added four individuated figures each carrying at least two real citations: Bisagit, the smallpox spirit who supplies the primordial earth in Evans's Tempasuk/Tuaran creation narrative (Evans 1913 JRAI 43; Evans 1953; Encyclopedia of World Cultures vol. 5, 1993); Monsopiad, the headhunter culture-hero of Kuai, Penampang (Phelan 2001; Gingging, Cultural Analysis 6, 2007; Syarifuddin, Geografia 12(5), 2016); Aki Nabalu, the ancestral grandfather-guardian identified with Mount Kinabalu and propitiated in the monolob rite (Evans 1923; Rutter 1929; Bidder, MySE 10(2), 2023); and Gayo Nakan, the giant 'Big Eater' king whose self-burial is an origin legend of Kinabalu (Bidder, Kibat & Saien 2014; Ali/Komuhakan 2021). Ponompulan, whom KDCA cosmology individuates as Kinoingan's rebellious son cast down to Kolungkud, could not be authored because that name is currently registered as an alias of kadazan_dusun_huminodun (his sister Ponompuan); maintainers may wish to review that alias assignment. Munsumundok/Umunsumundu and Minamangun/Kinharingan were treated as sub-group variants of the existing Suminundu and Kinoingan, not new figures. The Nunuk Ragang ancestor cluster (Aki Nunuk Ragang, Aki Rungsud, Aki Longuvai, Aki Turumpok) was excluded as attested only in community blogs and oral historiography rather than citable scholarship, and spirit classes (rogon, divato, tombiruo, miontong) were excluded as non-individuated.
