================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added six well-attested, individuated members of the T'boli creation pantheon that were missing from the corpus, all drawn from the same creation cycle as the existing figures: Bong Libun (daughter of the supreme couple, wife and slayer of Sfedat, keeper of the primordial land), Hyu We (sister-wife of D'wata and mother of the nature-gods who moulds the first humans from clay), and four of D'wata and Hyu We's children who each hold a distinct domain — Ginton the smith-god of Hulon volcano, Fun Bulol lord of the mountains, Tdolok god of death, and Lmugot Mangay god of life. Each is genealogically wired into the existing pantheon via parentIds pointing at Kadaw La Sambad, Bulon La Mogoaw, D'wata and the new Hyu We, plus directed consort/slayer/mother relations to existing figures. I deliberately stopped short of the remaining named children (Litik, Blanga, Teme Lus and Sedek We) and the omen-bird Muhen to keep the entries to the most robustly individuated cases; those thinner name-plus-domain figures could be added later but risk padding. Attestation rests on Casal's ethnography T'boli Art in its Socio-Cultural Context (1978), Jocano's Outline of Philippine Mythology (1969) and the CCP Encyclopedia of Philippine Art (1994).
