================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added six well-attested, individuated figures from the Bugis creation epic Sureq Galigo (La Galigo) who were missing from the corpus and who wire directly into the existing five: the Underworld sovereign couple Guru ri Selleng and Sinaung Toja (parents of the existing We Nyiliq Timoq); Batara Lattuq / La Tiuleng, second ruler of Luwuq and son of the existing Batara Guru and We Nyiliq Timoq; We Tenriabeng, the twin sister of the existing Sawerigading; the princess I We Cudai of Cina, Sawerigading's wife; and their son I La Galigo, the epic's eponym. Each is documented across standard scholarship — Pelras's The Bugis (1996), Koolhof's La Galigo encyclopaedia study (1999), Kern's manuscript catalogue (1939), Zainal Abidin (1974) and Andaya (1981). I stopped here because remaining named La Galigo personages (e.g. the celestial husband Remmang ri Langi, or the many local dynastic manurung) are either thinly individuated or shade into generic ancestor-classes the brief excludes; the genuinely individuated divine and heroic core is now covered.
