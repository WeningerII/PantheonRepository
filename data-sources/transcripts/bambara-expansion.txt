BAMBARA / BAMANA (Mali, West Africa) — pantheon expansion
=========================================================

Verify-first authoring for the Pantheon Registry. Scope: add WELL-ATTESTED Bambara
figures that are NOT already in the corpus and that are genuinely DISTINCT named
figures (no duplicates of the four already registered: bambara_faro, bambara_pemba,
bambara_mousso_koroni, bambara_ndomadyiri).

ROSTER DECISIONS (web-verified June 2026):
  AUTHOR  bambara_yo      — Yo, the primordial creative sound/spirit; the void's first
                             point, from which the cosmos and the creator-figures issue.
                             Distinct from Pemba (Yo PRECEDES and BEGETS Pemba).
                             Attested: Harold Scheub, "A Dictionary of African Mythology"
                             (Oxford Univ. Press, 2000), s.v. "Yo, the Creative Spirit
                             (Bambara/Mali)".
  AUTHOR  bambara_teliko  — Teliko, genie/spirit of the air and wind, master of breath;
                             conceives the aquatic twins who are the ancestors of the
                             fishermen (the first men); works with Faro to order the
                             world and is sometimes his antagonist. Begotten by Yo.
                             Attested: Scheub, "A Dictionary of African Mythology" (2000);
                             Dieterlen, "Essai sur la religion bambara" (Paris, 1951).

  REJECT  Bemba / Ngala   — NOT authored: a duplicate risk. In the dominant scholarly
                             tradition (Dieterlen 1951; Zahan, "The Bambara", 1974) the
                             supreme creator is Bemba = Ngala = Pemba (one deity, the head
                             of the fourfold quaternity). The corpus has ALREADY committed
                             this identification: bambara_pemba.name.alt already contains
                             "Bemba", "Ngala", "Pembélé", "Koni". A separate Bemba/Ngala
                             figure would duplicate bambara_pemba.
  REJECT  Nyale           — NOT authored: a duplicate. Nyale is a name of Muso Koroni and
                             is ALREADY listed in bambara_mousso_koroni.name.alt.
  REJECT  Gla / Mangala   — NOT authored: this belongs to the broader MANDE (Keita/Dogon-
                             influenced) "eight seeds" creation narrative, not the
                             specifically Bambara/Bamana roster, and overlaps Pemba's
                             earth-creating role.
  NOT AUTHORED (unnamed)  — Teliko's "aquatic twins, ancestors of the fishermen": attested
                             only as an unnamed collective pair, with no individual names in
                             the sources; recorded in Teliko's notes rather than as figures.

KINSHIP NOTES:
  - bambara_yo "creator of" -> bambara_faro and bambara_pemba are edges to EXISTING corpus
    figures (non-symmetric; validator only checks resolution). Reported below for the
    record. The existing corpus already roots Faro/Pemba differently (Faro born of Bemba's
    breath; Pemba self-created), reflecting the genuine multi-version Bambara cosmogony, so
    these are added as relations, NOT by overwriting parentIds.
  - bambara_teliko "enemy" -> bambara_faro is SYMMETRIC -> RECIPROCAL-NEEDED on bambara_faro.
  - bambara_teliko parentIds = [bambara_yo]; bambara_yo "creator of" -> bambara_teliko is
    fully internal and mirrored within this batch.
