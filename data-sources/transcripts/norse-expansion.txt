================================================================================
THE GAP: THE REGISTRY HELD SIGURD AND NOT THE DRAGON
================================================================================

A 51-name check across the WHOLE registry returned 11 hits and 40 absences.
Eight of the eleven were deliberate controls. The other three are false: "Sol"
matched `roman_sol` and `roman_mithraic_mysteries_sol`, "Mani" matched
`nuer_mani`, and "Nerthus" matched `continental_germanic_nerthus` — a Germanic
goddess under a different key, not the Norse figure.

The headline absence is the one this programme has now found in eight
traditions running. **The registry held Sigurd — and Fáfnir was not in it.**
Nor Regin, who forged the sword and set him on; nor Ótr, whose skin had to be
covered in gold; nor Hreiðmarr, who demanded the ransom; nor Andvari, whose
ring carried the curse that kills everyone in the Völsung cycle the corpus
already holds in full. Sigurd, Brynhildr, Gudrún, Sigmund, Signý, Sinfjötli,
Svanhild, Jǫrmunrekkr and a dozen more were here; the gold they all die over
was not.

Beyond that:

- **Mímir and Kvasir**, the two beings whose deaths are the source of Odin's
  wisdom and of poetry itself, in a registry holding Odin and Bragi.
- **Hœnir**, one of the three gods who make the first humans and one of the two
  hostages of the Æsir-Vanir war.
- **The whole sky.** No Sól, no Máni, no Nótt, no Dagr, and neither of the two
  wolves that chase the sun and the moon and catch them at Ragnarök.
- **Níðhöggr and Ratatoskr**, the dragon at the root of the world tree and the
  squirrel that carries insults up and down it.
- **Every jötunn Thor is famous for fighting**: Hrungnir, Þrymr, Útgarða-Loki,
  Geirröðr, Hymir. The registry held Þjazi and Angrboða and none of the rest.
- **Sigyn**, who holds the bowl over Loki's face, in a registry holding Loki
  and the sons whose entrails bind him.
- **Brokkr and Eitri**, who made Mjölnir.
- **Völundr**, the smith of the Völundarkviða.
- **Gullveig**, who is speared and burned three times and is, on the common
  reading, the cause of the Æsir-Vanir war.

proxy — so research went through server-side web search, and the citations
name the reference treatments consulted and, where the treatment quotes them,
the Eddas. Where the sources disagree — Sindri against Eitri for the name of
Brokkr's brother, or the identity of Gullveig — the record carries the
disagreement.

================================================================================
AUTHORED FIGURES — II. THE WAR, THE HEAD, AND THE MEAD
================================================================================

The Æsir-Vanir war ends in a hostage exchange and a truce sealed by spitting
into a vat, and both produce a being whose death is the source of something
the corpus already holds. Mímir is beheaded by the Vanir and his head goes on
advising Odin. Kvasir is made from the spittle, murdered by two dwarfs, and
brewed into the mead that makes poets.

The registry held Odin, who consults the head, and Bragi, who is the god of
the poetry the mead makes, and neither Mímir nor Kvasir.

AUTHORED FIGURES — III. THE SKY AND THE TREE
================================================================================

The Norse sky is a chase. A woman drives the sun, her brother drives the moon,
and two wolves run behind them and will catch them at Ragnarök. Night comes
first and Day is her son, each with a named horse. At the world tree a dragon
chews the root and a squirrel runs up and down carrying the insults between him
and the eagle at the top, which is why the tree is always dying and always
growing.

The registry had none of it.
