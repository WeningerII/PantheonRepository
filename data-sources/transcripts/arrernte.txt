With his stone knife, the banga, he cut them apart, slit their eyes, opened their ears, mouths and noses, and divided their fingers and toes. He then gave the new people the implements of culture — knife, spear, shield, fire, boomerang and the sacred tjurunga — circumcised the men, and instituted the marriage system and the first ceremonies, completing the making of humankind. He is the Western Aranda counterpart of the ungambikula of Spencer and Gillen's eastern account, and his myth ties the origin of circumcision and of ceremonial law directly to a Dreaming ancestor."
