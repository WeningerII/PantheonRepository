================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the six individuated figures of the Bemba founding-migration charter still missing from the corpus: King Mukulumpe of Kola, the sky-born ancestress Mumbi Mukasa, the royal siblings Nkole, Katongo and Chilufya Mulenga, and the diviner-guide Luchele Ng'anga. All are attested in Roberts, A History of the Bemba (1973), Richards (African Political Systems 1940; Chisungu 1956; 'Keeping the King Divine' 1968) and Hinfelaar (1994), and are wired to the existing Chitimukulu and Lesa via directed relations and parentage. Half-brothers Kapasa, Chimba and Kazembe appear in the charter but were left out as thinner, partly Lunda-affiliated figures; the named record beyond this set is essentially exhausted.
