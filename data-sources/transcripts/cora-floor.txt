REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added one figure, the Cora maize goddess ('la muchacha del maíz'), individuated in Preuss's 1912 Religion der Cora-Indianer and in successor scholarship (Guzmán 2002; Valdovinos 2008; Benciolini, Trace 73, 2018, on her ritual embodiment by the Malinches at Pachitas). Everything else the sources name resolves to figures already in the corpus: Sautari, Tahás and the Evening-Star counterpart are registered aliases/aspects of Hatsikan; 'Tatí/Our Mother', 'Grandmother Moon' and the Pacific rain-sender described in Jáuregui's Encyclopedia of World Cultures entry correspond to Tetewan; and 'Grandfather Fire' folds into Tayau in that same entry ('the Sun, and Fire'), with no independent Cora native name recoverable. Ritual classes such as the Malinches and unnamed ancestor collectives were excluded as non-individuated. Sources checked: Preuss 1912; Neurath (UNAM chapter and Artes de México 85, 2007); Benciolini 2018; Guzmán 2002; Coyle 2001; Jáuregui's Cora entry (Encyclopedia of World Cultures, via everyculture.com); Wikipedia/es-Wikipedia overviews for cross-checking aliases.
