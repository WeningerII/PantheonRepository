REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added four individuated figures that scholarship documents beyond the existing set. Three form the well-attested star-race charter myth: Lōktañūr (Capella), the culture-heroine who invents the sail; her youngest son Jebro (the Pleiades), who wins the founding canoe race to become the model paramount chief; and her eldest son Tūmur (Antares), the greedy rival who loses, each recorded by Erdland (1914), Tobin (2002), Genz (2018) and others and wired to one another by parentage and sibling/rival relations. The fourth, Jemeliwut, is the named brother-companion of the existing trickster Letao and son of the existing figure Wullep, attested in Downing & Spennemann (1992) and Kelin (2003) and wired in via parentage to marshallese_wulleb. I deliberately excluded the remaining, non-individuated star-sons of Lōktañūr, who survive only as star identifications without distinct personhood, to avoid padding.
