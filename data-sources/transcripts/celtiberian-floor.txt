================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the two remaining securely individuated, epigraphically attested Celtiberian deities: Airon, the chthonic god of wells attested at the Fuente Redonda sanctuary near Uclés/Segobriga (Lorrio & Sánchez de Prado 2002; Marco Simón 2005), and Dercetius, the mountain god of the Sierra de la Demanda attested on a Roman altar and in Braulio of Zaragoza's Vita Sancti Aemiliani. Other Hispano-Celtic names were deliberately excluded: Epona and the Matres are Gaulish or collective, Ataecina/Bandua/Reue/Nabia belong to the Lusitanian-Vettonian corpus, Vurovius is Autrigonian rather than strictly Celtiberian, and the Peñalba de Villastar readings 'Eniorosis'/'Tiato' are too disputed to author as figures. Beyond these, scholarship (Olivares Pedreño 2002; Marco Simón 2005) documents no further securely individuated Celtiberian theonyms.
