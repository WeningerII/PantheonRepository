ZULU (Southern Africa) pantheon expansion — verify-first authoring transcript.

Scope: add well-attested Zulu/Nguni figures NOT already in the corpus.
Already present (NOT re-authored): zulu_unkulunkulu, zulu_umvelinqangi, zulu_nomkhubulwane.

Candidate adjudication (verified via WebSearch; WORK-level citations only, no page numbers):
  - uMamlambo  → AUTHOR. Distinct female river-divinity / snake of fortune of the Zulu &
      other Nguni peoples; "Mother of the River" (umamlambo). Shapeshifting river-snake
      (serpent with a horse-like head, or a woman) who bestows wealth/good fortune in
      exchange for sacrifice; also a diviner's familiar acquired through ukuthwala.
      Sources: Felicity Wood, "From rain-bringer to wealth-giver: Changing forms of the
      snake in southern African belief systems," Tydskrif vir Letterkunde 62(3) (2025);
      Berglund, Zulu Thought-Patterns and Symbolism (1976); Wikipedia, Mamlambo.
  - iNkanyamba → AUTHOR. Distinct great storm/tornado serpent of the rivers and waterfalls
      (esp. the pool of Howick Falls on the Umgeni; also the Umkomazi system) of the Zulu
      & Xhosa; "snake in the sky," cause of tornadoes/hail, praised as iNkosi yamanzi
      ("Lord of the Waters"). A rain-serpent of the type documented in Zulu rain symbolism;
      rock-art of equine-headed "rain serpents" is read as antecedent.
      Sources: Berglund, Zulu Thought-Patterns and Symbolism (1976); Wikipedia, Inkanyamba.
  - Mbaba Mwana Waresa → REJECT (duplicate). Sources explicitly equate her with
      Nomkhubulwane/iNkosazana (rain, rainbow, harvest, beer) = zulu_nomkhubulwane.
  - uHlanga / uThlanga → REJECT (not a named figure). The primal reed-bed / marsh of origin
      (a place/origin-concept), from which Unkulunkulu grew and broke off humankind; already
      carried as the term "uhlanga" inside zulu_unkulunkulu. Callaway treats it as "a reed."
  - Tokoloshe / Tikoloshe → REJECT (below tier). A witch's familiar / mischievous water-imp
      of lower folklore (Berglund 1976), not a deity or ancestor; outside the registry's
      deity/ancestor tier.

Both authored figures are kinless Zulu nature-divinities (no attested parentage or symmetric
kin) → flagged SOLITARY. No within-batch symmetric relations; no RECIPROCAL-NEEDED edges.
