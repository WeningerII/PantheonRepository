================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added six well-attested, individuated figures that scholarship documents beyond the existing five. Chử Đồng Tử and his wife Tiên Dung complete the Four Immortals (Tứ bất tử), of which three members were already in the corpus; Thủy Tinh is the divine antagonist of the Sơn Tinh–Thủy Tinh flood myth; Kinh Dương Vương is the Hồng Bàng progenitor wired in as father of the existing Lạc Long Quân; and Mẫu Thượng Ngàn (daughter of the existing Tản Viên) and Mẫu Thoải complete the Đạo Mẫu Four-Palaces mother-goddess triad alongside the already-registered Liễu Hạnh. Each figure is grounded in at least two real scholarly or primary sources (Lĩnh Nam chích quái, Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư, Taylor 1983, Chapuis 1995, Nguyễn Đổng Chi, Ngô Đức Thịnh's Đạo Mẫu Việt Nam, Durand 1959, Fjelstad & Nguyễn 2006, Dumoutier 1907), and new figures are wired into the existing pantheon by parentage or directed relation wherever attested. I deliberately excluded vague spirit-classes and the Mẫu Địa palace (commonly merged with Liễu Hạnh rather than individuated).
