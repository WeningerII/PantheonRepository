Author pass: Lane 1 — the Roman tradition held 66 records, but only about 27 of
             them gods: the rest are the Julio-Claudian dynasty and the Alban
             king-list. This pass adds 44 deities.

================================================================================
WHY THIS BATCH IS LARGE
================================================================================

A corpus-wide name check found 48 significant Roman figures absent from the
WHOLE registry, not merely from the Roman tradition. Among them: Quirinus, the
third god of the Archaic Triad with his own flamen maior; Venus, present only
as an unrelated Pawnee homonym; Roman Apollo, present only in his Greek form;
Cupid; and the entire Roman state cult of deified abstractions — Victoria,
Fides, Concordia, Spes, Salus, Libertas, Pax, Pietas, Virtus, Honos, Felicitas,
Juventas — every one of which had a temple, a dedication date and a festival in
Rome. Rome deified abstract qualities outright and gave them state cult, which
Greek religion did only lightly; a registry of Roman religion without them is
missing a structural feature of the system, not a handful of minor names.

================================================================================
IDENTITY AND SCOPE DECISIONS
================================================================================

- Roman counterparts of Greek gods are kept as their own records, the practice
  this registry already follows for Tinia against Zeus and Jupiter. Venus and
  Apollo are linked to their Greek records with the non-symmetric
  `counterpart-of`, which states the relation without asserting identity.
- Dis Pater and Orcus are ALREADY carried as aliases of `roman_pluto`, and
  Lupercus as an alias of `roman_faunus`. They are dispositioned ALIAS, not
  added again.
- Roman Flora IS added despite `oscan_fluusa` carrying "Flora" among its
  aliases: Fluusa is the Oscan goddess of the Agnone circle, Flora is the
  Roman goddess of the Floralia with her own temple and flamen. Same practice
  that keeps Oscan Kerres and Roman Ceres apart.
- "Camenae" is REJECTED as a collective. The four individual Camenae —
  Carmenta, Egeria, Antevorta, Postvorta — are added as figures.
- Quirinus is linked to `roman_romulus` with `counterpart-of`, not
  `equated-with`. The identification is ancient (Ovid) but secondary: Quirinus
  was very probably an originally independent Sabine god whose altar stood on
  the Quirinal before Rome absorbed the cult. The record says both.
- All SYMMETRIC relations in this batch are between figures authored here, so
  no existing transcript is edited. Relations toward records already in the
  corpus use non-symmetric kinds only.
