================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added six individuated, well-attested Thai-Lao figures missing from the corpus: Khun Borom, progenitor king sent down by (and in most redactions son of) the existing Phaya Thaen (Souneth Phothisane 1996; Stuart-Fox 1998; Archaimbault 1959); the Luang Prabang guardian-ancestor couple Pu Nyoe and Ya Nyoe, wired as spouses and as subordinates of Phaya Thaen (Trankell 1999; Hongsuwan, Journal of Lao Studies 2018); Mae Khongkha, who completes the attested mother-goddess triad alongside existing Thorani and Phosop (Anuman Rajadhon 1956, 1968); Phaya Khankhak, the Toad King of the Bun Bang Fai rain myth (Tossa 1996; Tambiah 1970); and the epic hero Thao Hung/Cheuang (Chamberlain 1989-1990; Stuart-Fox 1998). All attestations and citations were verified by web research during authoring. Spirit-classes such as phi ban village guardians, ta haek field spirits, and mae sue birth spirits were deliberately excluded as non-individuated offices rather than named beings; Kabinlaphrom of the Songkran myth was considered but left out as the next-weakest candidate under the scope cap.
