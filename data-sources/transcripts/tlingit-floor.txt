================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added six well-attested individuated Tlingit figures: the cosmological earth-supporter Old Woman Underneath (Hayicā'nak!u/Agischanak), the two great wealth-bringing beings Gonakadet (Gunakadeit) and Wealth Woman (Tl'anaxéedáḵw/Property Woman), and the three canonical clan heroes Natsilane (Naatsilanéi), Blackskin (Dukt'ootl') and Kaats', each grounded in Swanton (1909), Krause (1956 [1885]), de Laguna (1972), Emmons/de Laguna (1991), McClellan (1963), Dauenhauer and Dauenhauer (1987), Beck (1989), and Sealaska Heritage Institute reference works. None has source-attested genealogy or a permitted directed relation to the existing Raven, Petrel, Thunderbird, or Fog Master entries (Raven's harassment of the earth-supporter is adversarial, for which no directed kind is registered), so all six carry cited solitary entries. Fog Woman was deliberately excluded because the existing tlingit_khanukh entry already claims the alias Fog-over-the-Salmon, and remaining candidates — the unnamed Tide Woman, the collective creek-women and Kushtaka land-otter people, Salmon Boy Aak'wtaatseen, and the borderline-historical Kaax'achgóok — were left out as unnamed, collective classes, or better handled in a future pass.
