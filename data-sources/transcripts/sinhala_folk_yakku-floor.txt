================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added three genuinely individuated, well-attested figures beyond the existing set. Dala Raja (the tusked chief of the twelve gara-yakku, also called Gara Yaka) and his sister-counterpart Giri Devi form the paired gara myth-cycle documented by Wirz (1954), Kapferer (1983) and the Pattini-cult scholarship of Obeyesekere (1984); they are wired to each other as siblings and are the ambivalent pollution-removing demons of the closing gara-yakuma. Oddisa (Oddi Yaka) is the mythic first exorcist and founder of the Suniyama rite, attested by Kapferer (1997), Wirz (1954) and Scott (1994); he stands solitary as an originating culture-figure rather than within the demonic genealogies. I deliberately excluded the individual eighteen Sanni demons (aspects subordinate to the already-registered Maha Kola Sanniya), the Kadavara group and the deviyo-class deities such as Devol, which fall outside the individuated-yaka scope or are spirit-classes rather than named individuals.
