  romance of the Sīrat ʿAntar. Named in the prose of arabian_antara.

  by the Banū Asad launches the poet's vengeance-quest. Named in the prose of arabian_imru_al_qais.
- arabian_adi_ibn_hatim: ʿAdī ibn Ḥātim al-Ṭāʾī, son of Ḥātim al-Ṭāʾī, tribal chief of Ṭayyiʾ who
