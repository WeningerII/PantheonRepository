Pantheon Registry — Mordvin (Erzya/Moksha) addition

Tradition: Mordvin (Volga-Finnic; Erzya and Moksha). Era: mythic (the only era registered for this tradition).

This batch completes the CORE of the Mordvin pantheon as it is organized in the scholarship: a dense system of -ava ("mother") and -atya ("father") spirit-owners — more precisely azor-ava / azor-atya, "master-mother" / "master-father" (Mordvin azor "master, ruler") — each owning a domain of the world. The existing registry already holds Ineshkipaz/Nishke-pas (supreme sky-god), Ange-Patyai (mother of the gods), Norov-ava (field/grain mother) and Vede-ava (water-mother). The well-attested figures still missing are the other great Ava mother-goddesses of the elements and the homestead, plus the Sun.

What I added (6 figures), and what I deliberately did NOT add:

1. mordvin_virava — Vir-ava (Viren azor-ava), the FOREST-MOTHER: ruler of the forest's beasts, birds, trees and berries; a fertility/midwife figure in some texts. Paired with a male Vir-atya (forest-father). Firmly attested.
2. mordvin_varmava — Varma-ava (Varmava), the WIND-MOTHER: owner of the winds and weather; notably a mediator between the living and the dead. The Erzya egg-cosmology also names a male wind-god Varma-pas / Varmanpaz; I treat the mother-figure here and note the god in prose. Firmly attested.
3. mordvin_tolava — Tol-ava (Tolen azor-ava), the FIRE-MOTHER: the female owner of fire and the hearth-flame. Attested as one of the standard trio of elemental "mothers" (forest/water/fire) in Holmberg/Harva and in the 19th-c. Folk-Lore Journal ethnography.
4. mordvin_mastorava — Mastor-ava (Mastoron azor-ava), the EARTH-MOTHER (mastor "earth" + ava): the goddess of the soil who grants good harvests and good health to the tillers. Distinct from the male earth/underworld figures Mastoron-kirdi and Mastorpaz (whom the corpus already references under Norov-ava). Firmly attested; also the title-figure of A. M. Sharonov's 1994 Erzya epic Mastorava.
5. mordvin_yurtava — Yurt-ava (Yurt azor-ava; Moksha Yurkhtava), the HOMESTEAD/HOUSE-MOTHER: guardian-mother of the dwelling, the yard and its property, invoked in Erzya marriage prayers ("O mother, Yurt azor ava") alongside the Home-Mother Kud-ava. Firmly attested.
6. mordvin_chipaz — Chipaz / Chi-pas, the SUN: the sun-god of the Erzya egg-cosmology, father of Nishkepaz (= the existing Ineshkipaz), of the earth-god Mastoron-kirdi and of the wind-god Varmanpaz; with the grain-mother Norov-ava the parent of the underworld-god Mastorpaz; molder of humankind from clay. The one luminary firmly attested as a personified deity. I wire Chipaz as PARENT of the existing mordvin_ineshkipaz and as co-parent (lover) with the existing mordvin_norovava — both relations cited.

DELIBERATELY OMITTED — the Moon (Kovpaz / Kov / Kovava). The sources are explicit that the Mordvins "prayed to the sun and moon, but always regarded them as creations of God" — the moon is venerated but is NOT given the status of a distinct, named, anthropomorphic deity in the verifiable scholarship (unlike the Sun/Chipaz, who is a cosmogonic actor). Per the no-padding / firmly-attested rule, no Moon figure is authored. (The supreme sky-god Ineshkipaz already absorbs sun/sky/fire-of-heaven functions in the Melnikov line, which further argues against a free-standing Moon deity.)

The five Ava mother-goddesses are treated as INDEPENDENT domain-powers: the verifiable sources do not make Ange-Patyai or Chipaz the parent of the elemental/household -ava mothers (those genealogies that do exist run to the -paz/-pas gods: Chipaz fathers Nishkepaz, Mastoron-kirdi and Varmanpaz). The five Ava are therefore flagged SOLITARY with that cited reason. Only Chipaz carries kin.

Cross-tradition / cross-system note: there are two partly-rival Mordvin cosmologies in the literature — the Erzya "egg" cosmogony (Sarv 2001; Hamari & Ajanki 2022), in which the Sun Chipaz fathers the high gods, and Melnikov-Pechersky's 19th-c. great-goddess scheme, in which Ange-Patyai is mother of all and Nishke-pas is himself the sun. I follow the reading the existing corpus already adopted (Ineshkipaz "born of the sun-god Chipaz"), and flag the tension in the notes.
