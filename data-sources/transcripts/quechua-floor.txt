REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added four well-attested figures of the post-conquest southern Peruvian Quechua/Q'ero folk tradition: Roal, the Q'ero creator spirit and chief of the apus (Ó. Núñez del Prado 1957; J. Núñez del Prado 1974); Qollari, the primordial woman created with Inkarrí (Ossio 1973; Ortiz Rescaniere 1973), wired to the existing inkarri figure as consort; and the two individuated paramount mountain lords, Apu Ausangate (Randall 1982; Allen 1988; Salas Carreño 2019) and Apu Salkantay (Reinhard 2007; Bernbaum 1990), typed as numina and subordinated to Roal per the recorded apu hierarchy. I deliberately excluded Pachamama, Viracocha and other imperial-cult figures (Inca corpus), generic spirit classes (ñawpa machu, machula aulanchis, generic apus), and the Q'ero local apu Wamanlipa, for which I could not confirm two scholarly citations in this session.
