Through these masks Dwo continually restores the precarious cosmic balance that Wuro established and that human activity disturbs. For the Bobo it is always Wuro who acts behind the mask, but it is the real presence of Dwo that gives the mask its sacred character."

Together with his brothers Dwo, the god of renewal embodied in the masks, and Soxo, the god of the bush, Kwere is one of the divine emanations through whom the withdrawn high god Wuro is present in the ordered world. As the celestial and storm power of the Bobo pantheon he governs the lightning; the Bobo treat death by lightning as a manifestation of his agency, and in the north such deaths, like deaths by fire in the mask, are marked by the participation of particular leaf masks in the funeral.
