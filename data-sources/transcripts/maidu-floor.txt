================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added three individuated figures well attested in the Dixon corpus and later ethnography. Kuksu (the first man, 'Maidu Adam,' molded from reddish earth by Earth-Initiate) and Morning-Star Woman (the first woman, his wife) are the ancestral human pair of the Maidu creation and are wired to the existing Earth-Initiate as their maker and to each other as spouses; Kuksu additionally carries the well-documented 'Big Head' ceremonial identity of the Kuksu cult (Dixon 1902; Kroeber 1925; Loeb 1933). Rattlesnake is the specific agent of the first death in the same creation cycle (Dixon 1902; Shipley 1991) and is listed as solitary because the sources give him a pivotal role but no kin. I stopped at three because the remaining Maidu narrative population (Bear, Deer, Loon, Mountain Lion, Muskrat, etc.) consists of generic animal-people classes rather than further named, individuated divine or ancestral beings beyond the existing set.
