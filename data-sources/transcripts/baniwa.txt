After Kuwai teaches the first initiation and is thrust into a great fire, an enormous paxiúba palm rises from his ashes; from it Nhiãperikuli fashions the first flutes and trumpets, which are Kuwai's transfigured body and are sounded at the initiation rites (Kwaipan) whose taboos his songs enforce.
