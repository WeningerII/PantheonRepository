================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added five individuated dema attested in the primary ethnographies of Marind-anim religion (Wirz 1922-1925; van Baal 1966) and comparative scholarship (Knauft 1993): Uaba, the sun-and-fire dema, and his partner Ualiwamb (Kanis-iwag), from whose union the first fire, cassowary and stork arose in the Mayo cult cycle; Aramemb, the healer-dema and phratry eponym tied to the cassowary and the origin of death; Yorma, the sea-dema; and Dawi, the cassowary-dema of the origin-of-fire and Habee-island episode. Uaba and Ualiwamb are wired to each other as spouses (both new figures); Uaba's attested fraternal link to the existing figure Sosom and membership in the Aramemb phratry are recorded in prose since sibling ties cannot be directed onto an existing id. I stopped at these five because the remaining named dema in the sources (e.g. Desse and Dawena, the depth pair) are only thinly individuated, and padding with them would lower the evidential quality of the set.
