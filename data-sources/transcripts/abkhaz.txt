Abkhaz pantheon — core figures still missing from the registry.

The five Abkhaz figures already in the corpus (abkhaz_antswa the supreme creator, abkhaz_afy the
thunderer, abkhaz_aitar the sevenfold livestock-god, abkhaz_azhwepshaa the forest-and-game lord, and
abkhaz_shashwy the smith-god) cover the male/celestial and economic core. What is still missing is the
attested female-and-agrarian tier and the great oracular mountain-shrine personage. I researched each
candidate by web search and cross-checked the standard scholarship: V. A. Chirikba, *Between Christianity
and Islam: Heathen Heritage in the Caucasus* (Brill, 2015); George Hewitt (ed.), *The Abkhazians: A
Handbook* (Curzon, 1999); Sh. D. Inal-ipa, *The Abkhazians* / Abkhaz ethnography; the *Mythological
Encyclopedia* (Мифы народов мира / Мифологическая энциклопедия) article on Caucasian-Iberian mythology;
and the modern field study R. Zelnitskaya, "The Sanctuary of Dydrypsh-nykha Yesterday and Today"
(Kunstkamera journal, 2023).

Three figures are firmly attested and authored below:
  • abkhaz_dzhadzha — Dzhadzha (Џьаџьа), goddess of agriculture, the earth and fertility.
  • abkhaz_erysh   — Erysh (Ерыш), goddess of weaving and women's craft (and of milking).
  • abkhaz_dydrypsh — Dydrypsh (Дыдрыҧшь), the apaimbar (earthly prophet/representative of Antswa) who
                      dwells on the sacred Dydrypsh mountain, chief oracular oath-shrine of Abkhazia.

DELIBERATELY NOT AUTHORED (verification failed the "firmly-attested / do not pad" bar):
  • Saunau — no reliable attestation found in any source after repeated targeted searches; the smith-craft
    sphere is consistently assigned to Shashwy (already in the corpus, abkhaz_shashwy). Excluded.
  • Atsany (Аҵаны) — attested (Inal-ipa) but as a collective dwarf-FOLK of the Abkhaz Nart epic, not a
    single named deity/figure; the only named individual in that cycle (the Nart Tsvitsv) is a hero born
    among them, not himself an Atsan deity. Per the "only if a named figure" instruction, omitted.
  • (Anana-Gunda, goddess of bees and marriage, and Dzyzlan, water-goddess, surfaced in the listings but
    fall outside the assigned candidate set, so they are left for a later batch.)

RECIPROCAL-NEEDED: (none) — the only relation toward an existing corpus figure, abkhaz_dydrypsh subordinate-to abkhaz_antswa, is non-symmetric (not in the reciprocal set), so no reciprocal edge is required on abkhaz_antswa.
