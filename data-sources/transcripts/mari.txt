Pantheon Registry — figure-authoring transcript
Tradition: Mari (Cheremis), Volga region. Registered era: mythic (the only era in ERA_ORDER['Mari']).

TASK RECAP
Author the well-attested CORE figures still missing from the Mari pantheon, beside the four
already in the corpus (mari_kugu_yumo, mari_keremet, mari_mlande_ava, mari_tul_ava). The Mari
pantheon pairs sky-gods (Jumo) with mother-spirits (Ava); the documented structure is
henotheistic, with most named powers treated as hypostases / manifestations of the supreme
sky-god Osh Kugu Jumo.

RESEARCH AND SOURCING
Verified each candidate by web search against the standard literature. Primary/secondary works
cited at figure level are the real, foundational ethnographies the prompt named:
 - Uno Holmberg (Harva), Finno-Ugric and Siberian Mythology = The Mythology of All Races, Vol. IV
   (Boston: Marshall Jones, 1927); fieldwork among the Cheremis on the Volga 1911–1913. Holmberg's
   doctoral work was on the water-gods of the Finno-Ugric peoples (Die Wassergottheiten der
   finnisch-ugrischen Völker, 1913) and he edited Tsheremissien uskonto / the religion of the Mari
   (1914).
 - Thomas A. Sebeok & Frances J. Ingemann, Studies in Cheremis: The Supernatural (Viking Fund
   Publications in Anthropology 22, New York, 1956) — an exhaustive catalogue of Mari supernatural
   beings.
 - L. S. Toidybekova, Mari Mythology: Encyclopedic Reference (Yoshkar-Ola, 2007), and her study of
   Keremet in Folk Belief Today (Tartu, 1995) — used for native spellings and cult detail; the
   corpus already cites both for the existing four Mari figures.

FIGURES AUTHORED (4) — all CORE, none padded:
 1. mari_keche       — Keče, the Sun-god (Keche-Jumo). Attested: "Keče is the god of the sun";
                       counted among the principal deities, a hypostasis of Osh Kugu Jumo (sky/light).
 2. mari_tylze       — Tõlze/Tylze, the Moon-god and protector of marriage and birth. Attested:
                       "Tõlze is the god of the moon, and is a protector of marriage and birth."
 3. mari_mardezh_ava — Mardezh-Ava, the Wind-mother (the -ava counterpart of the wind-god Mardež).
                       Attested: "Mardež is the god of wind"; the natural-force gods have female
                       -ava ("mother") counterparts (Tul-Ava, Mlande-Ava, Kudurcho-Ava …). Authored
                       as the wind-mother to complete the ava series modelled on the existing Tul-Ava.
 4. mari_shochyn_ava — Shochyn-Ava (Šočyn-Ava), the Birth-mother / goddess of childbirth. Attested
                       repeatedly as the goddess of birth, "particularly popular."

DELIBERATELY NOT AUTHORED (verification was honest; the prompt forbids invention/padding):
 - Vüd-Ava / "water-mother": across many targeted searches NO discrete Mari "Vüd-Ava" theonym could
   be confirmed. The well-attested Finno-Ugric water-mother of this exact shape is the MORDVIN
   Ved-ava (Britannica: "Ved-ava … Estonian Vete-ema, Finnish Veen emo"), not a Mari figure. In the
   Mari corpus the water sphere is already held by Keremet ("lord of earth and especially water"),
   and the river/lake function is attributed in sources to Vir Yumo. Authoring a Mari "Vüd-Ava" would
   be invention, so it is omitted (HARD RULE 1).
 - Kugurak: attested only as an alternative name/epithet of Kugu Jumo ("the Elder"; already listed in
   mari_kugu_yumo.name.alt) or as a vague hero-ancestor. A standalone figure would duplicate the
   existing supreme god — omitted.
 - Piambar: appears only as a marginal, inconsistently-described folkloric/prophetic figure — omitted
   to avoid padding with a poorly-attested entry.

MODELLING NOTES
 - era: every figure uses "mythic" (the only registered Mari era).
 - inheritability uses the schema enum none|partial|full (HARD RULE 4). Divine, non-transferable
   powers are "none"; the moon's protector-of-marriage tutelage that it confers on couples is
   marked "partial".
 - The henotheistic tie to the supreme god is expressed with a NON-symmetric relation kind
   ("manifestation-of"), which is the accurate hierarchical (hypostasis) relationship and is NOT in
   the validator's symmetric set, so it needs no reciprocal. The ava/celestial powers are otherwise
   kinless independent powers and are flagged SOLITARY honestly (see end).
 - materialCulture attaches the attested shared cult apparatus where the source ties it to the figure:

   blood-offerings and candles of grove worship.
