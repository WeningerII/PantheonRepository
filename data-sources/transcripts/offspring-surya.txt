PARENT EDGE (directed; no reciprocal required):
  - hindu_yami  child of  hindu_surya   (parentRoles {hindu_surya:"father"})
  - hindu_manu  child of  hindu_surya   (parentRoles {hindu_surya:"father"})
  The mother (Saraṇyū / Saṃjñā) is unregistered → not in parentIds (no resolvable id);
  recorded in notes/etymology instead. Both are children of Sūrya by Saraṇyū in canon.

SIBLING EDGES (symmetric; reciprocated WITHIN this batch):
  - hindu_yami  <->  hindu_manu     full siblings (both Sūrya×Saraṇyū). RECIPROCATED here.
  one-way edge to the already-existing sibling (per spec):
  - hindu_yami  ->  hindu_yama      TWIN sibling (Ṛgveda 10.10, the Yama–Yamī dialogue).
                                    Added one-way on Yamī; the parent agent may add the
                                    reciprocal twin-sibling on hindu_yama if desired.
  - hindu_manu  ->  hindu_yama      sibling (both sons of Vivasvat). Added one-way on Manu.
  (Manu and Yama are brothers in the solar genealogy though not the RV 10.10 twin-pair,
   which is specifically Yama & Yamī; the dyadic "twin" tag is reserved for Yamī↔Yama.)

TYPE BY DESCENT (anti-drift / anti-inversion):
  Fraction = average of parents' divinity fractions; an UNREGISTERED second parent counts
  as mortal(0), sum still /2. Sūrya = deity = 1.0; Saraṇyū unregistered → 0.0.
    → fraction = (1.0 + 0.0) / 2 = 0.5  →  band 0.375–0.75  →  DEMIGOD.
  Both Yamī/Yamunā and Vaivasvata Manu are authored as type "demigod". This is also the
  drift-SAFE choice ("when unsure pick the LOWER tier"): a deity claim (needs >0.75) would
  be auto-reclassified downward, whereas demigod is stable at the computed 0.5. (The
  pre-existing twin hindu_yama is a legacy "deity" entry parented to Sūrya only; the
  computation for the new figures follows the mandated formula and is noted per-figure.)
  Manu is additionally the archetypal FIRST MAN / flood-survivor progenitor; demigod (a
  divine-born mortal-ancestor) fits both the formula and the "first man" reading.

ERA: every figure temporal.era = "vedic"; every lifecycle.era = "vedic" (exact cluster era;
  keeps each child no earlier than the parent and prevents era-drift).

LIFECYCLE (only for figures who die / transform):
  - hindu_yami : transformation stage — descends/manifests as the earthly river Yamunā
                 (deified river-goddess). Stage era "vedic".
  - hindu_manu : he survives the deluge and lives on as progenitor; the registered stage is
                 his survival-of-the-flood and re-peopling of the earth (he is the ancestor,
                 not a god who dies). Stage era "vedic".

SOURCES (all REAL; primary + scholarly secondary):
  Ṛgveda 10.10 (Yama–Yamī dialogue); Ṛgveda 10.17.1–2 (Saraṇyū bears the twins to Vivasvat);
  Ṛgveda 10.14, 10.63, 8.52 (Manu / Yama solar genealogy); Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 1.8.1.1–6
  (Mādhyandina; the fish saves Manu from the flood — the earliest flood account);
  Matsya Purāṇa and Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.24 (Manu & the fish); Bhāgavata / Mārkaṇḍeya /
  Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa (Saṃjñā/Chāyā genealogy, Yamunā & Yama children of Sūrya by Saṃjñā);
  Yamunāṣṭakam (devotional hymn to the river-goddess).
  Secondary: Wendy Doniger, The Rig Veda (Penguin Classics, 1981), "Yama rejects Yami" &
  the Saraṇyū→Saṃjñā discussion; A.A. Macdonell, Vedic Mythology (1897), §§ on Yama, Manu,
  and the Indo-European Twin/Man pair; David L. Haberman, River of Love in an Age of
  Pollution: The Yamuna River of Northern India (Univ. of California Press, 2006).
