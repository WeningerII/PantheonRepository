================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added two genuinely individuated, well-attested figures missing from the existing set. Llao, the chief of the below-world and antagonist of Skell in the Crater Lake origin narrative, is documented by Clark (1953) and by Deur's NPS traditional-use study (2008), which records Klamath descriptions of ongoing battles between Skell and Llao along the caldera rim; he is left solitary because his only defining tie is an enmity with an existing figure that has no directed-relation equivalent. Weasel (Modoc Tcûskai) is the named younger brother and companion of Old Marten (the existing klamath_sqel), attested in Gatschet (1890) and Curtin (1912), and is wired in as subordinate to his elder brother. I deliberately excluded candidates that are spirit-classes rather than individuated persons — the cannibal Thunders (Leméis), the Witsduk snowstorm spirits, and generic animal-people (Eagle, Wolf, Grizzly) — and I did not author the Modoc trickster fox Wus, since it falls within the existing Coyote entry's attested aliases (Was/Wash).
