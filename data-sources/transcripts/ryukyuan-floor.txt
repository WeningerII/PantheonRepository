================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added six figures that scholarship individuates beyond the existing creation-cycle core: the household hearth deity Hinukan (Lebra 1966; Sered 1999); Tentei, the Heavenly Emperor of the royal chronicles who commissions Amamikyu (Chūzan Seikan 1650; Kerr 1958); the Maitreya-derived visiting god of plenty Miruku (Ouwehand 1985; Miyata 1988); Akariyazagama, the Miyako water-of-life bearer in the origin-of-death myth recorded by Nevsky (Nevskii 1971; Jarosz 2015); and the Miyako creator pair Koitsuno and Koitama of Harimizu utaki (Miyakojima kiji shitsugi 1748; Kiyomura 1927). New figures are wired into the pantheon where attested: Akariyazagama serves the sun deity Tedako, and the Miyako pair act under Tentei's command. Deliberately excluded as not genuinely individuated: Tenson (a dynastic eponym covering twenty-five unnamed generations), the masked visiting-deity complexes Pāntu and Akamata-Kuromata (village-variable ritual classes), spirit classes such as kijimuna, and the Nirai-Kanai and obotsu 'great lords', which the sources treat as aspects of the already-registered Kinmamon.
