================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added three genuinely individuated Urartian deities documented beyond the existing five: Selardi, the moon god equated with Mesopotamian Sîn; Tushpuea, the eponymous goddess of the capital Tušpa and consort of the sun god Šiuini; and Huba, consort of the weather god Teišeba and the Urartian form of the Hurrian mother goddess Ḫepat. Each is attested in the Meher Kapısı (Mheri-Dur) pantheon list and treated in the standard scholarship (Piotrovsky 1969, Zimansky 1985, Salvini CTU 2008, Chahin 2001, Wilhelm 1989, Adontz 1946), and I wired Tushpuea and Huba into the corpus as consorts of the existing Shivini and Teisheba. I stopped at three because the remaining dozens of names on the Meher Kapısı stele are, in the consensus of specialists, otherwise unattested and lack any individuating role, consort, or myth, so authoring them would amount to padding rather than documented figures.
