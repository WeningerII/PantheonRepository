================================================================================
RESEARCH SUMMARY AND AUTHORING DECISION
================================================================================

PRIMARY SOURCES. The Amarna royal children are attested archaeologically and
epigraphically rather than mythologically: the Amarna Boundary Stelae and the
royal-family talatat reliefs of Akhetaten (Tell el-Amarna); the Royal Tomb of
Akhenaten (TA26 / Amarna Royal Tomb), whose chamber gamma reliefs depict the death
and mourning of Meketaten; the tomb of Meryre II at Amarna (the Smenkhkare-and-
Meritaten scene); the tomb and treasures of Tutankhamun (KV62, Carter excavation
1922) and his Restoration Stela from Karnak; and the Hittite "Deeds of Suppiluliuma"
(the Dakhamunzu / Zannanza affair). The DNA/forensic study is Zahi Hawass et al.,
"Ancestry and Pathology in King Tutankhamun's Family," JAMA 303.7 (2010): 638–647,
which identified the KV55 mummy as Tutankhamun's father (Akhenaten) and the KV35
"Younger Lady" (KV35YL) as his mother — a full sister of Akhenaten, NOT Nefertiti.

STANDARD MODERN SCHOLARSHIP. Aidan Dodson & Dyan Hilton, The Complete Royal
Families of Ancient Egypt (Thames & Hudson, 2004); Aidan Dodson, Amarna Sunset
(AUC Press, 2009) and Amarna Sunrise (2014); Nicholas Reeves, The Complete
Tutankhamun (Thames & Hudson, 1990) and Akhenaten: Egypt's False Prophet (2001);
Toby Wilkinson, The Rise and Fall of Ancient Egypt (2010); Joyce Tyldesley,
Nefertiti: Egypt's Sun Queen (1998).

TYPE / TIER DECISION (anti-drift). By the descent math, a child of Akhenaten
(quartigod, 0.25) by a mortal-tier mother (Nefertiti, Kiya, or the unnamed KV35YL,
each 0) computes to (0.25 + 0)/2 = 0.125 — the scion band. HOWEVER these are
historical 18th-Dynasty royals, and the cluster instruction is explicit that the
Amarna royal children are to be authored as MORTAL regardless. All four are
therefore typed "mortal" (matching how the corpus already carries Tiye and
Nefertiti — mortal-by-descent New-Kingdom royals — and the historical-human rule).
Each figure's notes record this decision.

WIRING. Parent edges are directed (no reciprocal needed):
  - All four → parentId egyptian_akhenaten (father).
  - Meritaten, Meketaten, Ankhesenamun → also parentId egyptian_nefertiti (mother),
    who is securely their mother (six daughters of Akhenaten and Nefertiti). Both
    parents wired.
  - Tutankhamun → egyptian_akhenaten only. His mother is, per the 2010 DNA study,
    the KV35 "Younger Lady" (a sister of Akhenaten), her personal identity unresolved
    (Kiya and others proposed); she is NOT registered in the corpus and is NOT
    Nefertiti, so no second parent edge is asserted rather than guessing one. The
    point is recorded in his notes.
The four are full/half-siblings of one another and are wired as siblings RECIPROCALLY
WITHIN THIS BATCH (Tutankhamun is the half-brother of the three daughters, and also
the husband of Ankhesenamun — both a sibling and a spouse edge are recorded between
them, each reciprocated). No already-existing corpus figure is among these four, so
no out-of-batch sibling reciprocal is required.

EXISTING FIGURES REFERENCED (not authored here):
  - egyptian_akhenaten  (father; quartigod)
  - egyptian_nefertiti  (mother of the three daughters; mortal)
