================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added five individuated deities well attested beyond the existing set: Indr (storm/vine god, cognate of Vedic Indra, wired as father of Disani in one tradition), Wushum/Shomde (a localized creator-god held equal to or senior to Imra), Sudrem (the Prasun weather-god arising from Imra's breath, wired to Imra as creator/superior), Krumai (the wild-goat goddess of Tirich Mir linked to childbirth), and Arom (the tutelary tribal god of the Kata with his holy stone at Kamdesh). Crucially, Wushum/Shomde and Sudrem are documented as distinct deities rather than regional names of one weather-god, so both were authored separately. Each figure rests on at least two of Robertson (1896), Jettmar (1986), Buddruss and Klimburg's Encyclopaedia Iranica article, Klimburg (2008), and Halfmann's 2022 theonym study. I stopped at five and deliberately excluded thin or class-level entries (e.g. the yush giants, unnamed fairies/Kundrik, ancestral Gandeo, and the poorly individuated Sanju/Sanu) as either non-individuated or insufficiently attested.
