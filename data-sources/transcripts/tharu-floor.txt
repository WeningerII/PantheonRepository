================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added two genuinely individuated Tharu deities beyond the existing set. Saura is securely attested by Christian McDonaugh's ethnography of the Dangaura Tharu as the named eldest son of the goddess Debi (whose active aspect is the corpus figure Maiya), so I wired him to tharu_maiya as mother and to tharu_gurbaba, beneath whom he sits on the household altar. Daharchandi is the paramount goddess of the maruwa than village shrine in western/Dangaura Tharu practice, documented in Krishna Raj Sarbahari's study of Tharu shamanism and consistent with Rajaure's survey of Dang Tharu than-worship; she stands solitary because her only named kin (Draupadi) lies outside the tradition. I deliberately excluded Narayan, Mahadeo and Parbati (unmodified pan-Hindu borrowings), and left aside further than-list names such as Bagheswori, Kotiya and Kuiya Pani, which are more functional place-guardians and rest on a single source, to avoid padding the register with under-attested or non-individuated entries.
