================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added five individuated deities well attested for Sogdiana beyond the existing set: Tish (Tištrya), the rain-and-star god reconstructed as Nana's consort; Wašaγn (Verethragna), the victory god and patron of caravaneers; Miši (Mithra), the solar guardian of covenants; Rašn (Rašnu), the scale-holding judge attested on the Afrasiyab ossuary; and Farn (xᵛarənah), the personified fortune/glory. Each is wired into the pantheon (Tish as consort-of Nana; all five subordinate-to Adbag; Miši and Rašn as the paired divine judges of the Chinvat Bridge). Sources are Grenet's Journal Asiatique 2017 pantheon study and BAI 2010 article, Shenkar 2014, Compareti 2017, Grenet–Marshak 1998, de la Vaissière 2005, Boyce 1975, Panaino and Gnoli in Encyclopaedia Iranica. I stopped here deliberately: further Sogdian theonyms attested in onomastics (e.g. Srosh/Sraosha) are named in the judgment triad but are less individuated in surviving Sogdian material, so adding them would be padding rather than genuine individuation.
