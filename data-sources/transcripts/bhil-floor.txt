REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added five individuated, cited figures missing from the Bhil tradition: Raja Pantha (chief deity and consort of the existing Dev Mogra, per Naik 1956 and the Encyclopedia of World Cultures), Rani Kajal (mother/clan goddess and queen of the Bhilala Gayana creation song, per Baviskar 1995), Pithori Rani (consort of the existing Baba Pithora in the Pithora mural pantheon, per Jain 1984 and Sahapedia), Bhilat Dev (the Narmada-valley cowherd and serpent godling, per Russell and Hira Lal 1916 and Parmar 1972), and Salabai (the goddess of the 1922-23 Devi movement among the Bhils, per Hardiman 1984/1987). New figures were wired to existing corpus deities via directed consort-of relations where attested. Generic classes (Babadev village gods, Karkulia dev crop spirits, gatla ancestor pillars) and Hindu borrowings already individuated elsewhere (Tejaji, Ganesh as Baba Ganeh) were deliberately excluded.
