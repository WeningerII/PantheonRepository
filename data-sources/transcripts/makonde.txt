A single masked figure is a lipiko (plural mapiko), embodied by a costumed dancer wearing a carved wooden helmet-mask tilted back on the head so that he sees out through its open mouth. The performance is bound up with secrecy and gendered exclusion: the mapiko are said to terrify women and the uninitiated, who must not learn the identity beneath the mask.
