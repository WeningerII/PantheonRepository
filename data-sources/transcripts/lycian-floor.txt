REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added four well-attested, individuated figures that the existing set omitted. Natri is the native Lycian god of the Letoon triad (identified with Apollo, son of Eni Mahanahi/Lada and brother of Ertemi), wired to the existing corpus through his mother; Kakasbos is the indigenous club-bearing rider-god of northern Lycia and Pisidia known from more than a hundred votive stelai; and Sarpedon and Glaukos are the two named Lycian heroes of the Iliad, the former a Zeus-born ancestral king who received cult at Xanthos and the latter his Bellerophontid kinsman, linked to each other as joint commanders. Each rests on standard scholarship (Melchert's Lycian dictionary, Bryce, Keen, Delemen, Kirk) plus primary sources. I deliberately excluded vague spirit-classes such as the Eliyãna nymphs and the imported Corinthian Bellerophon, whose primary tradition is not Lycian, so as not to pad the set.
