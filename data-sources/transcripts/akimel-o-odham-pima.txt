  "notes": "Earth Doctor (Jewed Maka) is the primordial creator of Akimel O'odham cosmology, who emerges alone from the original darkness before earth or sky exist. Rubbing the substance of his own breast into a small ball, he fashions the earth, then the sky, sun, moon, and stars, and the first generation of living things. In the flood narratives he is one of three beings, alongside Elder Brother and Coyote, to survive the deluge sealed within a vessel of his own making; disputes over who first emerges from the receding waters establish the seniority that orders the later world. Sources differ on his relationship to Elder Brother, some tellings making the younger god his creation and others an independent rival, and in several versions Earth Doctor, angered by human wickedness, withdraws into the earth and calls up the flood himself. His mythology was recorded chiefly by Frank Russell and, from the Smith-Allison telling, by Donald Bahr."

  "notes": "Elder Brother, Se'ehe (also Siuuhu, and in Piman tellings I'itoi), is the culture hero and law-giver of the Akimel O'odham. Emerging in the world's second age, he shapes humankind, teaches the arts of living, and rids the land of monsters. In the central saga his rule grows tyrannical and he is slain, in most versions by the shaman Buzzard wielding the killing heat of the sun; he lies dead but rises again, journeys to the sun, and returns to lead the people up from beneath the earth to conquer the earlier inhabitants of the great houses whom archaeologists call the Hohokam. His monster-slaying exploits include the destruction of the cannibal witch Ho'ok and of the man-eating Eagle. Sources differ on whether he is Earth Doctor's son, successor, or rival. He is the figure depicted in the widely reproduced Man-in-the-Maze design."
