================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added five individuated Kamilaroi/Euahlayi beings beyond the existing Baiame-Birrahgnooloo-Daramulun core: Bahloo the moon-man, Yhi the sun-woman, Mullian the eaglehawk (Altair), Wahn the crow (Canopus), and Gawarrgay the spirit Emu in the Sky. All are attested by name in K. Langloh Parker's Australian Legendary Tales (1896) and The Euahlayi Tribe (1905) and independently confirmed with community elders in Fuller, Anderson, Norris & Trudgett's 2014 Kamilaroi/Euahlayi astronomy studies. I deliberately excluded literary elaborations (e.g. the expanded Yhi creation myth of later popular retellings) and unnamed spirit classes such as the wunda; the Yhi-Bahloo pursuit and Bahloo's subordination to Baiame are the only relations the sources support.
