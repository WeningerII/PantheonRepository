================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added seven individuated figures from the core Kiwai ethnographic corpus: Adiri, first master of the death-land and father of the existing Dirivo (Landtman 1912, 1917); Sagaru and Meuri, the wife and the slayer of the existing Sido in the first-death cycle (Landtman 1917, quoted verbatim; Lawrence, Gunnar Landtman in Papua, ANU E Press 2010); the wild woman Abere (Landtman 1917, 1927; Knappert 1992); the moon-being Ganumi (Landtman 1917, 1927; Ray 1933 for the word ganumi; Hughes 1959); and the crop-bringers Soido and Pekai (Landtman 1917 indexes both; Mitchell 2023, Archaeology in Oceania, calls Soido the Kiwai bringer of horticulture and fertility). Soido is deliberately distinct from kiwai_sido: Landtman's corpus separates Sido and Soido as different beings with different wives and tale-cycles, though reviewers should note that Torres Strait scholarship partly amalgamates the cognate hero Sida with both. Named but thinly documented figures (the marksman Mesede, the snake-man Maigidubu, place-fathers such as Samariabera) were left out pending fuller verification, and spirit classes (hiwai-abere, oboro, oriogoruho) were excluded as non-individuated. Direct fetching of full texts was blocked in this environment, so attestation was confirmed through search-indexed facsimiles, Google Books index snippets, and the ANU Press monograph on Landtman.
