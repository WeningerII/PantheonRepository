REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added the six further individuated figures that Minahasan scholarship documents beyond the creation quartet: Muntu-untu (chief of the gods, Graafland 1869's 'oppergod' and head of the order in Riedel's Das Toumbuluhsche Pantheon 1894), his wife the rice goddess Lingkan Wene, the brother mountain-lords Pinontoan (Lokon) and Rumengan (Mahawu), the augur Mamarimbing (explicitly recorded in Riedel 1894, namesake of the manguni owl), and the earthquake numen Makawalang beneath Lokon. Each carries two or more verified citations spanning colonial ethnography (Riedel 1894; Graafland 1869), the Indonesian government ethnography Adat Istiadat Daerah Sulawesi Utara (1978), Wenas's standard history (2007), Badan Bahasa and Grasindo folklore editions (Sorey 2016; Gedoan 2018; Pangkerego 1999), and recent journal scholarship (Tulus 2021; Pinontoan 2020; Marbun 2025). Deliberately excluded as insufficiently individuated or unverifiable to real citations: the consorts Ambilingan/Katiwiey, Taretinimbang and Rumintu'unan; the rice-giver names Sawur and Manaroinsong; assembly figures Kopero, Mandei, Makaliwe and Soputan; and the folktale mortals Mamanua, Lumalundung, Walansendow, Pingkan and Matindas, which belong to the legend cycle rather than the attested pantheon.
