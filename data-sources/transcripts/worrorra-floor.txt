================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added three individuated Worrorra beings beyond the generic Wandjina entry: Namarali, the paramount Worrorra Wanjina of Langgi (Crawford 1968; Blundell & Woolagoodja 2005; Clendon 2014); Wunggurr, the Rainbow Serpent life-force after which the Wanjina-Wunggurr cultural bloc is named (Capell 1939; Redmond 2001); and Wijingarra (Widjingara), the quoll ancestor whose death instituted mortality, localised at Wijingarra Butt Butt on Worrorra country (Visions of Mowanjum 1980). Other named Wandjina such as Wodjin/Wanalirri (Ngarinyin) and Rimijmarra (Wunambal) belong to neighbouring traditions and were deliberately excluded. Wunggurr and Wijingarra are wired to the existing aboriginal_wandjina figure via directed relations where the narratives attest the connection.
