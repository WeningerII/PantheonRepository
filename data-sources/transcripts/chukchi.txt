Chukchi pantheon — figure-authoring transcript
================================================

Tradition: Chukchi (northeastern Siberia). Registered era key: primordial.

Research scope: The Pantheon Registry already holds the Creator (chukchi_tenanto_mwan),
Raven the transformer (chukchi_kurkil), and two of the directional "Beings" (vaʼirgьt):
Dawn (chukchi_dawn) and the Nail-Star / Pole-Star (chukchi_pole_star). The task was to
add any further well-attested, individuated CORE Beings still missing.

Per Bogoras's ethnography (The Chukchee, Part II "Religion," 1907) and its faithful
reproduction in Czaplicka (Aboriginal Siberia, 1914, Pt III ch. XIII), the Chukchi
recognise a class of "benevolent beings sacrificed to" (taaronyo vairgit) inhabiting the
twenty-two "directions" of the compass. Two of these are named, individuated, and clearly
distinct from the existing four, and they recur in every summary of the system alongside
Dawn and the Pole-Star:

  * MID-DAY — a direction-Being and co-recipient of the chief sacrifices (with Dawn and the
    Zenith); "often identified with the 'middle-crown'." Attested, distinct from Dawn and
    from the Pole-Star → AUTHORED as chukchi_midday.
  * ZENITH ("being-a-crown," kana'irgin / "middle-crown," ginon-kana'irgin) — expressly named
    by Bogoras "the chief of these beings," the one residing in the zenith. Distinct from the
    Creator and from the Pole-Star (though the Pole-Star and Mid-day are "identified with" the
    middle-crown). Attested → AUTHORED as chukchi_zenith.

Verified-and-REJECTED candidates:
  * Pičvuʼčьn (a small benevolent / fertility spirit) — could NOT be confirmed in Bogoras or
    any reachable secondary source across repeated targeted searches. Per the hard rules it is
    OMITTED rather than authored on memory.
  * The kelet (malevolent spirits) — a spirit-CLASS, with no specific named individual surfaced
    in the sources; not authored (padding rule).

Result: two figures (Mid-day, Zenith) complete the attested individuated core of named
directional Beings; with the prior four the principal vaʼirgьt named by Bogoras (Creator,
Dawn, Mid-day, Zenith, Pole-Star) and the Raven-transformer are now all represented.

Key sources:
  - Waldemar Bogoras, The Chukchee, Part II "Religion" (Memoirs of the American Museum of
    Natural History XI; Jesup North Pacific Expedition VII), 1907 — pp. 303–304 on the
    "benevolent beings sacrificed to" (taaronyo vairgit), the zenith-being "being-a-crown"
    (kana'irgin) as chief, and Mid-day / the Sun / the Pole-Star identified with the
    "middle-crown."
  - M. A. Czaplicka, Aboriginal Siberia: A Study in Social Anthropology (Oxford, 1914),
    Part III "Religion," ch. XIII — reproducing Bogoras's account of the vaʼirgьt verbatim.
  - Ülo Siimets, "The Sun, the Moon and Firmament in Chukchi Mythology and on the Relations of
    Celestial Bodies and Sacrifices," Folklore (Tartu) 32 (2006): 129–156 — peer-reviewed
    survey listing Creator, Dawn, Zenith, Mid-day and the Northern Star among the "special
    (higher) beings" and confirming the zenith-being as chief.
