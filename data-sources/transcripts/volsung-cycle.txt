Rerir's own entry names his son Völsung, "eponym of the dynasty." This batch
adds the attested descent of the Völsung line from Rerir down four generations,
each figure sourced to the Vǫlsunga saga (13th-c. Old Norse prose), the heroic
lays of the Poetic Edda, and Snorri's Skáldskaparmál. Divinity dilutes by half
each generation (Rerir ¼ → Völsung ⅛ → …), so every descendant computes to the
scion tier. parentIds wire each into the existing lineage automatically.
