================================================================================
REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

I added the four best-attested individuated sons of the Kogi Great Mother (Gauteóvan) who stand distinct from the already-registered Sintana: Kashindúkua (the jaguar-priest and first curer), Búnkuasé (the 'shining one', patron of the máma priesthood and moral law), Duginávi (culture-hero of cultivation who descends in the King of Vultures' garb), and Noána-sé (a Jaguar Lord of the jaguar-men trilogy). Each is documented by Reichel-Dolmatoff ('The Great Mother and the Kogi Universe', 1987; 'Training for the Priesthood among the Kogi', 1976), with Preuss's Forschungsreise zu den Kágaba (1926) and Gómez Cardona's 'El jaguar en la literatura Kogi' (2002) as independent corroboration; all are wired to the existing corpus as children of kogi_gauteovan and siblings of one another. I stopped here rather than adding thinner names such as Ambuambu or Námaku, which recur mainly in enumerations without enough individuated narrative to sustain a cited standalone entry.
