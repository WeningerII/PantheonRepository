REVIEWER NOTE
================================================================================

Added only Gebeleizis, the sole additional individuated divine name securely attested for the Getae-Dacians (Herodotus 4.94; Eliade 1972; Detschew 1957), documenting carefully the ancient equation with the existing Zalmoxis while following Eliade in treating him as a distinct storm-sky deity. The equation is kept in prose rather than as a symmetric relation to the pre-existing Zalmoxis record. Other names sometimes attached to a 'Dacian pantheon' — Bendis, Kotys, Sabazios, Pleistoros, Derzelas/Darzalas — are Thracian or Greek-colonial Getic-coast cults attested outside the Dacian tradition proper and were excluded per the task note; kings such as Cotiso or Comosicus fall outside the divine/heroic scope and the registered era keys. The attested individuated record beyond Zalmoxis, Decaeneus, Burebista, and Gebeleizis appears exhausted.
